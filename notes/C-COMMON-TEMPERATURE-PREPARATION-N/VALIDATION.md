## Falsifiers and remaining obligations

Wrong donor weights, an uncounted target or cursor, a changed target gap,
or a marginal-only proof invalidates the thermal preparation claim.
Initial correlations beyond the admitted product boundary require a new
joint proof. Energy records cannot be traced out before a later operation
unless its work-independent action is established at the claimed scope.

A hidden initialized encoding, failed counter restoration, routing which
exchanges unequal physical spectra without funding, circular capacity
choice, or incomplete work sector invalidates the compilation claim.
Locality bounds are falsified by site collisions, excess interaction
support/range, omitted row copies, or treating a large physical qudit
as cost-free small hardware.

A negative raw edge, nonconservation of H_0, incorrect diagonal potential,
active-path escape, independent retrigger or unlocked loader invalidates
the Hamiltonian proof. All bad sectors must remain specified even when
the path proof applies only to the nominated component.

Omitted nested completion predicates, wrong row/time indices, missing
acceptance denominators, or transfer of an unproved old retention interval
invalidates the corresponding channel claim. The actual thermal output
cannot be reported as an exact occupied-memory SWAP.

The energy account fails if it drops small-probability high-energy states,
counts only the distinguished head instead of the whole bank, adds the
old interaction Hamiltonian twice, or identifies protocol work with the
minimum cost of manufacturing the initial resources. The free-energy
identity must include resource entropy, correlations and any admitted
nonthermal logical state at its stated scope.

The result is overstated if the inverted fuel is called positive-temperature
thermal equilibrium, if its preparation is claimed here, if all input
nonstationarity is said to disappear, or if bare-energy diagonality is
identified with full-H stationarity. A stationary complete input cannot
start this same closed fixed law.

Material acquisition of the inversion and common-temperature product,
manufacture and precision of the spectra and interactions, bounded small
onsite dimensions, physical readout, occurrence, replenishment and useful
reset remain open. The unchanged native U_rec does not itself provide
these new couplings. No merge, Canon promotion or material experiment is
performed by this candidate.

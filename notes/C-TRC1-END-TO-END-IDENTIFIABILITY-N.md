# Conditional record-chain closure and preparation identifiability

NON-CANONICAL. Analytic derivation with a proposed executable audit.
The new audit has not been publicly pinned or executed. Public Canon v80 is
unchanged. This note does not supply a physical realization certificate.

## 1. The chain that is actually specified

The existing [TRC1 contract](TWISTJ-REALIZATION-OCCURRENCE-CONTRACT-1.md)
selects a complete mathematical pipeline:

```text
pointed U head h
    -> one signed-head preparation z and five-site wave (0,Sz)
    -> finite cold-reservoir evolution in a fixed context
    -> complete signed tape and persistent transition records
    -> declared visible threshold batches
    -> pushforward of a preparation ensemble selected once.
```

The proposed [probe](../probes/P-TRC1-END-TO-END-IDENTIFIABILITY-1/PREREG.md)
implements the missing composition around three existing immutable engines.
It retains source identity, actual U checkpoint labels, both wave snapshots,
both apparatus snapshots, signed outgoing amplitudes, deposits, threshold
ordinals and history continuity. Compatibility must verify the actual
transition, not merely a heat ledger consistent with squared amplitudes.

There is one source kick. A later nonzero U checkpoint does not re-prepare
an initially zero wave. Each coupled step emits one accounting record,
including steps with zero threshold crossings. Passive rereading adds no
record. Administrative END preserves the completed memory and adds no
physical interaction. The disabled reset preserves state; it does not meet
the separate reset codomain required by issue #539.

The exact budget is

```text
E(P_t) + sum_x heat_(t,x) = ||S(z)||^2/2 = m_QDD(z)/2.
```

The same geometric or amplitude value may occur in multiple records because
the record carries a different transition position and compatible retained
history. This is a mathematical event-identity rule. It does not prove that
these records have a physical carrier, nor that repeated labels create a
new spatial direction.

## 2. A concrete obstruction to closing the prediction

Take the two canonical heads with signed first four coordinates

```text
z_a=(1,0,0,0), z_b=(0,1,0,0), n0=0, final two pentits=(0,0).
```

Both have total QDD weight 4/5, unnormalized LOW/HIGH weights (1/20,3/4)
and normalized weights (1/16,15/16). Their full QDD densities differ. The
calibration in this example deliberately consists of the stated scalar
weights, not the complete density or signed head.

Freeze the same context for both sources: only the origin port is active,
its conductance is 1, the threshold is 1/16, and the horizon is one step.
The inherited first-origin response gives deposits

```text
D_a = 1421^2 / 4860^2,
D_b =  349^2 / 4860^2.
```

Exact integer inequalities give

```text
4860^2 < 16*1421^2 < 2*4860^2,
0 < 16*349^2 < 4860^2.
```

Consequently the complete conditional pipeline predicts one threshold
crossing for a and none for b. The two strictly positive preparation
mixtures

```text
mu_1=(1/3)delta_a+(2/3)delta_b,
mu_2=(2/3)delta_a+(1/3)delta_b
```

agree on the entire declared scalar QDD calibration but predict crossing
probabilities 1/3 and 2/3. No apparatus parameter changes. The ambiguity lies
in the preparation mixture. This is a diagnostic mathematical comparison,
not an independently calibrated physical experiment or a contradiction of
context-dependent readings.

## 3. The exact condition for closing a finite prediction

For a fixed finite source family, put calibration outputs in columns of A
and held-out prediction outputs in columns of B. A column may contain
indicators of complete visible prefixes. Let C be A with a row of ones.
Then prediction Bp is determined by calibration Ap for every probability
mixture p exactly when

```text
ker(C) is contained in ker(B),
equivalently row(B) is contained in row(C).
```

This is an exact linear-algebra condition on a declared source and observable
class. A failed inclusion supplies a rational direction delta with
C delta=0 and B delta nonzero. Small opposite perturbations of an interior
mixture yield two admissible preparations with identical calibration and
different predictions. The [proof](../probes/P-TRC1-END-TO-END-IDENTIFIABILITY-1/PROOF.md)
also distinguishes this global criterion from a particular calibration
fiber, which may be smaller because of nonnegativity constraints.

This identifies the missing obligation precisely: either an independently
justified preparation law selects p, or independently justified calibration
observables determine every prediction of interest on the admitted source
family. A freely chosen p followed by exact computation does not meet that
obligation. In this example, resolving the a/b preparation mixture would
determine the crossing law. Reusing that same crossing law as its own
calibration would provide no new empirical prediction.

The selected ensemble is sampled once at preparation. Later conditioning
updates the weights of sources compatible with the observed prefix; it does
not re-sample the source or modify U. Exact prefix consistency therefore
does not assert independent events, Bernoulli behavior, or long-run measured
frequency convergence.

## 4. A second, already closed negative branch

The [reservoir partition proof](../probes/P-DECODER-RESERVOIR-QUADRATIC-PARTITION-1/PROOF.md)
already excludes a direct sharp-QDD interpretation of the complete positive
energy account when the origin is active and the horizon is positive.
One fine slot has positive weight on both

```text
z_H=(1,-1,0,0), whose QDD LOW weight vanishes,
z_L=(1,1,1,1), whose QDD HIGH weight vanishes.
```

A state-independent complete positive LOW/HIGH redistribution would need
to assign that slot LOW coefficient both zero and one. Averaging over an
ensemble cannot repair this pointwise contradiction. This rules out that
processing class for this source and coupling. It does not exclude coherent
mixing, another interaction, another observable, or physical Born behavior.

## 5. Where physical closure still stops

| Transition | What is available | What is still an input or missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native U to prepared wave | Exact head reader and once-prepared five-site source | Physical preparation map; the source was designed with knowledge of the QDD norm |
| U counter to wave evolution | Exact checkpoint provenance | One wave step per U step is a label convention, not an intertwining or physical clock calibration |
| Wave to retained record | Rational cold coupling, signed tape, exact ordinals | Material realization, fresh receiver capacity, power/loss boundary, pointer and post-state evidence |
| Record to visible packet | Explicit mathematical projection | Independent identification of a physical observable and acquisition coverage |
| Conditional histories to probabilities | Exact pushforward for a supplied preparation law | Independent occurrence law or sufficient independently calibrated preparation constraints |
| Probabilities to temporal frequencies | No new bridge in this probe | Repetition/dependence and convergence assumptions appropriate to the actual experiment |

The previous [pair-recovery result](C-REGISTRATION-PAIR-RECOVERY-N.md)
adds a separate warning at the final observational boundary: all-pairs
histograms, first-registration gaps and recovery hazards are different
objects. Their identification needs the stated source/renewal premises.
Neither exact temporal pair data nor the present implementation chooses a
native preparation law.

The mathematical recipe can be made executable and its admissible prediction
class can be delimited. The entire physical chain is not thereby derived.
The remaining work is a named preparation/realization/observation certificate,
not a numerical agreement obtained by selecting an ensemble after outcomes.
Issues #539, #830, #832 and #834 retain their separate ownership and scope.

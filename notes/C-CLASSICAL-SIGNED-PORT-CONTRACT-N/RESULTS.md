## Consequences for the decoder and physical reading

Signed four-port output is already injective on the selected four-dimensional
classical source: the exact decoder needs the first cut. The second cut is
a fixed independent consistency target for the same source and loaded port
law. A square/intensity output loses the global sign, so it cannot be
substituted for the signed interface.

The full wave-plus-tape update is reversible and conserves its declared
amplitude energy even with occupied tapes. The two-state pointer returning
to zero does not renew blank incoming memory. Mathematical backward access
to a complete state does not give a separately readable unbounded archive
or a physical reset at zero cost.

The native F5 five-context decoder, the rational classical source and the
legacy seven-mode endpoints remain separate types. The package supplies
neither a native-to-wave encoding nor a contact transducer realizing it.
Earlier T1/T2/T3 and the pentad theorem cannot be composed by matching names
or by replacing one exposed clock interface with another.

The old numerical precision/discrepancy conclusions and saved audit A are
unchanged. Lane B supplies a way to address the still-open independent
restoration step; it does not certify the old coherence rectangles merely
by writing their mathematical target.

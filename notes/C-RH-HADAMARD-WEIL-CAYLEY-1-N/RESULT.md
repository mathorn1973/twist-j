## 1. Exact Cayley pair

Let

```text
rho      = beta + i gamma,
rho*     = 1 - conjugate(rho),
xi       = 1 - 1/rho,
xi*      = 1 - 1/rho*.
```

Then

```text
xi* = 1/conjugate(xi).
```

Writing

```text
A = beta^2 + gamma^2,
B = (1-beta)^2 + gamma^2,
```

gives

```text
|xi|^2 = B/A.
```

Therefore

```text

  iff A = B
  iff beta = 1/2.
```




## 5. Finite Li/Cayley linearization fails

The decisive source-side test is analytic rather than numerical.

Write

```text

 = (1-2 beta)^2/(2 A B),
A = beta^2+gamma^2,
B = (1-beta)^2+gamma^2.
```

Direct exact differentiation gives

```text
Delta E = 2 P/(A^2 B^2),
```

where, with `d=beta-1/2`,

```text

 = 16 d^4
 + 32 d^2 gamma^2
```


By contrast, `xi(rho)=1-1/rho` is holomorphic. Any finite real linear combination of individual single-zero Cayley powers `xi(rho)^k` and their conjugates is harmonic on this domain. Therefore it cannot equal `E` identically.

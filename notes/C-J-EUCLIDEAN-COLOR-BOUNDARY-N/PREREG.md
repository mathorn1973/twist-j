## 1. Frozen objects and equalities

Let O=Z[j], K=Q(j), Phi_5(j)=0, J=1+j^2,
phi=-j^2-j^3 and lambda=1-j. Use the principal complex embedding.
For S in {O,K}, G_A(S) joins distinct x,y when |x-y|=phi^n
for some n in A. Treat A={0}, 2Z, 2Z+1, {0,1}, Z separately.
For 0<epsilon<1, H_epsilon(S) instead uses
1-epsilon<|x-y|<1+epsilon. Exact and interval adjacency are not equivalent.

Scalar-chart class: every nonzero additive F:O->C such that
F(Jx)=w F(x) for one constant complex w. Reader class: every nonzero
additive h:O->F5 such that h(Jx)=t h(x) for one t in F5.
Equality of maps is literal; any normalization, Galois action or permitted
output relabeling must be stated separately. These chosen classes do not
claim completeness among physical decoders.

## 3. Frozen finite audit

Both scripts use only the Python standard library, integers and Fraction.
No tolerance is used for an algebraic equality.

* Audit Phi_5, J inverse, j=(J-1)^3, phi^2=phi+1 and
  P(J)=0 for P(T)=T^4-3T^3+4T^2-2T+1.
* Audit all ten squared pair distances and all five residues of
  z_k=sum_{a=0}^{k-1}j^a for k=0,...,4.
* Enumerate all 624 nonzero coefficient rows in F5^4 and all five possible
  scalar responses t. Audit covariance on all four basis vectors.
* Audit +/-phi^n j^k for -8<=n<=8 and 0<=k<5, including its conjugate
  norm and squared residue against exponent parity (170 directed cases).
* Audit (2+j)/(2+j^4): norm one, nonintegral coordinates, legal local
  residue. Do not silently identify all K-unit-length directions with
  roots of unity or integral units.
* Audit d_n for 1<=n<=32 by exact ordering in Q(sqrt5), and the explicit
  windows epsilon=10^(-r), n=2r, 1<=r<=12.

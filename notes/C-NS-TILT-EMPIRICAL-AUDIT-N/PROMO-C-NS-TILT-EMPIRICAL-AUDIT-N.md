## The independent derivation obligation

An integer-derived primordial model must specify one accepted source,
preparation/measure and physical reading, derive its scalar correlations
and their scale identification, and then establish the comparison to sky
observables. Writing down a power law with the desired slope does not
supply this source. Agreement of a scalar ratio is weaker than agreement
of the same model's amplitudes, correlations, tensor contribution and
transported spectra. At least one independent consequence beyond n_s
must survive a separately declared test without tuning the source.

The public planar ETH-TT witness and homogeneous FRW dictionary retain
their stated scopes; neither substitutes for this primordial source.
No extra parameter, initial preparation, scale, statistical law or
cross-layer identification may be hidden in the comparison. The audit
opens no new Canon gate and closes no native physical O obligation.

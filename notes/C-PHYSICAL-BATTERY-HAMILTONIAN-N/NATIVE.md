# What the original law supplies, and what this apparatus adds

**NON-CANONICAL. L1. Analytical scope audit for claim #1753.**
The current normative input is Public Canon v105 at
892a0b11089d34e241e1eb76d2c03247022d18e6. This file distinguishes several
laws that cannot be interchanged merely because each has a conserved quantity.

## 1. The occupation capture law is already a declared model

The canonical [HOMOGENEOUS-CAPTURE-CURRENT-RECORD section][canon] specifies
one object pair, one carrier and one receiver, with unitary U_rec and its
occupation-space intertwiner. It explicitly supplies the occupation carrier,
coherent amplitudes, contact law, synchronized schedule and incoming spectral
preparation. It does not derive those data from the original six-pentit
checkpoint map. Neither that distinction nor the Canon's L1 status changes
in this note.

The local charge in that model is H_N=2 sum h_alpha^2. On its active
one-pair/one-carrier/one-memory sector the total is exactly 10I: four for
the object, four for token or pulse and two for the memory. Arbitrary
admitted helpers and references are identity spectators. Their occupied
charge is included, but they do not form an implicitly operating power supply.

Consequently relative phases among these active code states carry no
asymmetry with respect to this constant *total charge*. This is not a claim
that the state has no coherence relative to every possible energy reading.
The Canon separately distinguishes its spectral energy from H_N and does
not infer a local spectral-energy current from the local charge current.

If a new battery B and donor F are merely appended as spectators, the law is

    U_rec tensor I_(B,F).

For every number of native steps its reduced B,F state is unchanged. Thus
this unmodified extension cannot charge an initially empty battery or
transfer donor coherence into it. This statement concerns the specified
spectator extension. It does not rule out other newly defined interactions.

## 2. The Canon already has a supplied spectral-energy source

The canonical [SELECTED-SPECTRAL-ENERGY-SOURCE section][canon] starts with
specified bounded H_S, target V and a coherent sine state of a continuous
battery. Its energy-fiber unitary U preserves H_0=H_S+H_B exactly and
approximates the desired joint action with explicit bounds. Its source has
finite energy capacity but infinite Hilbert dimension.

That section also constructs a bounded interaction K with [K,H_0]=0 and

    H_total=H_0+K >= 0,
    exp(-i H_total)=exp(-i H_0) U.

Thus adding a commuting interaction to an additive energy is established
prior construction data. The present note does not claim that this formal
idea is new. The Canon explicitly leaves the coherent source preparation,
chosen energy reading and intervention law supplied. It does not establish
uniform physical-space locality of the battery interaction, a finite
integer carrier or a self-stopping controller.

Likewise the incoming spectral packet of the capture model can carry
spectral-energy coherence. It is not automatically stationary. Nothing here
proves that all native or canonically admitted inputs are incoherent. What
is missing is an admitted coupling and quantitative transfer from such an
input to the particular finite fuel bank of the selected interface.
One cannot replace that proof by identifying occupation charge, a chosen
spectral logarithm and a new qubit excitation energy.

## 4. Physical energy identification in both local variants

This note supplies a finite qubit model with the positive onsite term

    H_0 = epsilon sum_data_sites |1><1|,       epsilon>0.

Its source, apparatus, work and battery banks are tensor factors. H_B is
an explicit partial sum over battery sites, not a numerical label assigned
after reading the output. The respective charging permutations and all
elementary interactions commute with the full H_0 on all raw inputs.
The total generator is

    H_phys = H_0 + H_prop,
    [H_0,H_prop]=0,
    H_0>=0, H_prop>=0.

There is no negative counterterm canceling battery energy. Interaction
energy is included, and its initial mean and variance are counted.
At initialized code checkpoints the actual onsite energy agrees with the
selected encoded target, battery and fuel account plus the fixed
information/work offset. Between compiled gates the actual energy remains
the same onsite operator even when a logical code interpretation is
temporarily unavailable.

The battery bank includes its columns in every fresh data row. Its full
raw capacity counts those empty copies; the occupied one-row code capacity
is a smaller quantity. The same distinction applies to the physical fuel
bank. Neither empty hardware nor the charged information/work pairs are
omitted from the resource account.

This makes the battery physical *within the added Hamiltonian model*.
It is not experimental verification, and it does not make encoded particle
positions equal to physical positions on the new hardware lattice.
An intertwiner of both dynamics and the named physical observables would
be needed for that stronger native claim.

The two source contracts should be kept separate:

| Source contract | Added resource and output |
| --- | --- |
| Coherent full-state interface, FUEL.md sections 1-6 | Charged offset bank and specified phase-aligned coherent blocks prepare the pure b_L; their cross-H_0 coherence is consumed |
| Sharp-energy accepted-channel interface, FUEL.md section 7 | Q_star charged basis qubits prepare the sharp battery; no cross-H_0 coherence is supplied, and the full final history/battery state need not be a product |

Both also supply equal-gap qubits, fixed-excitation information and work,
clean empty storage, a localized clock boundary, exact hardwired local
interactions, and continuous-time Hamiltonian evolution. These are model
premises, not conclusions derived from the integer checkpoint map.
The coherent interface fixes epsilon=4g to cancel relative free-energy
phases at the endpoint. The sharp prepared sector has only a common
free-energy phase; the same choice remains allowed. Neither relation
calibrates an energy or time scale in SI units.

## 5. Preparation obligations and the limited obstruction

[FUEL.md](FUEL.md) proves the obstruction to preparing the nominated pure
coherent b_L from a state stationary under the conserved H_0. It does not
prove that every selective detector, or every accepted reduced channel,
needs a coherent energy donor. The sharp-energy construction is an
explicit alternative with a different ledger and output contract.
The broad coherent source remains a valid option for its stronger interface.

In the sharp construction, an exact conserving permutation transfers
epsilon Q_star from supplied charged basis fuel to an empty battery and
depletes that fuel. The fuel's H_0 variance and asymmetry are zero.
This is an included charging protocol, not a derivation of the initially
charged fuel. With no other net energy source, conservation forbids creating
that positive battery energy. An apparatus initially supported in the ground space of H_0
also stays there under this bare-energy-conserving law.
The clock interaction, whose mean is separately conserved here, cannot
silently pay a missing bare-energy charge.

Neither initial source contract eliminates clock readiness. The physical
clock has zero bare splitting, but its localized boundary state is
nonstationary under the interacting H_phys. In the sharp variant the
complete ready input has sharp H_0 and still has positive H_phys variance
g^2 K/(K+1)^2. For arbitrary mixed data, the clock-off-diagonal
commutator block is -a_0 G_0 rho_D, which is nonzero; PADDED.md section 7
states this direct nonstationarity proof. Positive variance alone would
not prove nonstationarity of a mixed state. This clock boundary is a
different resource from cross-H_0 coherence.
A preparation unitary commuting with the full H_phys preserves stationarity
under H_phys, so it cannot create this working initial clock from a
completely H_phys-stationary source. The source and clock states are
declared initial conditions. Their acquisition, cooling and reset require
a further physical preparation model.

## 6. Scope of the answer

There are finite local Hamiltonian constructions with an actual positive
battery term, explicit consumed fuel, counted clock and work energy, and
the selected output interfaces. Charging is included in the same compiled
word and Hamiltonian. Its history is coherent, so a circuit prefix is not
automatically a deterministic intermediate wall-clock instant. A
charging-only instance separately prepares the nominated battery at its
own endpoint.

The sharp option provides the accepted physical channel without the broad
coherent battery resource. Its padded physical readout contract is stated
separately in PADDED.md; no exact permanent storage or self-stopping
Hamiltonian is inferred. The coherent option retains its own complete-state
comparison and source assumptions.

The unchanged occupation law with spectator battery and fuel performs
neither charging protocol. The extra interactions, energy assignments,
charged fuel, clean apparatus and nonstationary clock boundary are declared.
The coherent option additionally supplies its specified cross-energy
donor. None is attributed without proof to J, the original checkpoint map,
or the canonical charge current. Deriving an energy-compatible native
source and coupling, realizing and calibrating the exact interactions,
cooling or resetting the apparatus, and obtaining a law of individual
outcomes remain separate obligations.

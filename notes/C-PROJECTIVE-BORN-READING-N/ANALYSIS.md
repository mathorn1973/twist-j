### Primitive height census

For a primitive integral pair v define h(v)=v* v and Delta=N_(F/Q)(h(v)).
Primitive means that its coordinate ideal is the whole integer ring. In the
two class-number-one fields, every projective ray has such a representative,
and two representatives differ by a unit. For a unit u,
N_(F/Q)(u conjugate(u))=1, so Delta is well defined.

If both coordinates are nonzero, put x=v0 conjugate(v0), y=v1 conjugate(v1).
Both real embeddings of x,y are positive and N(x),N(y) are positive integers.
Writing x1,x2,y1,y2 for their real values gives

Delta=N(x)+N(y)+x1*y2+x2*y1 >= (sqrt(N(x))+sqrt(N(y)))^2 >= 4.

At equality, N(x)=N(y)=1 and x1*y2=x2*y1; hence x=y. Both coordinates are
units, and their ratio has modulus one in every embedding. An algebraic
integer all of whose conjugates have modulus one is a root of unity. Thus
Delta=4 consists precisely of [1:z] for z in mu10 (K5) or mu8 (K8).
A zero coordinate gives one of the two poles and Delta=1. This proves
12 and 10 rays respectively, not merely a finite coefficient search.
The class-number-one premise is standard arithmetic: for K8, the Minkowski
bound 24/pi^2<3 reduces every ideal class to norm 1 or 2, and the unique

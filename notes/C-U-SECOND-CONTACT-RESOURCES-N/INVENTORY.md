## 1. The object that needs physical resources

The target is the second contact of #1355, with both receivers having
actually evolved from their common time-zero preparation. At counter six,
the second receiver is `(2,1,3,4,0,4)`, independently of both source labels.
Its source port is `s=a2+1 mod5`; the contact must export `4` to that port,
keep `P=(2,1,3,4)` and `r=4`, and set `q=s+1 mod5`. The subsequent native
bits are `011`. The first receiver and its retained reading remain part of
the same apparatus. These are mathematical requirements already specified
in the [two-contact contract, lines 35-61][two-contact].

The task is not to prepare that endpoint directly and call it an origin-zero
trajectory. It is also not a larger bank, another source-preserving SUM, or
a new simulation of an assumed interaction. Source consumption is admitted.

## 3. What the independent ion evidence supports

[Meth et al., arXiv:2310.12110v2][Meth], Appendix A and Appendix G.1,
report five-level ion registers and controlled rotations using auxiliary
ground states and the axial centre-of-mass mode. Their apparatus compensates
driven and spectator Stark shifts; the five-level implementation uses buffer
ions to reduce addressing cross-talk. This supports concrete carrier and
interaction ingredients. It does not report the TWIST-J contact or its
energy account. The simulated lattice-gauge Hamiltonian is not an
identification of the bare energy of TWIST-J coordinates.

[Schindler et al., arXiv:1308.3096v1][Schindler], Sections 1.2 and 2.2-2.4,
especially Section 2.3 and Figure 8, document a calcium-ion apparatus with
preparation, optical control, calibration and detection. A computer programs
an FPGA, while DDS electronics control laser modulation and timing. This is
evidence for actual external control hardware, not a complete work-store
model or a native-counter clock law for the second contact.

These are precedents from other experiments. Their parameters and performance
are not calibrations of a device available to this project. No laboratory
access, procurement, contact with a laboratory or successful project hardware
run is asserted here.

## 4. Why the existing project lanes do not supply the bridge

### Ion sideband writer

Public Canon v97 labels `QDD-ION-SIDEBAND-WRITER` as a conditional theorem
under ideal driven-ion Hamiltonians, explicitly not an experiment
[canon-ion]. Its project apparatus profile says `NOT_RUN` and distinguishes
its proposed device from acquired evidence [apparatus-status]. The theorem
is useful mathematics; its status does not convert the hardware proposal
into an executed physical result.

Its native comparison has four labels `h=1,2,3,4` and a five-valued relative
port, expanded to a 100-dimensional domain when its archive is included.
The chart uses

```text
h=(-1)^(n-3) sum(P),       e=r-r_n^0.
```

The domain and warning against applying this as a chart of arbitrary
checkpoints are explicit in [CHART-AND-CYCLE.md, lines 18-85][ion-chart].
For the present ready state, `sum(P)=2+1+3+4=0 mod5`; substituting into the
displayed domain formula at `n=6` gives `h=0`, outside those four labels.
This is a direct source/domain check, not a new classification of encodings.

There is also a port mismatch. The ion chart's `T_e` changes `q` and `r`
oppositely and preserves `z`; its hardware port is the deviation `e`.
The present `C` fixes `r` and changes `z` using a separate five-valued source.
Equal five-state cardinality does not identify these ports. A new bridge
would need its own justified encoding and dynamics; none is supplied by the
old chart. The [moving-frame qualification, lines 248-260][ion-time] already
says that encoded waiting does not derive natural ion evolution by `U`,
the energy supply or the laboratory timing reference.

### Affine control library

`QDD-AFFINE-CONTROL-REALIZATION [T]` assumes six distinguishable five-level
systems, Fourier controls and `H_ij=-hbar g N_i N_j`. It proves the relevant
pulse identity under these premises [affine]. It does not identify a device
having that pair Hamiltonian on the present coordinates. The documented
ion/mode interaction must not silently be substituted for this assumed law.
The Canon expressly leaves switching, work sources, native material energy
and compatibility with native tick slots unprovided [affine-limit]. No new
compilation of this library is part of the present audit.

### Funded involution and #1349

`INTEGER-ENERGY-FUNDED-INVOLUTION [T]` starts from a chosen involution,
chosen energy with integer differences, and an integer resource. Its exact
conservation and reversibility theorem does not choose any of those physical
inputs; its locality claim is conditional on their locality [funded].

The #1349 note defines its `Omega24` energy as a comparison account
[omega-energy]. Its native contract separately requires the carrier,
independent energy, degeneracy, available interactions and preparation
[omega-native]. The required source/counter holding and checkpoint relation
are obligations, not delivered mechanisms [omega-time]. That distinct
source-preserving task therefore supplies no physical resource certificate
for the consumptive second trace contact.

## 5. First decision gate

**HOLD** applies to claiming a physical realization, not to acceptance of the
already stated mathematics. The missing input package is a concrete device
or independently justified physical model with:

1. A state/coordinate identification covering the actual ready receiver,
   its source, both native histories and the retained first archive.
2. Independently justified spectra and interaction energies, plus physical
   models of the work source and controller, their admissible entry states
   and the obligations for reporting all outputs.
3. Available spatial couplings and their calibration or independent physical
   justification on that same carrier, including archive disturbance.
4. Independently specified dynamical and timing laws, and a checkpoint
   dictionary defining how to test the nonzero contact interval and the
   following native steps.

Constructing and verifying the complete path, intermediate resource
availability and actual outputs is the next stage after this input gate;
it is not already established by passing the gate. Only with these inputs
can that audit of the second contact be meaningful. A successful calculation with assumed
operations would not fill these missing entries. If a larger reversible
model implements native state mergers only after projection, its environment
must retain the distinguishing information; its complete states then do not
inherit the old collisions. A dissipative model also needs its environment
account. No extra environment is supplied by calling the contact reversible.

The inspected sources establish neither this physical certificate nor a
universal impossibility. No alternative platform, experiment, new proof,
verifier, enlarged bank or reset mechanism is introduced by this inventory.

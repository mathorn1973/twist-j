## 1. Verdict first

QIK is dead and stays dead. Its substrate (conscious binary entities), its
alphabet (p = 2), its first act (free choice) and its particle table (a fit
presented as prediction) are all incompatible with the current line, and the
mass table fails a parameter-free internal consistency test on its own numbers
(section 6). Nothing here is importable.

One instinct survived every re-founding and is worth naming because it is now
load-bearing: **there is no stage.** QIK refused to put physics on a background
of space and time and made both emerge from acts of relation between things
that have no properties in isolation. That refusal is what the current axiom
states in adult language.

## 2. What the document is

A seventeen-page framework overview at version 0.7, decimal-versioned, written
before the axiom existed and never mentioning J = 1 + zeta_5^2. It has no
preregistration, no verifier, no falsifier and no exact arithmetic. Validation
is argued from decimal agreement with check marks in a results table, which is
the same rhetoric the 2026-07-31 recon flagged as a hazard in the TWIST-6D
trio. It is the third pre-axiom body the project has now examined, after the
Thue-Morse guide and the Zenodo trio.

It is also the only one of the three that is not primarily about geometry. QIK
is about **communication**, and that makes it a different kind of ancestor.

## 3. The idea, in its own terms

The whole framework hangs on one Latin line on page one:

```text
Eligo, percipior, percipio.
I choose, I am witnessed, I witness.
```

Read in order, that is a construction of existence in three steps. A thing
first makes a free binary choice. It is then witnessed by others, and being
witnessed is what makes it exist. Having been made to exist, it can witness in
turn. Reality is the expanding network of those reciprocal acts, and nothing in
it exists alone: to be is to be in relation, and the relation is prior to the
relatum.

Everything else in the document is machinery hung on that sentence:

## 4. The one sentence worth keeping

Compressed until nothing false is left:

> Existence is not a property a thing has, it is a relation a thing is in, and
> time is the count of those relations being made.

That sentence has no consciousness in it, no bits, no free parameters, and it
is close to what the current line says as "time is a counter, space is a
commutator, reality is a closed plenum with no external clock and only internal
readout." It is the archivable content of QIK. The rest is scaffolding that
fell.

## 6. Falsification first: the mass table dies on its own numbers

The document's flagship is a seven-row table claiming particle masses to better
than two percent from m = kappa(N) N q(T)/c^2 with kappa(N) = A N^beta, a
universal A = 7.03 x 10^36, and beta piecewise at 2.3, 2.1, 1.9 with node
points at N = 200 and N = 1000.

Three things are wrong, in increasing order of severity.

**First, the parameter count.** One A, three exponents and two node points is
six adjustable numbers before any particle is named, and then N itself is
assigned per particle (200, 450, 1500, 1000) with no derivation. That is ten or
more adjustable quantities used to reproduce seven measured masses. It is a
fit, and the document presents it as a prediction with check marks.

**Second, the circularity is admitted in a footnote.** The W and Z rows carry
"N values derived from the rule N ~ m/m_0", that is, the count is read off from
the mass the count is supposed to predict. Those two rows contain no
information at all.

**Third, and fatally, the table contradicts its own formula.** Within a single
beta regime the ratio of two masses is
m_i/m_j = (N_i/N_j)^(beta + 1), which is independent of A and of q(T). No
tuning of the calibration can touch it. Computed readouts, this session, float
arithmetic, reported as readouts and not as canon:

```text
beta = 2.1 regime, muon against electron
  formula   (450/200)^3.1        =  12.35
  table     104 MeV / 0.52 MeV   = 200.00
  factor                            16.19

beta = 1.9 regime, tau against proton
  formula   (1500/1000)^2.9      =   3.24
  table     1780 MeV / 945 MeV   =   1.88
  factor                             1.72
```

The stated formula and the stated table cannot both be true. Two particles
inside one regime, with no freedom left between them, disagree by a factor of
sixteen. Evaluating the absolute formula with the published A and q(T) misses
every row as well, by factors from about 0.23 to about 76, in both directions.
So either A is silently per-particle, in which case the formula has no content,
or the table was produced by some other procedure that the document does not
state.

Under the current gates this is an F, and it would have fired at
preregistration. That is the honest reason QIK is in the archive and not on a
branch, and it is worth stating plainly: the framework was not abandoned
because it was too strange. It was abandoned because the one place it made
contact with measurement did not survive its own arithmetic.

## 7. Hazard

Nothing in this note may be cited as support for any row, on either line. The
same rule the name-lineage note states applies here with more force: a
structural resemblance between a dead framework and a live axiom is provenance,
not evidence, and reading agreement backwards into support is the failure mode
the manifest names in section 7.

Three specific things must not cross into current work:

```text
1  the mass table, in any form, including as an illustration. It is
   internally inconsistent and its check-mark column is false.
2  the precision-agreement rhetoric and the bare check marks. The current
   line fences measured comparisons and they move no status.
3  the vocabulary. "Percepton", "witnessing", "community decides" and the
   consciousness framing carry a metaphysics the current line does not hold
   and cannot be casually reused in exposition, where a reader will take
   them as claims.
```

The Latin motto is not in that list. It is quotable as history, attributed and
dated, and it is the only line of QIK that is better than what replaced it as
prose. It is worse than what replaced it as physics.

## The question this candidate answers

`P-QDD-RECORD-NATURALITY-FORK-1` left O2 as a choice between two physical
structures: strict law naturality under a fixed `S_4` action, or enlargement
of physical post-state equality to the `+-S_4` gauge quotient. The candidate
asks a different question first, and then shows the choice does not decide the
law.

```text
Q1  Which relabelings of the five J-orbit labels does the plenum actually
    perform?
Q2  Does the answer to Q1 decide the post-state law?
```

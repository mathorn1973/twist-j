## 1. Exact statements proposed

```text
S1 CEILING
   Let the five J-orbit labels carry the step map. The relabelings realized by
   J-motion are exactly the twenty affine maps x -> b + c x on F_5, generated
   by the step map and the doubling map. All 120 vertex permutations preserve
   the simplex form, so S_5 is a symmetry of the carrier geometry, but only 20
   of the 120 are motions. Every non-identity affine map fixes at most one
   label; every transposition fixes exactly three; therefore no transposition
   is affine. At every token the complete record stabilizer of order 24 meets
   the affine group exactly in the affine stabilizer of order 4, index six.

S2 SELECTION
   Let Q_k be the rank three HIGH projector on the rational J-simplex
   apparatus carrier and W_k = Q_k V. Let T satisfy the effect equation
   T^sharp T = Q_k and the support condition Q_k T = T. Then T restricted to
   W_k is an automorphism O of W_k orthogonal for the simplex form, T = O Q_k,
   and T^2 = O^2 Q_k. Hence T^2 = delta T with delta in {+1, -1} holds if and
   only if T = delta Q_k. Both signs occur and are one physical class under
   the registered sign equality. No symmetry group enters the statement or the
   proof.

S3 SCOPE CLAUSE (proposed as scope text, not as a separate row)
   Consequently the naturality fork of P-QDD-RECORD-NATURALITY-FORK-1 does not
   decide the post-state law. The C_4 centralizer family, the 48-member S_4
   normalizer with 24 sign classes, the enlarged family rho(h) X, and a
   sample of the full orthogonal family of W_k are all cut to the single
   Lueder class by S2, which mentions no group. The remaining obligation of O2
   is therefore single and group-free: derive or falsify class level
   idempotence from the record writing protocol.
```

## 2. Falsifiers

```text
F-S1  fires if the group generated by the step map and the doubling map is not
      exactly the twenty affine maps; or if some transposition is affine at
      some token; or if the complete record stabilizer meets the affine group
      in anything other than the order four affine stabilizer.
F-S2  fires if some T with T^sharp T = Q_k and Q_k T = T, other than +Q_k and
      -Q_k, satisfies T^2 = +T or T^2 = -T at any token.
F-S3  fires if S2 fails, or if some family named in S3 contains a class level
      idempotent operator outside {+Q_k, -Q_k}.
```

Grade note for the fold, stated rather than argued: S1 is finite and
exhaustive, so T is earned by computation once the two-platform run is green.
S2 is a written derivation certified by exact witnesses on finite and sampled
families, in the same style the repository already uses for scalar lemmas. A
fold that declines to carry a written lemma at T should register S2 as C at
the computed range and leave the derivation in the Canon prose. Both are
defensible; C is the conservative choice and nothing downstream in this
proposal depends on which is taken.

## Scope in one paragraph

The isotropy-6 audit put one obligation at the very front of the photon
continuum lane: read the symmetry group of the canonical step set as a
group of orthogonal transformations, before any operator symbol is
expanded, because the cubic branch (the fourth-order cone), the icosahedral
branch, and the "neither" branch send everything downstream three different
ways. The publicly available canonical spatial object is the carrier of
GATE-LIFT-KERNEL-Z (frozen in PREREG-C-PHOTON-SPATIAL-SYMBOL-1): the
integer model L = ker(Tr) in Z^4 with the divided Galois Gram, isometric to
the FCC lattice D_3. This candidate computes, exactly and exhaustively: the
full integral point group of that carrier, its structure and element
orders, its transport to the D_3 picture, its mod 5 reduction inside the
finite orthogonal group (the icosahedral non-lift, independently of the
amendment that first reported the counts), the dimension of its quartic
invariants, and the fourth-order anisotropy data of the first four shells,
with the resulting isotropy cone. The step set is taken to be the
minimal-vector shell of the carrier; the declared limit of that reading is
field 4 of the preregistration.

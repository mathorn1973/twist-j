The plane ordinals are forced, not arbitrary. The 806 planes partition by

of that block by construction. This is a structural confirmation of
`enumeration_certificate=ae18fbdb65ebef61` that does not depend on the hash.

Arithmetic cross-check on the stated gate size: 15,625 states times 30,000
measured ticks is 468,750,000 transitions, consistent with the reported
"468-million-transition raw-W gate".

## 3. The break attempt

Preregistered target, stated before the computation:

```text
F-PAIR-LUT-PHASE-SILENT
  There exists a transition word w != 01 and a live state s such that the
  signature of s under w IS PRESENT in a published table and decodes to the
  wrong answer.

  If this fires, the "Decoder boundary" section is incomplete: NOT_FOUND is
  not a sufficient guard, and the CSV must carry a mandatory word assertion
  rather than only an ordered-basis assertion.
```

Result, all four transition words against both tables:

```text
word   775 hits  775 wrong   776 hits  776 wrong
00            0          0          0          0
01        15625          0      15625          0
10            0          0          0          0
11            0          0          0          0
```

NOT FIRED. And not fired in the strong way: at every wrong word the hit count
is zero, not merely the wrong count. An out-of-phase read cannot land inside
either table at all. `NOT_FOUND` is therefore a complete guard, and the
decoder cannot silently corrupt.

That is a real property of the artifact and it is stated nowhere in the
export. It belongs in the "Decoder boundary" section, because it is exactly
the assurance a consumer needs and cannot derive from the text as written.
The mechanism was not investigated here; only the fact was established, and
only for these two planes.

## 4. The scope is understated on one axis

The export says the decoder is "phase-specific: `n_start=0`". The signature
depends on `n_start` only through the ordered pair `(tm(n), tm(n+1))`, since
the observation at each tick is a function of the current state alone and the
state advances by the tick's own bit. The true scope parameter is the header
field `transition_word=01`, and `n_start=0` is one witness of it.

The word 01 occurs at 1,366 of the first 4,096 ticks, the first ten being
0, 3, 6, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 24, 27. The Thue-Morse pair frequencies are
00 and 11 at 1/6 each, 01 and 10 at 1/3 each, so the export is valid on a
density 1/3 set of ticks, not on a single one. The 1/6 in that same census is
the gyron density that carries the Basel gate, so the decoder's admissible
phase set and the sealed clock result are two readings of one pair census.

Recommended header and prose correction: keep `n_start=0` as the witness,
promote `transition_word` to the stated scope, and say that any tick with
`(tm(n), tm(n+1)) = (0,1)` is admissible.

## 5. The certification is not uniform across the two files

The export presents both tables under one certification procedure. They do
not rest on the same kind of evidence and should not be labeled together.

```text
plane 776, state table       warmup=0, ticks=0, joint_certificate all zero.
                             Pure finite combinatorics. Fully rebuilt here
                             from the generators. Nothing measured enters it.

plane 775, raw-W table       warmup=400, ticks=30000, joint certificate
                             36f10be3026bb853, 19 raw targets. The key set,
                             the fiber partition and the multiplicities are
                             pure combinatorics and were rebuilt here. The
                             (Hplain, Q, Hcov, A) VALUES and the target IDs
                             come from a 30,000-tick measured run that this
                             audit did NOT reproduce.
```

G13 above is therefore self-referential for the raw W values: it takes the
per-state ground truth from the published table itself. It confirms that the
file is internally consistent, not that the tuples are right. Reproducing
them needs the 468-million-transition path, which is out of reach for a
Python leg and is precisely what the C++ gate exists for.

Consequence for labeling: the 775 raw W content is C at a stated finite
range, warmup 400 and 30,000 ticks, and cannot exceed that without a
stability argument for the window. The 776 table carries no window at all.
One certification header covering both reads stronger than the weaker half.

Under the taxonomy, and as candidate labels only:

              than 01 is present in either table.
candidate-C   the raw W tuple values, at the stated window only, and not
              independently reproduced here.

Not T, and the reason is precedent, not caution. The project already draws
this line: CENSUS-313 stays C although it enumerates all 15,625 seeds
exhaustively, while T-Z-KERNEL-TRACE-FLOW reaches T because the affine trace
laws make the table forced by the generators rather than merely observed.
Exhaustive enumeration over the complete finite universe is what these
tables have. A forcing argument is what they lack.

Route to T for the 776 separation claim: derive WHY the pair (p1p, p4p + t)
separates, from the generators, at three ticks. The zero-hit result of
section 3 suggests there is a word invariant carried by the symbol
trajectory that a symbolic argument could exhibit directly. That is one
session of work and it is the highest-value follow-up in this lane.

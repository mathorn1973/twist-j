## Why the previous proposal was rejected

The single-sentence form, gram22 is sufficient and no invariant of the
same or lower dimension suffices, fails twice: if gram22 itself lies in
that dimension the second half contradicts the first, and "invariant"
without an explicitly enumerated class is not a decidable claim. The
owner split it into two separate frozen questions and named a third that
must not be conflated with them.

## Q1, sufficiency

    gram22(x) = gram22(y)  =>  orient(x) = orient(y)

Nothing else fires this question.

## Q2, minimality

Among a PREVIOUSLY AND EXPLICITLY ENUMERATED admissible class of
invariants of dimension strictly lower than gram22, none is sufficient
to decide orientation on the same stratum.

The complete admissible class must be written into the prereg BEFORE any
computation. Without that enumeration, minimality is not a decidable
claim and must not be posed. The enumeration is part of the freeze, so
the prereg for this lane carries it in Field 1 and the owner sees it
before the pin.

## Q3, uniqueness, NOT part of this freeze

suffices is a third, separate question, uniqueness, not minimality. It
is not frozen here and needs its own prereg if ever posed.

# Native law, added clock source and the preparation boundary

**NON-CANONICAL, L1. Analytical scope audit for claim #1755.**
This note does not change the status of any Canon or predecessor result.

## 1. Which law is being completed

Public authority remains main/canon-v105 at
`892a0b11089d34e241e1eb76d2c03247022d18e6`. The canonical
[HOMOGENEOUS-CAPTURE-CURRENT-RECORD section][canon] gives a specified
occupation-space law U_rec, contact dynamics, incoming state and current
reading. It does not derive that entire occupation carrier and preparation
from the original six-pentit checkpoint update.

On its active one-pair, one-carrier, one-memory sector, the native occupation
charge totals 10I. Arbitrary helpers and references are spectators under the
stated extension. The Canon distinguishes this charge from spectral energy.
A preserved charge, an energy chosen from a logarithm of a step, and an
onsite qubit energy cannot be interchanged without an additional intertwiner.

The present target is the finite, sharp-energy encoded apparatus in
[SHARP.md of #1754][sharp]. It implements the chosen accepted physical
channel of #1752 under its changed clock/auxiliary/pulse energy account.
Object coordinates and messages are degenerate for that account. Its
source/filter bounds remain conditional on #1746. The new clock-source
construction neither strengthens those premises nor independently reviews
them. The candidate #1754 itself remains an explicit added local model.

## 2. What the unmodified law can and cannot do here

Merely appending a source and cursor gives U_rec tensor I_source,cursor.
Every source/cursor marginal is then constant under repeated native steps,
also when initially correlated with a passive reference. In particular an
empty cursor cannot receive an excitation from the appended source. The
transfer term sigma^+_1 sigma^-_0 plus its adjoint is absent from that law.

This is a statement about this unchanged spectator extension, not a theorem
forbidding all possible newly specified native couplings. No construction
here identifies the new physical qubit excitation or its onsite gap with
the original finite-field update or occupation charge.

The canonical [SELECTED-SPECTRAL-ENERGY-SOURCE section][canon] already
supplies an energy-conserving source construction and a positive total
generator, with a prescribed coherent source state. Likewise an incoming
spectral packet may carry spectral-energy coherence. These facts prevent a
blanket claim that all originally admitted states are stationary for every
energy reading. They do not supply the local cursor, its occupied boundary
site, this exchange coupling, or a quantified source-to-cursor transfer map.

## 3. The specific additions

The new law adds the following explicit physical model data:

- A line of two-state cursor sites, a neighboring source site, a stationary
  empty cursor sector and one initial source excitation.
- Equal positive bare gaps epsilon and an additional cursor onsite/control
  scale g, with the complete source/interface energy counted as epsilon+g.
- Fixed local exchange couplings, whose amplitudes depend on the total
  program length. Reverse adjoint hops are included for Hermiticity.
- The already supplied conserving data circuit, its prepared fuel and work,
  empty data rows, a charged dual-rail completion latch, an idle loading
  runway and an idle output tail.
- A continuous-time Hamiltonian law and a specified initial boundary. The
  parameter g sets the physical duration once an energy unit is chosen;
  neither an SI calibration nor a material realization is derived.

The source and clock use a conserved real excitation in this added model.
In contrast to the preceding domain-wall clock, the all-empty clock now
stays inactive even with its internal couplings present. One local occupied
site supplies the extra clock charge. The data-battery preparation does not
create that charge: cursor number is separately conserved, and its source
excitation is an additional declared resource.

Geometric locality is locality on the added qubit lattice. Original particle
coordinates remain encoded information. A simultaneous intertwiner of the
original dynamics, physical positions and energies would be required for a
stronger claim of native material implementation.

## 4. What preparation has actually been achieved

The local source injects its excitation under the same fixed Hamiltonian
that later executes the data circuit. During the quantified loading window,
the complete state is close to an explicit moving runway packet tensor the
unchanged ready data. This gives a physical transfer mechanism and a useful
approximate preparation stage without an external operation that starts the
processor at a chosen later instant.

This replaces the previous clock carrier; it does not prepare the exact old
domain-wall boundary state as an independently usable intermediate output.
The source is the first vertex of the larger one-excitation history path.
The earlier all-zero domain-wall initial state already failed to commute
only at its first active propagation edge. The novelty asserted here is
therefore not the locality of the term causing that commutator. It is the inactive vacuum,
explicit conserved injection, inclusive local source energy and quantified
interval before data actuation.

The isolated source |1> is an energy eigenstate and needs no relative phase
between its two levels. The isolated empty processor is stationary too.
Their prepared product is nevertheless not stationary for the coupled H.
The fixed source-contact initial condition supplies that nonstationarity.
The global energy distribution depends on the entire coupled chain, even
though the only initially noncommuting Hamiltonian term is the first bond.

The decoupled Hamiltonian H_sep in SOURCE.md is a comparison used to identify
the interaction responsible for this fact. No operation that switches from
H_sep to H is included or assumed in the claimed autonomous evolution.
Interpreting the comparison as a laboratory assembly or sudden coupling
protocol would introduce a further intervention whose preparation and work
fluctuations need their own model. A zero mean hopping energy on the initial
state would not by itself make that intervention free.

## 5. The irreducible declared boundary

If a complete initial density operator commutes with the same fixed H that
will govern it, its state is constant in time. A unitary commuting with H
maps it to another H-stationary state. Such a preparation cannot produce the
specified working source/contact state, whose commutator is explicitly
nonzero. This exact obstruction is restricted to the stated closed,
energy-conserving preparation class; it does not forbid using an additional
nonstationary source, an external intervention or another physical law.

Also, with cursor number conserved, an all-empty source and cursor cannot
create a running cursor. The source excitation and the clean vacuum are
supplied inputs. The construction does not manufacture them from a thermal
or unknown state, and does not give a unitary reset of that state. An extra
emitter that prepares the source |1> would have to be specified and charged
against its own resources, rather than silently assumed.

Finite exact Hamiltonian evolution cannot newly enter and stay in a proper
subspace throughout an open interval unless it stayed there from the start.
The loading window and later retention therefore have stated finite errors.
The exact endpoint is followed eventually by recurrence, not a permanent
one-way halt or an inexhaustible clock supply.

The result is a finite local source mechanism with an explicit residual
initial condition. It is not a derivation of all prepared resources from the
original law, a proof that the original law can never admit such a source,
an empirical occurrence rule, or independent acceptance of the candidate.

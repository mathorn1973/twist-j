# Local Hamiltonian execution of the complete capture circuit

**NON-CANONICAL. L1. Analytical candidate-T; independent acceptance pending.**
Public claim: [#1749](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1749).

There is an explicit finite, geometrically local, time-independent Hamiltonian
whose endpoint implements the complete encoded circuit of #1748, including
preparation of its reservoir from a basis state. The construction uses qubits
on a two-dimensional lattice, interactions on at most six nearby sites, and
an initially all-zero domain-wall clock. It retains rejected histories and
works with arbitrary logical inputs entangled with a reference.

This is an existence result for an added local quantum simulator. It does
not derive a Hamiltonian for the original TWIST-J particles, identify the
encoded reservoir with physical energy storage, or remove the initialization
boundary of a closed experiment. Those distinctions are part of the result.

## 2. Explicit construction

[COMPILATION.md](COMPILATION.md) specifies the full finite target and an exact
algorithm for its one- and two-qubit nearest-neighbor gate word. No large
matrix is treated as an elementary local site. Invalid codes and dirty work
registers have total unitary actions; the target equality requires the
specified clean initial workspace.

[PREPARATION.md](PREPARATION.md) gives a basis-to-reservoir unitary using
explicit ladder rotations. Its local compilation is included in the same
word and resource bound. The physical clock starts in a product basis state;
its coherent motion is generated by the Hamiltonian. A local preparation
Hamiltonian specifies the required basis-ready apparatus, without claiming
that unitary dynamics resets arbitrary old information for free.

[HAMILTONIAN.md](HAMILTONIAN.md) places fresh data rows and a snaking clock
path in the same two-dimensional geometry. Every gate acts near the clock
bit that schedules it. This avoids the nonlocal data-to-clock couplings of
an abstract history graph with repeatedly reused distant data wires.

For K local clock transitions, choose an added energy scale g>0 and

\[
 a_k=\frac{g}{K+1}\sqrt{(k+1)(K-k)},\qquad 0\le k<K.
\]

Local positive transition terms and local diagonal clock terms give

\[
 H_{\rm phys}|_{\rm legal}=gI-A,\qquad
 A\simeq\frac{2g}{K+1}J_x.
\]

Here the unitary history gauge includes every data gate, not just a success
branch. The Hamiltonian is positive on its entire hardware Hilbert space.
Each local term has norm at most g. At the exact time

\[
 \boxed{\tau=\frac{\pi\hbar(K+1)}{2g}},
\]

its evolution takes the initial clock to the final clock, applies the full
compiled word, and adds only the common phase -i. No measurement or discarded
clock component is used to obtain this endpoint equality.

The initialized state, including any logical reference, has

\[
 \langle H_{\rm phys}\rangle=g,\qquad
 (\Delta H_{\rm phys})^2=\frac{g^2K}{(K+1)^2},\qquad
 0\le H_{\rm phys}\le(K+1)gI.
\]

These are properties of the declared interaction Hamiltonian. They are not
the energy cost of fabricating, cooling, initializing or resetting the device.
The gate-count bound is deliberately enormous. This is a finite construction,
not a practical apparatus estimate or an efficient implementation claim.

## 3. What forward-only means here

The original filter word uses controlled V and no controlled V-adjoint on
the object. That circuit-level statement survives as a description of the
target word. It does not become a microscopic directed-Hamiltonian claim.

A nonzero block |k+1><k| tensor G_k in a Hermitian Hamiltonian necessarily
has the adjoint block |k><k+1| tensor G_k-adjoint. Our Hamiltonian contains
both. Its intermediate state is a coherent superposition of computation
prefixes; the clock is not a sequence of independent monotone transitions.
The generic finite-matrix compiler also does not preserve oracle query
counts or promise that every physical gate is a native forward W step.

Thus the answers are distinct: an exact endpoint implementation of the
forward circuit exists in this added local model; a Hamiltonian made only
of nonzero one-way history couplings is not Hermitian. The latter obstruction
is restricted to that literal directed history representation.

## 4. Energy and preparation boundary

The old selected account E_add=E_system+E_reservoir is conserved by the
funded target steps after logical preparation. It is not asserted to commute
with every compiled gate or with H_phys. The preparation prefix itself changes
that account. Its reservoir is therefore an encoded resource register in this
construction, not a demonstrated physical battery with E_reservoir as its
contribution to the total time generator.

This distinction cannot be removed by supplying more incoherent fuel. If the
initial joint state commutes with E_add and the entire preparation conserves
E_add, the reduced ladder reservoir remains energy-diagonal. For the desired
flat coherent state on L levels, any such reservoir has trace distance at
least 1-1/L from the target. PREPARATION.md proves the statement, including
arbitrary ancillary degeneracies and correlations allowed by stationarity.

The remaining supplied resource in our positive construction is a basis-ready
state that is nonstationary for H_phys, together with specified local
couplings. Its nonzero energy variance is explicit above. The original law
does not prepare these qubits or couplings. No initial coherent reservoir or
coherent clock is silently supplied, but initial low-entropy apparatus is.

## 5. Inherited output and physical time

At the default capture endpoint h=0, the encoded output is exactly the funded target output.
Consequently the predecessor's conditional complete-state claims transfer
under its stated hypotheses: full-basis accepted weight greater than 1/20000,
undesired accepted fraction below 1/1000000, and preservation of the occupied
memory's logical information and its reference correlations. These are squared
norm statements at L1, not a law of frequencies of actual events.

Spatial coordinates of the original object are encoded values. Intermediate
hardware swaps do not assert its native light cone in the hardware lattice.
Likewise the H logical release steps and their memory-buffer result do not
establish H units of physical read time under this Hamiltonian. The h>0
variants inherit the finite memory-buffer conclusion, not an unproved
continued bound-band purity guarantee after further contacts. The final
physical state is not an absorbing halt. The finite approximate endpoint
read window is bounded in HAMILTONIAN.md; permanent readout, useful reset and
repeated operation remain separate constructions.

## Frozen object

Let \(\Lambda_L^D\) be a periodic \(D\)-dimensional hypercubic cell complex.
Let \(n\) be an oriented plaquette \(2\)-chain with coefficients

\[
n_p\in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]

For an odd prime \(p\), impose

\[
\partial n\equiv0\pmod p
\]

edgewise and define the integer current

\[
j:=\frac{\partial n}{p}.
\]

The comparison over \(p\) is mathematical only. It does not alter the
TWIST-J axiom \(p=5\), and it is not a physical prime-selection argument.

## Frozen claims to prove or break

### G1. Local incidence bound

Every oriented edge of the \(D\)-dimensional hypercubic complex is incident
to exactly \(2(D-1)\) plaquettes. Therefore

\[
|(\partial n)_e|\le2(D-1).
\]

### G2. Unit-current window

If \(p>D-1\), then

\[
j_e\in\{-1,0,1\}
\]

for every edge. If \(p>2(D-1)\), then \(j\equiv0\).

Hence the local nontrivial unit-current window is

\[
D-1<p\le2(D-1).
\]

At \(D=4\), the unique odd prime in this window is \(p=5\).

The word "nontrivial" in this window means that the local bound does not
force \(j=0\). Actual realization at \(p=5\) is a separate clause G3.

## 4. Native messages: a positive construction and a sharp reset boundary

**Status: NON-CANONICAL L1, candidate-T proof / candidate-C primary audit;
a separately authored independent review has been submitted.**

The late n=5 construction admits a common ready receiver for every one of
25 ordered F5 message pairs. Original U produces

    BLANK -> PRESENT(lambda) -> BLANK -> PRESENT(mu),

with the final message retained permanently and the whole receiver restored
at the middle BLANK. A fixed affine repartition of the entire six-coordinate
cell permits a two-coordinate receiver, minimal for five present values and
a distinct absence label. Its reference includes the original source sum.
This is not a reduction under an unchanged local source-blind access rule.
The first message shares that reference and the second source is not emptied.
Every stated trace-zero 0011 launch has no immediate U predecessor, so these
are stipulated late initial conditions, not internally reached reset states.

The origin-zero question has a different answer. For any affine R(x)=Lx+h,
if the entire receiver is common at n=2, the next native operation has form
-x+t_d or -x+t_e. Therefore there are at most two receiver states at n=3,
not five distinct second messages. With BLANK at n=2 and eventual permanent
nonblank retention, the inherited recurrent-support NOWRITE theorem reduces
the bound to at most one eventual nonblank label. Enlarging the affine
receiver or postponing permanent retention does not repair this contract.

## 5. The new rational-wave memory result is still a candidate

**Status: NON-CANONICAL, conditional L1 candidate-T / candidate-C.
Independent review #1528 remains outstanding.**

For the supplied finite wave/tape F, let rho be the two current contact
projections. The candidate introduces scalar wave coordinates gamma and
delta and proves

    rho(FX)   = gamma(X)*ones2 + 2M a0,
    gamma(FX) = delta(X) - h^T a0/5,
    rho(F^2X) = (delta(X)-h^T a0/5)*ones2 + 2M a1.

The symbols and all matrices are defined in the linked complete proof.
Known initial tape slots a0,a1 are separate inputs. A fixed linear wave
summary admits exact reading at times {0,2} iff its kernel is contained in
ker(rho,delta); its minimum rank is three. Requiring {0,1,2} instead gives
ker(rho,gamma,delta) and minimum rank four. The output decoder may be
nonlinear, but the summary class is fixed rational linear. Rational
coordinate rank is neither bit capacity nor a physical detector count.

The three-coordinate xi=(rho0,rho1,delta) also predicts both exact works and
funding decisions of the two supplied contacts separated by two F steps.
It includes arbitrary occupied waves and known tapes. No delta(F^2X) update
or indefinite repeated operation is thereby supplied.

The explicit occupied pair u=0,v=+/-delta_(10,0,0) starts with equal rho,
gamma, four zero tapes and energy 1/2. Both tapes and energies remain equal
through two F steps, yet final rho has opposite entries +/-1/1049760.
The same second contact accepts both and has works 2/5 +/- 1/1049760.
Thus adding gamma alone does not close the two-step prediction, even when
energy and all tapes are also supplied. Common-ready membership is not
asserted for these arbitrary occupied-wave witnesses.

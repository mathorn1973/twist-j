## Result and corrected finding

The stated conditional construction is consistent after one correction.
The original prepared-image recognition recipe did not explicitly require
the recovered piston sum to equal the class decoded from b0. Readiness alone
would accept a native endpoint formed with one class's generator word and
pistons from another class, paired with the first class's classical output.
PROOF section 6 now requires that sum equality and the endpoint counter,
stage and program. The corrected check is sufficient: the sum fixes all
three actual selectors, while the total inverses of F and C verify the
remaining wave, tape and work coordinates against the common preparation.

## Admission boundary

The native receiver supplies only the five sums h in this observation; the
classical output does not reconstruct all 625 literal piston tuples. The
complete retained endpoint does distinguish them. Present zero is included;
an independent absence class is not supplied.

C is an added mixed-carrier operation, not a compiled or admitted native
generator. Its common bias and controlled rational lift are additional
structure. The N0 obstruction is correct only for the declared uncoupled,
source-independently scheduled class; it is not an impossibility theorem for
all native or physical contacts. Thus this review supports the conditional
NC result, **not closure of the user's actual native-to-classical bridge**.
Native admission remains OPEN and physical status remains NOT_READY. The
original S/I/V qualification and the independent B restoration lane remain
separate obligations.

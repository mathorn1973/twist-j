## 1. Frozen research question

What new signed-output and energy statement is determined by the existing native-accessible five-point encoding set $Z_5 \subset \mathbb{Q}^4$ and an independently frozen calibration within a declared target-independent model/comparison family, and what intervention or observable is required to distinguish that statement from an ordinary classical wave/port device implementing the same selected contact and wave law?

This package addresses four ordered obligations frozen in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) and [PREREG.md](PREREG.md):

1. **Preparation mismatch and rank-two source span (Obligation A):** Compare the native-accessible source set $Z_5 = \{\eta(C_1(p)) : p \in \mathbb{F}_5^4\}$ with the classical 9-preparation identification set $Z_I$ and 24-preparation validation set $Z_V$. Establish the exact linear and affine span of $Z_5$ in $\mathbb{Q}^4$, the exact intersection of $Z_5$ and $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{Q}}(Z_5)$ with $Z_I$ and $Z_V$, the necessity and sufficiency of two quotient-independent source directions modulo $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{Q}}(Z_5)$ to span $\mathbb{Q}^4$, and the exact split between subspace-identifiable functionals and complementary unidentifiable functionals under $Z_5$-only preparation.
2. **Target-independent model/comparison family and exact-clone bound (Obligation B):** Freeze a hierarchical family of models $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{aff}} \subset \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{full}} \subset \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{nl}}$ with explicit literal state and observable equality before deriving predictions, including the ordinary rational classical wave/port comparator $m_{\mathrm{cl}}$ implementing the same $(S, F, \eta, X_{\mathrm{blank}}, Y_2)$. Decide whether any preparation protocol (on $Z_5$ or on all of $\mathbb{Q}^4$) can separate two models that share the same complete transition, source map, ready state, and observable on the tested domain.
3. **Calibration-to-prediction criterion $I(m_1)=I(m_2) \Rightarrow V(m_1;s)=V(m_2;s)$ (Obligation C):** For each frozen model class and source protocol pair $(I, V)$, decide whether calibration equality $I(m_1)=I(m_2)$ implies held-out prediction equality $V(m_1;s)=V(m_2;s)$, proving universal implication where valid and exhibiting explicit complete model pairs $(m_1, m_2)$ where it fails, together with exact dimension/witness bounds on additional source directions, contact contexts, or resource/state observables needed to eliminate the surviving ambiguity.
4. **Prospective finite test specification and qualification debts (Obligation D):** Specify an executable-on-paper prospective protocol for $Z_5$-accessible operation and for conditional augmented-source / mechanism-separation tests, keeping signed response, full energy closure, source qualification, and mechanism attribution strictly separated and preserving the legacy $1/10000$ engineering allowance on its declared protocol without refitting.

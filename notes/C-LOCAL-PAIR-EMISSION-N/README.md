# C-LOCAL-PAIR-EMISSION-N

Status: NON-CANONICAL; L1; candidate-T. No Canon authority is claimed.

One fixed positive, bounded, time-independent Hamiltonian admits a stable
two-dimensional core subspace and decay of its orthogonal bright state into
two simultaneous outgoing excitations with a residual core. The emission
channel has two independent half-line coordinates. Its massive excitation
has gap 2; its other excitation is gapless with quadratic low-momentum
dispersion. The latter is not a Weyl particle.

All matrix entries are rational. At the fixed coupling `g = 1/4`, the
continued bright resolvent has one simple resonance in `|z - 10| < 1/2` and
its width obeys the exact bounds `3/32 < Gamma < 7/32`. The bright state has
no bound component, and its probability of occupying the paired-output
sector tends to one. The decay is not exactly exponential.

The equations and proposed targets were published in
[issue #1798](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1798). This is a
proof-first incubation note, not a public probe or an independent promotion.
No scientific numerical calculation was used.

The core construction is related to the three-channel rank-one core used in
the sibling one-lead calculation. Its one-lead resonance formula is not
used here: the paired channel has its own Green function and proof.

The model supplies an emission mechanism. It does not identify a proton,
neutron, electron or neutrino; derive statistics, spin or a mass ratio; or
derive the supplied Hamiltonian from the native TWIST-J update. The
remaining core is pinned, so recoil is not represented.

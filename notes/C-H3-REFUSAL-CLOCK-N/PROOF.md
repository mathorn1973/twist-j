## 6. H: two independently moving gears, finite-delay theorem

H uses two independently supplied free vertical C
words on the two material rails of the same column.
For a fixed height, read row, and desired positive
tick T, write the raw local values as u(t),v(t).
Assume their joint target (u(T),v(T)) differs from
every joint value at t=1..T-1. Equivalently the
earlier occurrence-time sets for their own target
values have empty intersection. Neither component
needs to be individually unique. Both wheels in
the finite search must have a changing contact
and prior unequal refusal at the read row.

There is an exact construction of a first B1
at T under this hypothesis. Use r0=u(t),
r2=M*v(t)+c0 with odd positive integer M and
common translation c0=u(T)-M*v(T)+2w, w=N*1.
Odd scaling and translation commute with the
actual parity-completed C, since M is invertible
modulo2 and admitted pairs are linear. At T the
material half-difference d=-w. Supply f=-J*w,
CLK=2f throughout column0, CORR=0 at the read
row and 2f at all other rows, uniform horizontally.
Other columns have zero material. Until first B
both free words are actual trajectories and the
incoming radiation is constant. Nonzero B can
occur only at the fixed read cell. Its balance
conditions are h(d)=h(w) for B1 or
h(d)=h(J)^2 h(w) for B2, in addition to parity.
At T exactly B1 qualifies.

For each earlier t with v(t)=v(T), the difference
u(t)-u(T) is nonzero. Put
d=(u(t)-u(T))/2-N. The scalar necessary equalities
q(d)=q(N) and q(d)=q(J^2*N) are nonzero rational
polynomials of degree at most2 in N. The first
has nonzero constant q((u(t)-u(T))/2); the second
has nonzero quadratic coefficient since
q(J^2)!=q(1). The positive trace Gram form implies
the stated constants are nonzero. Each earlier
t forbids at most4 integers. Therefore some
N in 1..4(T-1)+1 avoids every earlier algebraic
balance of this type. For other earlier t,
with v(t)!=v(T), the corresponding d is
(u(t)-u(T)-M*(v(t)-v(T)))/2-N. Both scalar
balance polynomials in M have positive
quadratic leading coefficient
q((v(t)-v(T))/2). Thus the first
4(T-1)+1 odd positive M include one avoiding
all remaining earlier balances. No parity
assumption is needed to exclude those earlier
events. The verifier checks the stronger full
ring inequalities, not only the scalar bounds.
Induction on actual B-idleness gives the desired
first native B1 at T. This is a finite-delay
existence theorem conditional on the supplied
two free words and unique joint target. It is
not a theorem that every T has such words or
that the pulse remains held, renews or measures
an independent input.

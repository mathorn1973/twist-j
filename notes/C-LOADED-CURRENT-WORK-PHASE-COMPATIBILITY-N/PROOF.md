## 1. Exact domain and inherited premises

Let O=Z[j], 1+j+j^2+j^3+j^4=0, rho=j^2, phi=-j^2-j^3,
and J=1+rho. Equality is coefficient equality in (1,j,j^2,j^3).
Conjugation sends j to j^-1. Write

    h(v)=v vbar=A(v)+B(v) phi,
    Q(v)=Tr(h(v))/2=2A(v)+B(v),
    g(u,v)=Tr(u vbar)/2.

Here 2g(u,v) is integral on O; g itself need not be integral. In power
coordinates Q(v)=(5 sum(v_i^2)-(sum v_i)^2)/2. There are no floats or
measured inputs. The primitive form from #1924 is

    Omega(u,v)=u0 v1-u1 v0+u1 v2-u2 v1+u2 v3-u3 v2,
    Omega(d,rho d)=B(d).

The #1927 field is used at its matched marked edge, a=z/(2c)=1.
The concrete marked choice is c=1/54, z=1/27. We do not derive these
premises or select them by comparison with alpha. Let F denote the marked
component of E-KA/(2z). A collision fixes A and the other E components.
Its exact field-energy increment under E -> E+i delta is

    H'-H=Q(i)+2g(F,i).                                      (1)

For x,y,e in O the loaded law is

    d=(2y-x-e+F)/4.

If d is not in O, the entire collision is identity. Otherwise

    i=(rho-1)d,
    x'=x-i, y'=y+i, E'=E+i delta, e'=e, A'=A.                (2)

Only x and y are charged endpoint coordinates in this declared Gauss law. The spectator e, phase-pointer pair and reader-reference slots are separately supplied control/read carriers. Their contact operations do not also inject electromagnetic current into E. Extending the same field coupling to every such slot would be a different model requiring its own accounting.

The fixed background field may be rational. On EVERY admitted collision,
however, F=4d-2y+x+e is integral. Thus i and (1) are integral. This fact
is specific to a=1 and integral source slots; it is not a universal statement
about arbitrary coefficient ratios or arbitrary field operations.

## 2. Center, inverse and the missing energy term

S=x+y and b=F-y are invariant during a collision. Set

    v=(S+e-b)/4,    d=y-v.

Then y'=v+rho d, F'=b+v+rho d and x'=S-v-rho d. Consequently
admission is invariant, the collision inverse is its fourth power, and its
fifth power is identity, including the refused complement. This is the base
collision only, not the collision-plus-propagation macrostep.

Put K=Q(x)+Q(y), V=Q(y-e). Direct expansion yields

    Delta H=2g(F,i)+Q(i),
    Delta K=2g(y-x,i)+2Q(i),
    Delta V=2g(y-e,i)+Q(i).                                (3)

Their sum is 2g(2y-x-e+F,i)+4Q(i)
=8g(d,i)+4Q(i)=4[Q(rho d)-Q(d)]=0.
Thus K+Q(e)+V+H is conserved, and

    Delta H = -Delta V - Delta K.                          (4)

Equating field work with released potential work on all occupied states
would drop Delta K. No common scalar conversion repairs that omission.

For comparison the full weighted alternating expression is

    Omega(x,x')+Omega(y,y')+Omega(y-e,y'-e)+Omega(F,F')
      =Omega(2y-x-e+F,i)=4 Omega(d,rho d)=4B(d).           (5)

The potential coordinate and field coordinate in (5) are essential. They
are not extra independent source slots, and (5) does not assert a physical
symplectic action or a canonical selection of this form.

## 3. The literal source-only carry fails on a loaded transition

The #1924 modular function, with residues taken in F5, is

    z0=a0+a1+a2+a3, z1=a1+2a2+3a3,
    z2=3a1+2a2+2a3, z3=a1+3a2+2a3,

Consider the following SINGLE matched loaded collision:

    x=e=1, y=-1, A=0, F=4J+4, d=J,
    i=(-2,-1,-1,-1), x'=(3,1,1,1), y'=-x'.

The old source area is

    area_source=(Omega(x,x')+Omega(y,y'))/2=1.

    area_source - Delta Psi = 1-8=-7,

which is not an integer carry. Replacing source area by B(d) while retaining
the old source potential also fails: (-1-8)/5=-9/5.

This state can satisfy Gauss on the periodic 27-site carrier. Take

and zero on other directions. Translation permutes that direction's edges,

## 5. Exact correction on an occupied fixed-center cycle

For any admitted initial state, repeat the same collision without free
propagation. Set I_c=(rho^c-1)d, and W_c=H_c-H_0. Because A is fixed,

    W_c=Q(I_c)+2g(F_0,I_c).                               (9)

Let i_0=(rho-1)d and i_1=rho i_0 be the actual first and second currents.
Then I_1=i_0, I_2=i_0+i_1, and I_1-I_2=-i_1. The cyclotomic identities give

    Q(I_1)=5(A(d)+B(d)), Q(I_2)=5A(d).

Consequently the corrected common-state identity is

    5B(d)=W_1-W_2+2g(F_0,i_1).                            (10)

    chi=j^[(W_1-W_2+2g(F_0,i_1))/5].                      (11)

Cold F_0=0 recovers the old formula. In (7) the background cross term
supplies the entire phase exponent while both work cuts are zero. This
term is fixed by the actual initial field and second current; it is not
an alpha-dependent correction factor. A physical instrument for the signed
bilinear reading has NOT been constructed here.

The five-collision hypothesis is indispensable. With free propagation

## 6. Transport the metric of the actual current

The current is i=(rho-1)d, not d. Since

    (rho-1)(rhobar-1)=2+phi,
    (2+phi)^-1=(3-phi)/5,

writing h(i)=A_i+B_i phi gives

    h(d)=[(3A_i-B_i)+(2B_i-A_i)phi]/5,
    Q(d)=A_i, B(d)=(2B_i-A_i)/5.                          (12)

In particular a current admitted by (2) obeys the indicated divisibility.
Using h(J)=2-phi yields two equivalent exact identities

    Q(i)=5(A(d)+B(d)), Q(Ji)=5A(d),
    5B(d)=Q(i)-Q(Ji).                                    (13)

On a fixed-center cycle Ji=i_0+i_1 is the actual accumulated two-contact
current, so another form is

    5B(d)=-Q(i_0)-2g(i_0,i_1).                           (14)

On an arbitrary propagating trajectory, Ji remains an algebraically
transformed current. It is not automatically the next accumulated current,
field energy, an independent physical work channel, or an implemented
receiver. Equations (12)-(14) avoid retaining an old metric after merely
renaming the current. They do not select charge dressing or alpha.

## 7. An explicit phase extension on every admitted loaded trajectory

For completeness, in O/5O write ell=log j, ell^4=0. Then phi=3+ell^2 and rho=1+2ell+2ell^2+3ell^3. The displayed z coordinates are the coefficients in 1,ell,ell^2,ell^3. Multiplying by rho and substituting into f=2z0z3+z1z2 gives f(rho d)-f(d)=z0z2+2z1^2. Multiplying d by its conjugate, which sends ell to -ell, gives B(d)=2z0z2-z1^2 modulo five. Thus

    2[f(rho d)-f(d)] = B(d) mod 5.

For admitted d define

    k(d)=[B(d)-2fhat(rho d)+2fhat(d)]/5 in Z,              (15)

and set k=0 on refused collisions. After the actual collision, apply the
ordinary total contact on the pointer pair to power 3k modulo 5. On an
arbitrary occupied pointer this is a bijection preserving its sum and Q.
On (j^r,-j^r) it sends r to r+k. At d=0 the carry vanishes.

The inverse recovers the previous source/field via C^-1, recomputes its d
and k, and reverses the pointer power. It does not need an external history.
Five repetitions of one collision give sum k=B(d), because the fhat terms
telescope. Thus the source/field return while the pointer advances by
j^B(d). The complete static lift has order dividing 25, not generally five.

Define a complete propagating macrostep as this lifted collision followed
by L, explicitly assigning ZERO pointer update to L. It remains a
bijection: first reverse L, then the lifted collision. This works even
when the next collision refuses and needs no periodic emitter.

This is a NEW DECLARED EXTENSION. It agrees with the old carry on centered
ordinary source pairs x=-d,y=d, including the cold prepared orbit and (7).
It does not inherit equality with the old source-only carry on arbitrary

For an arbitrary finite sequence of admitted collisions, put

    b_n=B(d_n), ell_n=2[fhat(rho d_n)-fhat(d_n)].

The exact trajectory identity is

    5 sum k_n = sum b_n - sum ell_n,
    chi_out/chi_in = j^(sum k_n).                         (16)

All terms use the actual pre-collision states. Refused events contribute
zero. The ell sum telescopes only with an additional relation between

## 8. A finite retained actual-work receiver and complete funding

At a=1 every actual admitted field work w=Delta H is an integer by section
1. Refused events have w=0; L has zero field-energy change. Choose a finite
number N of record slots R_1,...,R_N in Z and a supplied finite calendar
c in Z/(N+1). A forward step performs the base macrostep and pointer update,
then, when the old c is 0,...,N-1, adds w to R_(c+1), and advances c.
At old c=N no archive is written. The ready state has c=0 and blank archives. The first N forward steps visit
old c=0,...,N-1 and write distinct slots. Within this ready horizon, the
calendar distinguishes acquired zero from a blank slot at each specified
inspection cut.

This unfunded map G uses exactly one complete lifted macrostep of section 7
and is a complete bijection. Its inverse decrements c, reverses that lifted
macrostep exactly once (including its pointer update), recovers its forward
work w, and subtracts w from the selected current archive. Within the first
N forward steps, every acquired archive is retained while later slots are
written. After calendar wraparound, a reused slot accumulates signed work
and can cancel an earlier value; there is no indefinite history guarantee.

Let E_core=K+Q(e)+V+H+Q(p)+Q(q), E_0=E_core+sum R_j^2.
Retain a nonnegative funding bank P and direction epsilon in {+1,-1}.
Propose G^epsilon and P'=P-[E_0(proposed)-E_0(current)].
Accept iff P'>=0. Otherwise keep every other coordinate and flip epsilon.
Every accepted edge has an accepted reverse edge. If R flips direction,
RU is an involution, so U^-1=RUR and E_0+P is exactly conserved.

For blank archives and the first N forward steps, funding
P*=sum_(n=1)^N w_n^2 is necessary at the endpoint and sufficient at every
prefix of that fixed trajectory. It is a proved cost of recording those
actual works, not a source of free work and not a physically selected
preparation rule. A later read-only continuation can retain the completed
archives, but it requires an explicitly specified reversible protocol change;
it is not the continued cyclic G just defined.

One accumulated register R+=w would instead equal R_initial+H-H_initial.
It would be a running total, NOT a retained history. The distinct ports and
calendar above are necessary to justify the stronger finite-history claim.
No finite-shell or recurrence theorem is claimed for the rational global
field: its gauge/zero modes require a separate treatment.

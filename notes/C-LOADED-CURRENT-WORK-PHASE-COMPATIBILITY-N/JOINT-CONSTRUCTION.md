# A reversible joint preparation and five equally priced coupling words

**NON-CANONICAL / L1. Conditional analytical candidate-T. No physical phase selection or Canon authority.**

A fixed ordinary-contact word can put a source into the phase of an initially coherent reference donor without knowing either phase. The donor is changed and retains the original relative phase. The construction therefore supplies a concrete joint preparation, not an attracting synchronization or a free erasure. A separate residue invariant prevents manufacturing the required donor/reference slots from a sparse source and blank or residue-zero funding.

## 1. Carrier, resources and exact question

Use O=Z[j], j primitive of order five, rho=j^2 and Q(x)=Tr(x xbar)/2. Ordinary contact C(x,y) is identity when x-y is not in 2O. Otherwise, with c=(x+y)/2 and d=(x-y)/2 in O, it gives (c+rho d,c-rho d). It preserves x+y and Q(x)+Q(y), and C^5=I on the entire carrier. A literal inverse replaces each contact by four copies in reversed chronological order.

The source is a balanced pair

    (p,q)=(j^r,-j^r).

One balanced donor pair is supplied in the opposite slot order:

    (a,b)=(-j^s,+j^s),       h=j^s.

Both r and s may be unknown. The four occupied slots have account 8 and sum zero. A fresh #1925 reference bank may also be present:

    R_k=S_k=-h j^k,         k=0,...,4.                   (J1)

It has account 20 and sum zero. The donor and this bank are assumed coherent with the same h before the joint preparation. This initial correlation is an explicit resource; the construction does not copy an unknown state or create the bank from blanks. JOINT-OBSTRUCTION.md states the exact preparation restriction.

The task is to set the source phase relative to this untouched bank using one fixed word for every r,s, while retaining all changed resources.

## 2. The 25-contact word

Define the following chronological words:

    A=C(p,q),
    K=C(p,a) C(q,b).

A always admits on the prepared source pair and changes its exponent r by 2. The two contacts in K act on disjoint edges. Since O/2O is F16 and j has order five there, both admit exactly when r=s modulo five. If they admit, both source and donor exponents increase by 2; otherwise both refuse.

Now execute, from left to right,

    P = A K A K^2 A K^3 A K^4 A.                       (J2)

This is five A contacts and twenty cross contacts, twenty-five ordinary attempts in total. There is no branch chosen by an external controller based on r or s; the inherited contact admission rule is the only state-dependent operation.

Let d=r-s modulo five. Immediately before K^k the phase difference is d+2k, since each A adds 2 and K leaves the difference unchanged. If d is nonzero, exactly one k in {1,2,3,4} satisfies 2k=-d. That whole K^k block admits and adds -d to both phases. For d=0 no K block admits. The five A contacts together add 10=0 modulo five.

Consequently, in both cases,

    P: (r,s) -> (s,2s-r),                               (J3)
    (source phase, donor phase): (u,h) -> (h,h^2/u).

Thus the source aligns with the INITIAL donor and with the untouched bank. The USED donor is not also aligned: the final source-minus-donor phase difference is still d.

Every individual slot remains a root unit with Q=2 throughout this prepared family. The change is a reversible phase-record transfer with zero change of any individual slot account, not a release of source work. The output donor phase h^2/u is a bijective function of the original r for fixed h. It carries exactly the five distinctions removed from the source port.

The literal inverse has 100 contacts and works on arbitrary occupied inputs. A shorter exact inverse has forty contacts: A^-1=A^4, K^5=I because its two edges are disjoint, and K^-k=K^(5-k). These global word identities do not assume a prepared phase input. The forty-contact count is a construction, not an inverse-length minimum.

On the prepared two-phase family only,

    P^n(r,s)=(r-nd,s-nd),       d=r-s,
    P^5=I.                                                   (J4)

For d nonzero, a second application no longer leaves the source aligned with the untouched bank. This is a controlled preparation cut, not a permanent attracting state. Equation (J4) is not asserted for arbitrary occupied four-slot states.

## 3. Attach the existing reader, keeping both records

With the fresh bank (J1), P acts only on the source and donor. After P, the #1925 reader applied to the source has relative input zero and hence returns its established labelled difference vector

    D(0)=(5,0,5,0,5).

If the untouched bank instead has an independent phase j^v, the same word still sends the source to j^s and the reader returns D((s-v) mod 5). It does not repair an initially mismatched donor and bank. This makes the coherent-donor premise directly testable.

The complete preparation has account 8+20=28, sum zero and fourteen occupied cyclotomic slots. If the source is one pair in the retained five-pair clock, the other eight clock slots are retained zeros, giving twenty-two slots and the same account 28. This account describes that supplied ready state; it is not the complete work source of #1924.

Reading disturbs the aligned source again. The complete inverse therefore reverses the five-contact reader first (twenty literal contacts), then P. The donor's phase record is retained until this reversal. All endpoints and accounts return, and the new records are erased. No free reset with a permanent copy is claimed.

Alternatively, instead of reading the aligned source, use (b,a) as the positive/negative pointer into the same fresh bank. The donor then reads relative phase s-r=-d. These are alternative receiver experiments or require a funded reset between them; this statement does not measure both untouched pairs with one undisturbed bank.

If an incoming work source had encoded a phase j^B relative to h, P transfers that record to the donor as j^-B and sets the source/bank ratio to one. It does not change B, derive a new work-phase law, or show that the reset source pointer still equals the original work-record character. Those records must be interpreted at their correct cuts.

## 4. Five words with identical readiness, support and edge counts

This successful preparation does not prove that the elementary contact law or account conservation uniquely prefers relative offset zero. Use the SAME source, donor and fresh bank preparation for all t in {0,...,4}. Let B=C(a,b), and put k_t=3t modulo five in {0,...,4}. Execute chronologically

    P_t = B^k_t P B^(5-k_t).                            (J5)

On the prepared donor pair, B increases s by 2. Since 2k_t=t modulo five, direct composition gives

    P_t: (r,s) -> (s+t,2s+t-r).                         (J6)

The source/bank ratio is j^t, and the donor/bank ratio is j^(t-d). The source/donor ratio remains j^d. The same prepared bank can distinguish these actual different couplings.

Every P_t has exactly thirty ordinary contact attempts, including a full B^5 block at t=0. Its edge counts are identical: five on (p,q), ten on (p,a), ten on (q,b), and five on (a,b). Every member preserves the complete account, ring sum and residue inventory, is covariant under simultaneous multiplication by ±j^v, and has a literal 120-contact inverse on arbitrary occupied states.

The chronological placements of the B contacts differ. Once that complete word is fixed, its relative output is determined without later phase adjustment. Account, support, per-edge counts, reversibility and common-phase covariance do not choose which word is physically required. Equal contact counts are not a proof of equal physical time or equal additional geometrical action; no separate action accumulator is included here.

## 5. A faster contact construction and a swap control

An alternative fixed preparation uses four already coherent donor pairs

    (a_k,b_k)=(-h j^k,+h j^k),    k in chronological order (2,4,1,3).

For each k execute C(p,a_k), C(q,b_k). A matching phase advances by 2 along the path 2 -> 4 -> 1 -> 3 -> 0. Every source phase therefore ends at h. The donor pairs changed are the suffix beginning at the old relative phase. For d=0,1,2,3,4 their counts are respectively 0,2,4,1,3; these five records are distinct.

This takes eight contacts and has a literal thirty-two-contact inverse. Source plus donors have account 20 on ten slots; adding the fresh reference bank gives account 40 on twenty occupied slots. The faster construction uses more prepared phase resources. Neither construction is claimed optimal over arbitrary circuits.

Whole-slot swaps provide an additional control against an overbroad impossibility claim. With a coherent positive/negative donor pair, two swaps can replace the source state by the donor state and retain the old source in the donor slots. This also has source-plus-donor account 8. It is a state exchange, and the original phase is retained. The contact construction (J2) shows that a definite preparation can also be obtained using ordinary contacts at fixed source ports, with different donor backreaction.

## 6. Relation to the other active branches

The completed [common-clock transport construction #1934](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1934#issuecomment-6705242023) already prepares supplied reference pairs by sending them through the same clock as the signal. It removes a shared clock-frame label. That result is credited to its owner and is not reproduced or re-owned here.

The present construction concerns a different operation: replacement of a source's unknown relative phase at a marked cut, with its old value retained in a counted donor, plus the preparation and reversibility obstructions. It neither integrates the full field/current action nor compiles the controller left open in that branch. The [native-U digit construction #1937](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1937#issuecomment-6705532134) uses a larger operation set; the ordinary-contact residue obstruction must not be applied to it indiscriminately.

The prior independent-review lock #1935 and PR #1942 target the older #1930 head 3d8d364cb981bb5d53bbede432fb5a4ac4181709. These new additive results are outside that pinned review and do not enlarge its acceptance scope.

The strongest conclusion here is concrete but conditional: native contacts can establish a prescribed source-to-bank phase at a declared cut, with full backreaction and inverse, provided a coherent donor and bank already exist. They do not, from account and neutrality alone, manufacture that preparation, erase the old phase, or uniquely select a physical source/reference law.

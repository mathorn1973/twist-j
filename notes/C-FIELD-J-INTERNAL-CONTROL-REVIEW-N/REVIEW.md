## 3. The pre-run parity correction and its precise effect

Frozen specification section8 states that approximate finite preparation
can give nonconstant words under a repeated context. As an existential
claim within this candidate's positive even-supported, D=I subclass,
that wording is too broad. The independent paper derivation found the
following stronger invariant before any run.

P preserves every pointer's even subspace exactly. In each clean C round,
the parity of the shift is LOW/HIGH's source-bin value. A parity branch
therefore confines the source to that context's coarse eigenspace even
if imperfect pointer coherence records finer information inside HIGH.
The next identical context cancels the preceding postcontext, and the
next active pointer is again even-supported. Its recorded bin is the
same. Induction gives exactly zero formal weight for every nonconstant
word in a repeated-context run, without requiring ideal E coherence.
The coherent proof gives the same statement before any intermediate read.

The correction was publicly disclosed before either run and appears in
author PROOF section10a. The immutable specification, operational law,
finite domains and failure threshold were not edited. This review does
not silently accept the overly broad sentence: the accepted positive
result includes the sharper even-support invariant. It does not upgrade
the actual finite within-HIGH joint state to the ideal instrument.

The independent verifier records zero nonconstant weight for the positive
repeated-context domain. Its separately retained initially odd-pointer
negative control gives nonconstant weight 13/9 for an unnormalized
reference vector of squared norm13/9. This is not a probability exceeding
one, a positive-domain witness, or a sampled outcome. The raw total
evolution of that off-domain state remains invertible.

The general error theorem must still retain all ideal-zero branches and
only grants weight<=epsilon when the ideal branch vanishes. It does not
assert that every such branch becomes positive in this particular device.
Changed contexts and normalized branch-state differences continue to
need the complete comparison. Thus the finding corrects a scope
overstatement; it does not falsify the exact control equality or its
preparation-error bound. No remaining counterexample to those intended
mathematical claims was found in the proof/source comparison or completed

## Paper finding frozen before any run

The positive even-pointer subclass has a stronger repeated-context
invariant than the cautionary wording in the specification's section8:
with D=I, its repeated outcomes are constant even for imperfect pointer
coherence. The verifier checks zero nonconstant weight there. A separately
retained odd-pointer negative control has positive nonconstant weight.
The proof states why; neither statement identifies formal weights with
actual outcomes. The general epsilon comparison must still keep every
ideal-zero branch, whether its actual weight is zero or positive.

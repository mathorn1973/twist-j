## Declared targets and falsifiers

1. Establish a bounded positive self-adjoint operator with integer local
   matrix coefficients. An incorrect bound or nonlocal displayed term
   defeats that claim.
2. Prove an exact two-dimensional compact eigenspace at energy 1, and
   classify the rest of the spectrum. An extra point eigenstate, endpoint
   atom, missing singular measure or false dark eigenpair defeats the
   affected claim.
3. Derive the physical endpoint resolvent, normalized bright spectral
   density and the lower second-sheet pole, keeping the square-root sheet
   explicit. A sign, normalization, cancellation, residue or pole error
   defeats the affected claim.
4. Prove bright survival tends to zero and a controlled large-K rescaled
   survival limit. A nonvanishing survival remainder or invalid limiting
   argument defeats the corresponding claim.
5. A same-session analytic strengthening supplies a finite-K absolute
   exponential approximation by comparison with a normalized Lorentzian.
   Its entire bound and assumptions must be proved, and exact exponential
   decay or relative tail accuracy must not be substituted for that bound.
6. State all conservation and robustness claims at their proved scope.
   The small-perturbation isolated spectral-cluster argument does not assert
   arbitrary perturbation robustness or preservation of compactness.

Every target is analytical. Incomplete proof is OPEN/STOP, not a successful
finite experiment or a negative result for all possible models. A later
scientific executable requires a separately published complete source and
domain before its first execution; none is included in this package.

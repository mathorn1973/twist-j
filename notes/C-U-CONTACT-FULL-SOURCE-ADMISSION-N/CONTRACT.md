## 1. What is fixed, and what remains a choice

The preceding [occupied-SUM probe][sum] supplies the additional classical
contact on seven field coordinates. In the centered chart

```text
(x,u,y,w,q,r,s)=(p1-1,p4-3,p1p-4,p4p-2,q,r,s),
h=y-x,
C:(x,u,y,w,q,r,s) -> (x,u-2hs,y,w+2hs,q,r,s).             (1)
```

Arithmetic in (1) is in F5. Equality is literal complete-coordinate equality.
The inverse replaces s by -s in the two shifts while retaining the actual
stored s. The contact is a global permutation; the complete counter-driven
native continuation is not a global permutation. The prior proof establishes
source-coordinate retention relative to the declared free motion, not a
physical identification of all source degrees of freedom.

The representative (1) was chosen in the earlier, explicitly restricted
covariant-contact class to produce the desired occupied SUM increment. This
history is exposed. Compiling that chosen operator from an independently
described control alphabet does not retrospectively select it as a natural
law. Many other operators can be compiled from the same alphabet.

This note asks two separate questions:

1. Can (1) be implemented exactly in a previously specified conditional
   control model, without hiding an initially blank work register?
2. In an explicitly stated quantum comparison, which meanings of preserving
   the source are compatible with this informative contact?

The answer to the first question is a circuit theorem. The answer to the
second distinguishes classical copying from preserving an arbitrary quantum
state. Neither answer supplies the missing physical carrier or native gate.

## 2. Declared comparison model and preservation contracts

For the quantum comparison only, adopt seven distinguishable five-level
systems with orthonormal field-label bases and the tensor carrier
H_S tensor H_D = C^5 tensor (C^5)^(tensor 6). D includes the entire original
checkpoint, not merely the two receiving pistons. The added source is S.
Reference systems, when present, are distinct and not acted upon.

The reference unitary U_C maps every basis vector |s,v> exactly to
|s,C_s v>, with no relative phase. The comparison uses ordinary complex
linear quantum operations, density-operator equality and partial trace.
These are additional premises, not a derivation of native quantum states,
the Born occurrence law, or a tensor factorization from J. In particular,
the six checkpoint coordinates here are six five-level basis labels, not
three canonical coordinate-momentum pairs.

Unless stated otherwise, the receiver preparation rho_D is fixed and
independent of the unknown source. For an external reference A, arbitrary
rho_SA is allowed but the initial receiver is still independent. All
physical controls, work stores, histories and environmental outputs must be
included in the admission problem even though the circuit theorem treats
its admitted pulses as ideal controls.

| Preservation requirement | Precise meaning | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Classical value | Each basis input s returns the same s. | Proved for (1). |
| Source diagonal | All probabilities in the declared s basis are unchanged. | Proved for U_C, including correlated inputs. |
| One source marginal | One particular rho_S happens to be unchanged. | Possible; does not certify other states or reference correlations. |
| Universal complete source | The induced channel is the identity on every source density operator for one fixed independent receiver preparation. | Incompatible with an informative receiver output; exact conditions in SOURCE-PROOF. |
| Universal source/reference | Every rho_SA returns unchanged after discarding the receiver. | Equivalent to the preceding identity-channel condition under this product-preparation contract. |
| Complete joint information | The whole isolated source-plus-receiver state is reversibly recoverable. | Proved for the adopted contact unitary; not local source preservation. |
| Clean apparatus | All extra physical resources return in a common state with required correlations and conservation laws accounted for. | Not provided by a circuit that takes externally scheduled controls as given. |

These are endpoint contracts for the complete contact or net protocol.
The circuit restores its computational labels after completed gadgets;
intermediate data and source states need not be unchanged during pulses.
Preservation at every physical instant is stronger and would require the
actual continuous control Hamiltonians and their entire prefix evolution,
not just the exact endpoint operator proved here.

The contact acts trivially on q,r and is independent of them, so it
preserves their full operator algebra at contact. It also fixes x,y basis
values, but depends on x,y; their off-diagonal operators need not be fixed.
The full joint source s,q,r is not protected merely because q,r are.
Subsequent free native motion of q,r is a separate, already stated comparison.

## 4. A discriminating comparison outside the SUM target

Fix the centered receiver basis labels x=0, y=1, u=w=0, q=r=0. This has
h=1, outside the supported occupied-SUM orbit where h is 2 or 3. Formula
(1) predicts, for each basis source s, the two labels (u,w)=(-2s,2s),
with every other label unchanged. It therefore tests the complete chosen
contact beyond its original unit-gain SUM task.

For the same independently prepared receiver and source
|+>=5^(-1/2) sum_s |s>, the joint output is

```text
5^(-1/2) sum_s |s> |x=0,u=-2s,y=1,w=2s,q=0,r=0>.
```

Consequently the reduced source is I/5, whereas its diagonal populations
are unchanged. With X|s>=|s+1>, the source expectation changes from 1 to 0;
measuring its Hermitian real/imaginary parts would distinguish this from an
unchanged coherent source. This is an exact conditional prediction, not a
reported measurement. Preparing |+> and measuring such operators require
their own accepted resources. Classical population-only readout cannot
perform this phase test; compare the separate [point-readout lane][point].

The effect is not a stochastic loss in the full closed contact: the joint
state remains pure. The phase information is in joint correlations. A
shared inverse restores both input systems and removes the new record;
retaining a copy elsewhere leaves the source correlated with that copy.

## 5. Why a field point table does not specify a Hamiltonian source kick

A different, unadopted interpretation would add a coordinate kappa and
equip (x,u,y,w,s,kappa,q,r) with the polynomial symplectic form

```text
Omega=dx wedge du+dy wedge dw+ds wedge dkappa+dq wedge dr.
```

This is an extra eight-coordinate formal model over F5. It is neither the
seven-system quantum carrier above nor a real Hamiltonian or SI energy.
No native symplectic structure or conjugate source coordinate is inferred.

Under i_X Omega=dH, the polynomial H=-s h^2 gives a shear with the unit
parameter map

```text
u'=u-2hs, w'=w+2hs, kappa'=kappa+h^2,
x'=x, y'=y, s'=s, q'=q, r'=r.                           (2)
```

This follows by differentiation: H_x=2sh, H_y=-2sh, H_s=-h^2,
and the coefficients stay fixed along the shear. The parameter here is
formal field-valued translation, not elapsed physical time.

But the equally explicit polynomial lift H*=-s h^2+s h^10 has the same
u,w derivatives in characteristic five and gives

```text
kappa'=kappa+h^2-h^10.                                  (3)
```

For every F5 point h^10=h^2; thus (3) fixes kappa while giving exactly the
same point contact (1). Indeed H* is zero as a point function, although its
formal derivatives need not vanish. Polynomial differentiation does not
descend to arbitrary finite-field point functions: the polynomial z^5-z
vanishes on every field point but has derivative -1.

Even after choosing the quadratic lift H, a source-only term +4s leaves
the receiver contact unchanged and changes the kick to h^2-4, which is
zero on the prepared SUM orbit. Thus neither a universal nonzero source
kick nor a particular source energy follows from the seven-coordinate
table. A physical polynomial/continuous lift, symplectic structure and
energy dictionary must be fixed independently before such inferences.

The separate NON-CANONICAL [native pentit note][pentit] also excludes a
common nondegenerate alternating form for the linear parts of the native
generators in its stated six-dimensional realization. We do not import
that as a Canon theorem or a prohibition on wider models; it reinforces
the need to name, rather than presume, a different symplectic lift.

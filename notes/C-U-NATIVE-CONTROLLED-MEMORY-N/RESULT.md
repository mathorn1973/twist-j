## Continuation boundary

The controlled exchange uses the initial transient before synchronization.
For every origin-zero preparation, from a common m>=3 onward the generator
word is shared by all retained head labels. In differences,

    Delta x_n = (-1)^(n-m) P^(theta_(n-1) XOR theta_(m-1)) Delta x_m,

where P swaps the two entries within each piston pair and fixes q,r. The
source pair, memory pair, q and r then evolve separately. No common ready
complete block can receive a new source-dependent value from another block.

BOUNDARY.md proves the stronger precisely scoped statement for a fixed affine
target with a common complete state at m: such a target is again common at
infinitely many later times, so no reader of its current state, even with the
counter, can retain a nonconstant new payload at every sufficiently late tick.
This is a direct analytic consequence of existing native structure, not a
new general no-writing theorem. Nonlinear subsystem choices, retained history,
off-sheet resets, new couplings and broader architectures are outside it.

The canonical four-tick paired-port shifts preserve this candidate's entire
logical reading because the readers depend on q,r only through q+r. They
therefore preserve old records while shifting the port; they do not create a
record that such a pulse happened. This candidate does not supply a second
independently selected write, a cycle counter, or a growing archive.

actual native words, fixed-piston-pair swap/hold response, and complete

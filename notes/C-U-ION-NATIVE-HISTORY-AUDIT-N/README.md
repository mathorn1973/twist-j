# Native step: current-state control and retained history

**NON-CANONICAL analytical interface audit.** Reservation
[#1366](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1366).
Public authority remains Canon v97. The two local contact constructions in
[#1365](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/1365) remain accepted in
their conditional ideal scope; this note does not optimize or reopen them.

The first missing operation is already the native part of the first update.
Two actual preparations require the same complete data output there. Thus
a new physical operation must transfer their distinction across the cut
between prescribed data and an accounted remainder. Arbitrarily complicated
unitary control confined to the data cannot do this. Merely allocating a
register, or closing a motional loop, supplies no such transfer.

The [proof](PROOF.md) establishes this restricted obstruction and spells out
a reversible retained-selector target. That target is **not** an admitted
ion gate. The table below identifies the missing physical inputs before any
positive native-step probe or preparation-to-nine construction is pinned.
No new scientific program was executed or imported for this audit.

## 2. Actual first native step

All entries below are physical level labels in F5. Write
`P=(p1,p4,p1p,p4p)`, `kappa=sum(P)`, and `t=a2+1`. The common fixed-K
preparation and the first contact require

```text
before W_1: n=0, S1=s, S2=t, R1=(0,0,0,0,1,0), R2=(0,0,0,0,0,0);
after  W_1: n=0, S1=1, S2=t, R1=x_s=(0,0,0,0,s,0), R2=(0,0,0,0,0,0).
```

Here s ranges over all five values; it is a present port label, not an
external history identifier. The native selectors on these post-contact
data give `j1=s`, `j2=1` because theta_0=0. R2 must undergo btil and become
`(0,0,0,0,3,0)`. R1 must undergo:

| Present s | j1 | Generator | Complete R1 output |
| ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | a | (0,0,0,0,0,0) |
| 1 | 1 | b | (0,0,0,0,4,0) |
| 2 | 2 | c | (2,1,2,1,4,0) |
| 3 | 3 | d | (2,1,3,4,3,1) |
| 4 | 4 | e | (2,1,3,4,3,1) |

S1 remains 1, S2 remains t and the common counter becomes 1. Consequently,
for a fixed a2, s=3 and s=4 (a1=2 and a1=3) require the same complete
fourteen-coordinate data output, not just the same pointer reading.
This table follows by direct substitution; it is not a new enumeration of
the 25 complete histories.

With faithful orthogonal input codewords, a common pure initial remainder
and exact reversible execution, the two corresponding output remainders
must be orthogonal. Known input-dependent phases cannot replace that
distinction. If the initial remainder is mixed, its purification may be
included in the account; the data-only obstruction below needs no pure
motion assumption.

## 5. A restricted impossibility result, not merely missing synthesis

Enlarge the contact library for this argument: allow **any** unitary V on D,
with arbitrary fixed phases and any inter-data addressing, followed by any
history-independent unitary on the remainder. Thus a completed native step
has the form `V_D tensor W_remainder`; the counter endpoint is the same for
both histories. This class is more generous on the data than #1365.

For the two actual orthogonal data inputs s=3,4, denote their common required
full output codeword by eta. With success defined by that rank-one data
projector, orthogonality gives

```text
p_3 + p_4 <= 1,   hence min(p_3,p_4) <= 1/2
and worst-input complete-data error >= 1/2.
```

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the short operator proof. This is an obstruction
to exact or below-half-error complete-data realization in the declared
factorized class; no attainable optimum for a physical pulse library is
claimed. It applies to the actual first
step and does not replace its target with a full SWAP or an arbitrary-state
task. It does not hold for the coarse archive reader alone.

Every completed ideal LS loop and leading-order carrier in #1365 factors
from motion. Their finite compositions therefore cannot make motion the
new history store while retaining that endpoint property. Allowing even
arbitrary closed-loop addressing among the fourteen data ions would not
remove the obstruction. A memory ion, an unclosed mediated interaction,
work-source record, controlled measurement or another accounted degree of
freedom could change the class by carrying the lost distinction. None is
silently supplied by the local contact result.

This proof does not use exchange symmetry. In particular, #1365 admits
different stationary residual one-port phases L_S,L_Q during its raw loops;
their cancellation over the affine echo does not imply that every raw
allowed pulse is port-symmetric. Reusing #1361's symmetry premise here
without that qualification would be invalid.

As a separate support check, using only the two fixed contact pairs leaves
the piston factors outside the source-information path. Such a literal
embedding cannot produce an a2-dependent W2. This does not establish that
all extended ion architectures lack a suitable path; it identifies an
additional obligation besides storing the merger information.

In the ideal harmonic algebra, LS closure is an operator identity on the
oscillator and does not require a Fock cutoff. For a physical error claim,
#1365 separately chose input support 0,...,10. Neither this audit nor a
symbolic composition proves that an implemented native step maps the real
inherited motion into that validation domain. A candidate must give a
compatible endpoint map or bound, including correlations, for each handoff.

The abstract retained-selector lift permits arbitrary correlated old H as
an untouched reference. It does not prove that the actual device can leave
it untouched. Erasing M after a collision while keeping all other outputs
fixed is impossible; moving its information into H, a battery, bath or
controller is a transfer that must be counted, not free uncomputation.

## 7. Phases, time, energy and the next admissible test

The compulsory data target remains the basis-coordinate law. PROOF.md gives
a zero-phase coherent permutation as a stronger **mathematical target** on
data and branch memory. A physical candidate may declare known phases, but
must specify their action on superpositions and inherited correlations.
The laboratory frame phases from #1365 remain in the composed frame ledger.
No diagonal phase is mistaken for an orthogonal history record.

Every branch is selected from present data. The physical word and its
endpoint time must be fixed for every input; branch-dependent protocols
would require explicit controlled timing/padding and output accounting.
A prescribed finite clock schedule may provide theta_n for n=0,...,8, but
does not compute a data-dependent j. A physical clock and its outputs are
still resources. No unbounded physical counter is required for this horizon.

For independently assigned spectra the first-step endpoint contribution is
`Delta E_D = sum_i [E_i(output_i)-E_i(input_i)]`, with memory, fields, motion,
switching and source changes separately included. In the two colliding
inputs the output data energy is identical; their input energies can differ.
Thus even a zero-energy contact does not fund the native step. A proposed
work source may itself retain the distinction, but then its complete output
must be part of the nonfactorizing implementation. No numerical work or
duration is assigned to an uncompiled native map.

A positive native-step probe is ready to freeze only after it supplies:

1. One supported physical information path for selector evaluation and a
   compiled conditioned native action, or a direct composite realization.
2. Actual memory encoding/preparation, retained old information, full output
   map, admitted correlated input domain and declared phase equality.
3. Finite common times, independently admitted parameter ranges, work at
   every substep and endpoint motion/auxiliary conditions compatible with
   the next operation.
4. Complete-data success and leakage rules, occupied-archive disturbance,
   treatment of all failed readings and a uniform error contract fixed
   before results. One failed pulse search may reject a candidate, not the
   whole declared class.

These fields are not closed by the current contact model. Consequently this
deliverable is the requested interface audit, not a new formal execution
asserting a physically available native step. Repository/CI checks validate
the documents; they are not a scientific run. No sealed probe, Canon file,
status, dictionary or prior HOLD is changed.

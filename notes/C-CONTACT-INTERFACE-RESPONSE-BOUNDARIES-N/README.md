# Contact-interface response boundaries

NON-CANONICAL / L1 / conditional analytical candidate.
Owner: A. M. Thorn, contact-interface response review session, 2026-11-05.
Reservation: [#1918](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1918).

This note records what can already be proved about the proposed contact
interface, and separates it from computations that still need accessible
source custody. It supplies complete analytical arguments. It does not
supply a new scientific run, a physical load law or an alpha determination.

The central distinction is between an entry of a fixed contact matrix and
a coefficient of a parameterized scalar response:

    3/5 = [x^2](1+x/5)^(-5).

The absence of the rational entry 3/5 from Z[j][1/2] is not by itself an
obstruction to this Taylor coefficient after an independently specified
normalization and readout. A separate projector-load theorem does exclude
that coefficient in its explicit interface class.

## What is established here

| Statement | Exact scope and consequence |
|---|---|
| Fixed projector-load bound | For a prescribed unit input, fixed unitary mixing, matched unloaded readout and loads (Id+x P_k)^(-1), a linear coefficient -1 forces Re[x^2] at least 1. This excludes 3/5 in that class. |
| Projection versus constrained inversion | Passive reading, projection after each solved load and a constraint imposed during the solution give different responses. Finite positive stiffness leaves a strictly positive Schur correction in the coupled direction. |
| Four-contact current transfer | An explicit prepared staircase transfers delta*(-1,-1,-1,-1,4). Four pair contacts are necessary and sufficient on those five slots for nonzero delta. Preparation and restoration are separate obligations. |
| Returned-auxiliary decoupling | On a fixed branch, exact restoration implies block decoupling when permitted free-input differences span the entire free space. A fixed or correlated bank can defeat that premise. |
| Arithmetic consequences | Under decoupling, each accepted cross-block edge has multiplicity divisible by five. A two-slot free block is a power of the direct contact. A five-slot two-level endpoint cannot change under the stated contact-only hypotheses. |
| Resource and clock boundaries | Retained attenuation requires an explicit destination for the conserved account. A clock-only segment that is exactly identity on every reached state can be erased without changing the endpoint transfer. |
| Finite and statistical scopes | Fixed integral norm shells are finite, despite an infinite formal admitted-matrix group. Dyadic itinerary frequencies require a specified uniform residue measure. |

Every statement is conditional on the hypotheses in its proof. In
particular, the cross-edge count is necessary, not sufficient. A
one-parameter encoded family is not independent variation of every
coordinate that its author calls free. Repeating a scalar readout five
times requires a full continuation contract; it does not follow from one
successful input/output reading.

## Disposition of the accumulated work

The fixed 322-contact five-input witness already has public replay in
[#1908](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/1908). Its complete
integer-domain classification is already in
[#1912](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/1912): that one word
succeeds exactly at -2,-1,0,1,2. These need no duplicate probe here.

The common action/work/clock construction in
[#1910](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/1910) and overlapping
composition in [#1917](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/1917)
remain conditional smooth constructions with declared added structures.
The native paired-phase work boundary in
[#1915](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/1915) concerns its own
arithmetic reading. Their original restrictions remain in force. All five
are public draft results, not newly accepted Canon statements through this
note.

The new short proofs belong in this analytical note now. A numerical scan
cannot replace their hypotheses or upgrade their scope. Independent
mathematical review is the next acceptance step.

The reported larger switched-current constructions, detailed clock traces,
120-order census and broader catalyst search need their actual source
package, literal word, complete preparation and continuation contract
before an audit can accept their counts or endpoints. No such computation
is certified here. A future formal replay should freeze an exposed
candidate, exact falsifiers, complete state and immutable verifier before
its first new scientific execution. It should preserve resource consumption
and failures of repeated use, then use the existing two-architecture gate.

## Next interface decision

A successor should fix the following data independently of the desired
Taylor coefficient: the full integral carrier, admissible preparation
family and its rank, literal contact word, changing output/load coordinates,
ready-state return conditions, the parameter x, and the scalar readout.
If x is only defined on discrete inputs, its formal or analytic continuation
also needs a contract.

The next useful positive construction would show how a counted output
channel receives the account while the working device returns, and how the
particular clock dynamics determines the response. A theorem excluding a
precisely frozen interface class would also be a substantive result.
Neither route may identify x or choose a readout after seeing the desired
coefficient. Physical work, action normalization, charge response and alpha
remain open.

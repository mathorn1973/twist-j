## Mathematical sources and attribution

The following Canon links refer to the fixed authority base above.

- [U-FINITE-HISTORY-CLOCK-PHASE][history] proves recurrence for fixed legal
  native/clock windows and its eventual-constancy consequence. Its declared
  class excludes the extra persistent feedback memory treated here.
- [U-FINITE-READER-INDEPENDENCE-1, exact proof][reader] supplies the earlier
  fixed-window event recurrence result, with its class restrictions.
- [Finite permutation cycles][cycles] give the established closed-finite
  recurrence boundary. The new controller comparison retains the infinite
  recurrent clock rather than pretending that whole native U is finite.
- [TM-CHECKPOINT-HULL-STABLE-IMAGE][hull] supplies the canonical synchronized
  graph and its reversible restriction. This graph, not the union of both
  raw trace sheets at one bit, permits a common finite-fiber chart.
- [Occupied-contact proof][sum] fixes the actual first and second readout
  intervals and the counter-based one-time contact. It remains unchanged.
- M. G. Nerurkar, [Ergodic continuous skew product actions of amenable
  groups][nerurkar], Pacific Journal of Mathematics 119(2), 1985, 343-363,
  p. 361, proof of Corollary 2.4, invokes the general fact that a distal
  extension over a minimal base decomposes into minimal sets. This provides
  background attribution. The note's needed finite-group case is proved
  directly; none of that paper's genericity or ergodic-measure conclusions
  is imported into the controller claim.

The standard minimal/group-extension argument and substitution minimality
are not claimed as new general mathematics. The specific contribution is the
complete reversible-controller interface, its applicability to the native
stable sheet, the occupied-write obstruction, and a precise finite-horizon
escape within an explicitly enlarged comparison model.

## Physical and normative ceiling

The controller class is explicitly chosen for an applicability test. It is
not a classification of all physically admitted apparatus. Neither the
clock supply nor the access to its legal windows is physically derived.
No energy/work, quantum coherence, source-selection, outcome law or physical
L5 event follows from the recurrence argument.

The [physical apparatus manifest][apparatus] still requires independently
admitted physical fields and complete family/equality certificates. This
note fills none of its unresolved physical identifiers and changes no QDD
owner disposition. Public Canon v101 and its 26 H/O entries are unchanged.

# Analytic interface restrictions and composition

## 1. Exact invariants

On an admitted contact the mean stays fixed and the difference rotates by
rho. Since rho conjugate(rho)=1, the sum of both Hermitian squares stays
2h(s)+2h(d). Thus sum, H and Q are unchanged. Each coordinate changes by
a multiple of rho-1, so its residue modulo1-j is unchanged. Refusal is
identity. Admission persists along an accepted orbit and rho^5=1, proving
the total inverse C^4. SWAP is an isometry and transports residues.

Since (sum z_i)^2<=4 sum z_i^2, Q(z)>=sum z_i^2/2, strictly positive for
nonzero z. This positive-definite form is used below.

For the old G_t all six residues are(0,0,0,0,0,2).
No contacts can manufacture a residue3 at any labelled port, and even with
SWAP the full multiset has no3. Supplying the seed(J,-J), residues(2,3),
removes this necessary obstruction; it does not prove reachability.
Replacing the two varying height ports by a fixed-account clock while
returning every other initial coordinate would force10(1+t^2) to be
constant. The retained R_t in the contract prevents that invalid requirement.

## 2. Fixed-trace affine isometry

For one specified admission/refusal trace, each operation is either its
K-linear accepted matrix, identity, or a permutation, K=Q(j).
Their product M is a linear isometry for summed Q on K^m. On every input
realizing that trace it agrees with the actual guarded program.
An affine family A+tD therefore has affine outputs on that trace.
This is a piecewise statement, not global linearity of the guarded contact.

If three distinct heights in the five-input family follow one trace and
their clock pair has constant account6, its affine form a+t b has a
quadratic account. Three equal values force its quadratic coefficient
Q_pair(b)=0, so b=0. The three output clocks coincide.
An injective five-phase converter therefore needs at least three traces.

There is a sharper constraint for adjacent integers t,t+1 under r=t+2.
Their initial squared distance is2Q(B)=10. Their desired clock distance
is2Q(J(1-rho))=10: J(1-rho)=1-rho^2 and Q(1-rho^2)=5.
If they shared a trace, isometry would force the remainder distance tozero.
Equal remainders and equal clock accounts would then give equal total
accounts. But39+10t^2 differs from39+10(t+1)^2 for integer t.
Thus every adjacent pair must have different traces, for any finite common
stock. A successful candidate must satisfy this necessary test.

For the stronger optional nondemolition contract retaining the entire affine
input exactly and returning all other non-clock outputs to one common state,
any two different phases must have different traces: the retained input
already exhausts the entire initial squared distance, leaving no positive
distance for distinct clock outputs. Hence all five traces must be different.
The actual contract does NOT impose this stronger endpoint.

## 3. No all-integer periodic phase converter in this class

Let the initial full state be A+tD in O^m for ALL integers t, with m finite
and all common stock included. Take any finite fixed contact/SWAP word
with L contacts. It induces a finite partition of Z into dyadic cylinders
t=r+2^e n, e<=L. On each cylinder every intermediate and final coordinate
is affine in integer n.

Proof by induction on the word: on one cylinder a contact's difference is
a+n b. Modulo2O it depends only on n modulo2. Admission is uniform on the
cylinder, or splits it into its two parity children. Substitution
n=2q+epsilon makes each child uniform; admitted division by2 is integral
for both constant and slope. Refusal and SWAP are linear. Each contact
requires at most one split along any path. This proves a finite exact
partition, independent of whether anyone computes it.

If a designated output coordinate has bounded Q on all integer n in a leaf,
its affine slope iszero by positive definiteness. It is constant on that
entire leaf. But2^e is invertible modulo5, so t traverses all five residues
on every leaf. Hence the target rho^(t+c)J, for any fixed c, cannot be
realized on all integers. The five target elements are distinct.

This no-go permits arbitrary finite common stock and arbitrary retained
spent outputs. It excludes exactly finite words with affine integer input,
not the finite five-height target, nonlinear preparation, an unbounded
iteration, or an input-dependent external program.
Equivalently, every bounded output of this class is dyadic-periodic; a
nonconstant five-periodic output is impossible.

## 4. Why a bare clock seed and zero helpers do not suffice

Consider the narrower class with only L_t=(u+tB,u-tB), one(J,-J) clock
seed and any number of zero helpers; allow contacts and fixed SWAPs.
For even t the two height values remain of form u +/- t rho^a B when they
contact one another. Their mod2 class is always u.
Clock values remain opposite rho^b J and have mod2 class in J<rho>.
Zero helpers remainzero. These three families cannot interact.

Indeed in F16=O/2O, J=u^2 and u^3=j^4. Thus u has order15:
u^15=1, u^3!=1 and u^5!=1 (if order5, u^3=j^4 would give u=j^3,
contrary to1+j!=j^3 in the degree4 field).
The quotient u/J=u^-1 is not in the order5 group<rho>.
So every cross-family contact refuses; within-family operations depend only
on the fixed schedule. SWAP merely moves those families along fixed paths.
The output clock evolution is the same for t=-2,0,2, and cannot yield three
different target phases. A target clock value must descend from the clock
family: height and zero descendants have residue0 modulo1-j, while the
target clock has residues2,3. Extra zero slots cannot repair this.

This does not obstruct the eight-slot contract: its old helper
X=1+j-2j^3 is u modulo2, and the additional value2 belongs to the zero
parity class. The old helpers provide interactions missing from this
narrower preparation. Sufficiency is still open.

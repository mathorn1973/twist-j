Owner: A. M. Thorn. Public claim: issue #1750. Apache-2.0.

This is an abstract supplied-gate clock and output-channel construction,
not the geometrically local compiler being developed separately in #1749.
The predecessor candidates #1746 and #1748 are unchanged and unaccepted.

PROOF.md constructs a finite positive time-independent Hamiltonian with an
energy-sharp finite battery. It executes the complete target circuit exactly
at its endpoint in a fixed total-energy sector, preserves the accepted reduced
physical channel, and gives explicit read and post-read retention intervals.
The microscopic generator includes reverse clock edges. It is not a strictly
forward-only hopping Hamiltonian.

The new account counts clock, auxiliary registers and pulse arrangement but
makes physical position/coin and logical-message degrees degenerate. This is
not the spatial-square account of #1748. The battery becomes correlated with
clock and retained histories; the complete product-state comparison of that
predecessor is not asserted. The full initial state remains nonstationary
under the new generator. Battery and clock preparation are still resources.

General clock/energy/channel lemmas are self-contained. The concrete inherited
p>1/20000 and bad-band fraction<1/10^6 remain conditional on #1746's pending
source/spectral proof. The small exact audit complements but does not replace
the analytical proofs. PREREG.md must be publicly pinned with code before the
first execution. SOURCES.json fixes the actual inputs; RESULT.md and RUN.md
will record only work actually executed.

No native implementation, spatially local Hamiltonian, physical energy
identification, SI time calibration, terminal event, useful reset, unlimited
archive, Canon edit or promotion is claimed. A finite high-fidelity read window
is not an absorbing halt. Repeated measurements require their own instrument
and occurrence contract.

## Carrier and equations

Two distinguishable walks on a periodic ring of L sites, 4 dividing L,
with the full four-dimensional coin; mu>0 and real contact angle g.

    U_mu(k) = exp(i k Z) (I+i mu X)/sqrt(1+mu^2)
    H_mu    = sqrt(1+mu^2)(U_mu^*-U_mu)/(2i)
    H0      = H_mu tensor I + I tensor H_mu
    P       = sum_x |x,x><x,x| tensor I_coin
    K_g     = I + (exp(-ig)-1)P
    V_g     = K_g (U_mu tensor U_mu).

The initial vector a has momenta (0,pi) and coin |-x> tensor |+x>.
It is a normalized H0 eigenvector of energy 2mu.
No positive-band projection, identical-fermion constraint, physical
electron identification or prepared outgoing target is assumed.

## Frozen questions

1. Derive the complete first moment, including the coincidence compression
   PH0P, and a second-moment check on the same full carrier.
2. At L=4 and mu=2, derive the complete energy distribution for arbitrary
   g, then specialize g=2pi/5. Include normalization, all energy signs,
   zero-energy channels and the distinction between unshifted and
   nonnegative accounts.
3. Construct or exclude a finite nonnegative sharp battery completion for
   this preparation. State its complete carrier, inverse/unitarity,
   actual conserved account, preparation, energy range and retained/lost
   coherence.
4. Prove any level-count and readiness minimum only in this exact class:
   a sharp initial battery energy, nonnegative battery spectrum, exact
   additive conservation, all five prescribed energy outputs, no
   additional energy store. Degeneracy does not count as extra energy
   levels. Unrestricted ancillary states/classes are not covered by that
   minimum.
5. Determine whether an arbitrary initially stationary COMPLETE source
   can produce the pure contact target under exact additive conservation.
   Include the controller and reference resources in that stationarity
   premise. Give a quantitative target-state bound if obstructed.
6. State the consequence for the phase/sine-energy question without
   selecting a unique physical energy, inventing a native-U bridge,
   deriving electron mass, or changing the 2/atan(2) comparison.

## Failure conditions

- Any missing output channel, incorrect spectral multiplicity,
  normalization, moment or contact matrix identity defeats that assertion.
- Any failure of unitarity, complete-carrier conservation, nonnegative
  readiness or the asserted reduced output defeats the battery claim.
- A member of the frozen sharp-ready class with fewer required levels or
  lower sufficient readiness defeats the corresponding minimum.
- A stationary complete source satisfying the displayed conservation
  assumptions and attaining the nonstationary pure target defeats the
  source obstruction; a fidelity above the stated bound defeats that bound.
- A source/basis/hash/authority mismatch is integrity STOP, not a
  mathematical counterexample.

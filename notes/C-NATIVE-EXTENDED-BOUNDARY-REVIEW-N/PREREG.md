## 5. Failure threshold and disposition

Zero algebraic tolerance; exact arithmetic only. Each listed claim
receives exactly one scoped disposition:

- `ACCEPT` at the exact stated scope: the reviewer's own check
  reproduces the inference on the whole declared domain, quantifiers
  included;
- `CHANGES_REQUIRED`: a false literal step, an unjustified quantifier,
  a missed generator/domain point or a scope overstatement, shown by
  an exact counterexample or a named gap that the pinned text does not
  close;
- `STOP`: a definition, pin or custody item needed to decide the claim
  is missing or inconsistent, so no honest verdict exists yet.

An isolated notation defect does not by itself refute a separately
proved statement, and a correct restricted statement does not rescue
an overclaimed summary; both directions are recorded separately.
Inherited Canon results are identified as inherited, not re-owned.

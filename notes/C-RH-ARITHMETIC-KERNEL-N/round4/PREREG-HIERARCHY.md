# C-RH-ARITHMETIC-KERNEL-N / large-prime-count hierarchy

NON-CANONICAL paper continuation of issue #2087, draft #2089. Public source supplied at `09ae5168191eed9cee484c08c5d65f58b31494d1`. No authority, repository changes or scientific computation. Exact cutoffs, the signed weight `w(d)` and the residue kernel `S_chi` are inherited. Paper statements have candidate-D status only after proof.

1. Split the actual tail `d>X^(2/3)` by the number of split prime factors `p>X^a`, for fixed `1/3<a<1/2`. Establish or falsify the exact matched identity that counts each such large prime with multiplicity one and the exact unordered two-prime formula with the full cofactor transform `T(X/(pq))`. Explicitly account for distinctness, factors repeating a selected prime, all strict endpoints, and any lower-tail subtraction before analytic replacement.
2. Derive the signed two-prime asymptotic by a uniform cumulative prime-pair estimate followed by coefficient summation and Mellin moments. Determine its first nonzero term and the resulting signed coefficients for exactly one large prime, exactly two, and their union. Decide if a fixed cutoff can cancel the first union term and determine its next surviving order and sign, or state which coefficient would remain unresolved. A computation-free sign proof or a negative result with exact inequalities is preferred.
3. Relate the count split to the inherited `R=H+B` at the same argument whenever the definitions allow. State fixed-vs-growing uniformity, the actual residual exposed by the decomposition and precisely why this proof supplies no square-root estimate. Do not use summaries of neighboring work as evidence. Do not pursue the separate moving support-count asymptotic if the actual-tail theorem closes.

Falsifiers include any missed equality endpoint or duplicate divisor, any PNT replacement of a signed cofactor without controlled variation, a lost density or factorial, or disagreement between the claimed first surviving coefficient and the exact identity. No program, numeric search or verifier is authorized by this paper-only registration.

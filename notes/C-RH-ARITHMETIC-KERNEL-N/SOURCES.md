## Primary external sources

- H. L. Montgomery and R. C. Vaughan, *The large sieve*, Mathematika 20
  (1973), 119-134, https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300004708 . The note proves
  the weaker absolute-constant form it uses directly; it does not claim the
  sharp classical constant.
- C. Aistleitner, I. Berkes and K. Seip, *GCD sums from Poisson integrals
  and systems of dilated functions*, JEMS 17 (2015), 1517-1546,
  https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/12412 . Prior gcd/dilation
  methodology, not a source for the exact new kernel statement.
- M. A. Bennett, G. Martin, K. O'Bryant and A. Rechnitzer, *Explicit bounds
  for primes in arithmetic progressions*, arXiv:1802.00085v3,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.00085 . Fixed modulus five is sufficient; no
  uniform theorem in a growing modulus or GRH assumption is used.
- E. S. Lee and N. Leong, *New explicit bounds for Mertens function and
  the reciprocal of the Riemann zeta-function*, arXiv:2208.06141v4,
  Theorem 1.1, https://arxiv.org/html/2208.06141v4#Thmtheorem1.1 . The classical
  unconditional rate suffices. No numerical constant from their computations
  is imported. A later v5 exists; the cited theorem is explicitly pinned to v4.
- A. Hildebrand and G. Tenenbaum, *On a class of differential-difference
  equations arising in number theory*, Journal d'Analyse Mathematique 61
  (1993), 145-179, https://tenenb.perso.math.cnrs.fr/PPP/Diff-diff_eqns.pdf .
  Classical Dickman normalization and uniqueness; the prime-tuple coefficient
  identification and positivity needed here are also proved in the note.
- Y. Fujisawa and M. Minamide, Theorem 1.3 with number field Q,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.4348 , for the classical Mertens/RH equivalence.
- J. Kaczorowski and J. Pintz, *Oscillatory properties of arithmetical
  functions I* (1986),
  https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~gerg/teaching/592-Fall2018/papers/1986.Kaczorowski_1.pdf .
  Antecedent for the signed power-window method.
- M. Ward, *The prime divisors of Fibonacci numbers* (1961),
  https://msp.org/pjm/1961/11-1/pjm-v11-n1-p31-s.pdf , classical divisibility
  background. The coefficientwise lifting argument is given explicitly.

## Scientific boundaries

All new proof statements remain candidate-D pending external review. The
recorded finite checks are candidate-C only. Source checks and repository CI
do not promote them. The root and helpers share one research session and
are not independent external witnesses. A single x86_64 lane does not
satisfy the public two-architecture scientific gate.

The result removes a moving support from an arithmetic criterion and locates
necessary large cancellations. It does not establish the remaining criterion,
a new RH proof, an improvement to the full Mertens exponent, a native TWIST-J
physical mechanism, or any cross-layer lift. The masked `2/3` obstruction
does not apply to the full unmasked sum; the fixed-`u` asymptotics do not
apply uniformly to `u` increasing with the argument.

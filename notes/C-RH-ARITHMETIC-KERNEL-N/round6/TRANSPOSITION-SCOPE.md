NON-CANONICAL, no authority. Paper only. The initial [prospective scope](PREREG-LOW-HEIGHT.md) covered exact completion and transfer of the actual prime/cofactor masks. This narrower record is result-exposed: the short-support identity, cube-root sufficient threshold, and diagonal p=q term were derived before this separate record was requested. It is not a blind independent preregistration. No scientific computation has been or will be performed here.

The frozen claims to oppose are (i) the exact signed discrepancy between the full primitive conductor mask and the raw d mask, supported on e≤D/z, (ii) its translated weighted square-norm estimate uniformly down to z=D^(1/3), including application of the Jackson multiplier, and (iii) the original-star replacement with T at smaller arguments, with the mixed term retained and repeated-prime term included. Falsifiers include an extra large prime in the cofactor, an unaccounted diagonal, using the sharper norm for arbitrary subthreshold z, or silently transporting a masked pair difference by the norm square estimate. The narrower h≤log^14 restriction is outside the target-norm claim. The earlier √D release is retained for its separate signed asymptotic.

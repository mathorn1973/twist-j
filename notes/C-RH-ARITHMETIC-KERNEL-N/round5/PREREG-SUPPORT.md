# C-RH-ARITHMETIC-KERNEL-N / weighted smooth support

NON-CANONICAL, candidate-D only after proof. Paper-only continuation of issue #2087 and draft #2089 at `d26ca6caab5dda3875333f9d2ba73b74b55ba674`. The arithmetic object is the actual squarefree split weight `s(d)=(-2)^omega(d)` with the exact residue kernel `S_chi(n/d)`, inherited boundary `d^3>n^2`, and finite-block target of order `X^(2+epsilon)`. No Git or remote writes, no verifier and no numerical experiment.

1. Derive the exact first two logarithmic scales of the unsigned Rankin bound with `P^+(d)<=c(log X)^2`. Identify how split density times unit weight enters the sharp constant, and distinguish strict `O(sqrt X)` from `O_epsilon(X^(1/2+epsilon))`. Attempt a matching paper lower bound at the critical constant, with explicit precision required of the weighted saddle-point concentration. If this cannot be justified, give the exact scope of the upper bound and avoid claiming sharpness of the constant.
2. Test an unsigned deletion of all supports with largest prime at most a quadratic logarithmic cutoff, allowing the cutoff to depend on the dyadic block or argument. Prove whether this deletion preserves the actual one-sided-window or all-dyadic square criterion. In the opposite direction, seek a terminal slab of original coefficients with `S_chi=1` demonstrating the power boundary for a fixed superquadratic logarithmic exponent. A sign claim for the entire smooth piece would require more than an unsigned counting example.
3. Distinguish an estimate for deletion from the time-domain sum `T` from a deletion of Fourier conductors or determinant pairs in `P_X^rem`. Look for a genuine finite-block consequence without assuming compatibility of support, rational conductors and numerator cutoffs. State what signed problem remains and whether an unsigned cutoff could erase all relevant long supports.

Falsifiers include a lost split density, an uncounted squarefree restriction, a cutoff moved independently of its argument, a signed conclusion from an unsigned bound, or a purported pointwise sharp lower bound that only estimates an average or counts terms with vanishing residue kernel. Scope and thresholds are frozen before deriving results.

   complete inactive controls are exactly
   pi(u,eta)=(eta xor c,u xor d(eta)); involutivity gives precisely the
   two complemented/uncomplemented exchanges. The corresponding one-way
   and sign-blind restrictions exclude inactivity. Restricting both

2. **Factor-access record boundary.** For any fixed pre/post words in
   the complete factor-compatible permutation class, identity and a
   factor-trivial contact have identical factor readout on every full
   input. The supplied Ucal leaves this class; the actual state-selected
   U is a differently typed source and is not covered by the obstruction.
3. **Boundary-specific conditional selection.** In the classified
   family, endpoint invariance under S_2 plus nonconstant chi selects
   exactly 00, whereas the corresponding literal W covariance forces
   11. Preserving h after K, E_W and W has respectively all-four,
   exactly-00 and only-11 consequences. The additional principles
   have no independent physical admission in this note.
4. **Complete-control group and prefix separation.** With the remaining
   v100 alphabet fixed, the 24 involutive profile-00 W have identical
   complete E/K and complete A1--A18 macros, but split into 16 Alt(F)
   and 8 Sym(F) cases. Explicit prefix and conjugating-word witnesses
   separate them. Reapplying the constructive compiler to changed
   letters is possible for even targets but does not identify the
   old syntax-defined T_alg or its particular full q lift.

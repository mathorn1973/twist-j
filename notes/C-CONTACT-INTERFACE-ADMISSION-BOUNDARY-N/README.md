# Contact interface admission boundary

**NON-CANONICAL, L1. Reviewed conditional proof: candidate-T.**
No new candidate-C evidence or scientific run. Owner: A. M. Thorn.
Public reservation and delivery record: [#1398][issue].

The independent admission of the control class remains open. This note
proves what several proposed interfaces cannot do, gives exact conditional
selectors for profile 00, and shows why equality of contacts does not
identify the controls used inside subsequent words.

## Results

1. On every inactive nonzero h pair, the complete source-table control
   has exactly the form

       (u,eta) -> (eta xor c, u xor d(eta)),  h=(-1)^u k.

   It transfers the old source orientation to the bit and the old bit
   to the output orientation. If involutive, it is either the exchange
   (u,eta)->(eta,u) or the exchange with both labels complemented.
   One-way access and sign-blind control decisions exclude inactivity.
2. The two separate complete quadratic piston records determine h^2
   and every endpoint coefficient chi. They do not determine the two
   control decisions of an inactive source-table pair. A controller
   restricted to those records has profile 11.
3. Arbitrary fixed words whose factor output is independent of q cannot
   distinguish identity from the factor-trivial contact by a factor-only
   record. A q-to-factor interface is a necessary extra capability for
   this word class; the given Ucal has it. The actual state-selected U
   is outside this theorem's class.
4. Within the four-profile class, commutation of K with the specified
   right-scaling S_2, together with nonconstancy of chi, is equivalent
   to profile 00. These are unadmitted selection premises. Imposing
   that literal covariance on W with the bit fixed instead forces 11.
5. Keep W_a and the rest of the compiler alphabet fixed. Among the
   24 involutive profile-00 controls, all have identical complete E_W
   and K and preserve the complete A1--A18 macro maps. Nevertheless
   16 generate Alt(F) and 8 generate Sym(F), according as the h=0
   branch preserves or flips the bit. Explicit words have different
   prefixes and conjugated seed supports. The original T_alg and its
   q lift cannot be transferred merely by substituting a letter.

All contact statements use one fixed port i=x or i=y and hold on the
complete carrier, including unknown q, arbitrary references and occupied
eta. The group equality is specifically on F=F5^10 x {0,1}.

## What is still missing

An independent rule must first admit the complete source/control class,
the accessible signed reading and reversible register interaction, and
the reference access needed to observe the contact. Only then can a
physically justified principle select 00 within that class. A requirement
equivalent to 00 does not independently justify itself.

The specified C, W_a, source preparation, autonomy and physical readout
remain separate premises. QUADRATIC-MEMORY-NATIVE-CONTACT remains O.
Canon v100, registry, frontier, gates and the predecessor package are
unchanged. These written proofs are not a formal probe or a new gate.

## 4. Evidence required to close an individual item

A review records the source hash or Git head, exact statement and
admitted inputs, proof reference, any counterexample, and one scoped
verdict. Missing physical admission stays an explicit premise. A failed
identity is a failure of that identity; failure of a chosen realization
is not an unrestricted impossibility theorem. Infrastructure PASS and
source-hash agreement are recorded separately from proof acceptance.

No Canon, registry, frontier or gate status follows from importing the
notes or opening their consolidation PR.

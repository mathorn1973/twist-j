This addendum sharpens the finite-dimensional reading of the binary Nyman–Beurling route. It does not change the review's main verdict: the missing asymptotic admissibility step remains a twisted Nyman–Beurling approximation problem at RH strength.

## 1. Rate, not membership, is the closure debt

The projection census in lane A of PR #1020 computes exact finite distances for the same residue-function family

\[
r_k(n)=n\bmod k.
\]

For the mean-channel application of PR #979, the closure-strength estimate does not merely require that a target lie in the closure of the residue span. The span lemma gives a quantitative lower bound of the form

\[
\|Y\|^2\ge 2J^2 d_K(a_j)^2-\frac14\|D\|^2,
\]

so a bound `||Y||^2 = O(J)` forces

\[
d_K(a_j)^2=O(1/J).
\]

Along the dyadic cut schedule `K` grows exponentially in `J`, hence this is an `O(1/log K)` rate requirement. Membership `a_j in V` without a rate is therefore strictly weaker than the closure target used by that lane.

This is the useful cross-reference to the present review: the unresolved routing/admissibility step is not repaired by finite membership evidence or by showing that a few averaged approximants improve. It requires an asymptotic approximation theorem with sufficient quantitative control.

## 2. Finite diagnostics do not settle convergence

The finite census through `K <= 72` and the sharp-cut/averaged data through the inspected dyadic cuts are exact finite computations. The correct statement is:

- the inspected sharp cuts do not show sustained decrease;
- the inspected Cesaro averages improve over the same finite range;
- neither observation proves convergence or nonconvergence of the infinite sequence.

This wording supersedes any compressed sentence saying simply that the sharp cuts "do not converge" or the averages "do converge" at the tested scope.

## 3. A separate exact finite obstruction in the H norm

The 2026-09-16 verifier added to PR #1020 solves the complete weighted least-squares problem on `n=1,...,144` over

\[
E_{72}=\operatorname{span}\{r_2,\ldots,r_{72}\}
\]

for the constant target `1` in the auxiliary H norm

\[
\|x\|_H^2=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{|x(n)|^2}{n(n+1)}.
\]

It proves

\[
\frac{7601923}{10^9}
<
\min_{q\in E_{72}}
\sum_{n=1}^{144}\frac{|1-q(n)|^2}{n(n+1)}
<
\frac{7601924}{10^9}.
\]

This is more than `7000` times the threshold `9/2^23` used by the finite certificate target (29) of PR #979. Therefore no coefficients supported only on `r_2,...,r_72` can satisfy that target.

This finite obstruction is not an obstruction to density of the full infinite residue span. It says only that the support class through 72 is quantitatively far from the particular threshold required by (29).

## Independent exact breaker

The standalone standard-library Python 3.10+ program uses Fraction and
integer arithmetic. It reads no repository/runtime data, invokes no
predecessor or author code and uses no floating point, eigenvalue fitting,
random sampling or trace-norm approximation. Its domain is finite exact
internal mathematical data, not arbitrary Python-object parsing.

The following audit is fixed before any execution. These counts describe
loops, not successful observations. Universal conclusions rest on PROOF.md.

1. **Full local reflection.** At L=3,6,8 and every edge, construct sparse
   columns on the complete 2L pointer times two bath states. Check reflection,
   inverse, normalized columns, odd-sector identity and all 436 complete
   basis inverse cases. On all 3020 pointer matrix units with fresh bath
   zero, compare the direct retained-column partial trace against a separate
   dense Q/J calculation, including odd/even cross units. Check channel
   completeness, the projector generator, dyadic entries and the dirty-bath
   E-fidelity loss. The flat energy is scalar on every affected factor;
   the proof covers all untouched C labels and bath correlations.
2. **Sweep inequalities.** At L=3,4,6,8,11 construct the no-jump product,
   its telescoping loss, unnormalized derivative rows and the overlap
   coefficient matrix. Check the exact row identity and absolute row/column
   sums. Exact rational Schur complements certify five positive-semidefinite
   differences: graph gap versus 8/L^2, four times loss versus graph,
   loss versus 2/L^2, full adjoint gain versus (2/L) loss, and full gain
   versus (4/L^3)(I-P_E). These five dimensions are audits of the analytic
   proof, not spectral extrapolation to 613.
3. **Finite forward densities.** At L=3,4,6,8, take every basis density,
   I/L, P_E, P_(d0), P_(s0): 37 states. Follow three full sweeps, giving
   148 state/boundary cases and 627 collisions. Check trace, positivity,
   fidelity monotonicity, the rational bound from the initial fidelity,
   accumulated bath marks, exact E stationarity, and dyadic closure whenever
   the input is dyadic. Check the identical-population first-edge control.
4. **Retained correlated bank.** For L=3, two pointers and six distinct bath
   cells, evolve the unnormalized vectors |0,0,0_R>+|1,1,1_R> (norm squared
   two) and |0,1,0_R> (norm squared one), with all baths zero. Use sparse
   amplitudes retaining every bath bit and reverse all six reflections.
   Compare the traced pointer/reference state with a separately composed
   two-pointer Q/J density map. Check the unchanged reference marginal,
   both marginal fidelity estimates and the product-ready union bound.
   Project the full vectors onto the one-dimensional ready bank; construct
   the orthogonal remainder and check the exact output-environment marginal
   decomposition, including the vanishing partial trace of cross terms.
   This audits the correlated-state proof without numerical trace norms.
5. **Phase and exactness controls.** At L=3,5,6 enumerate all 104 edge-sign
   patterns. The signed difference matrix must have rank L-1 exactly when
   the sign product is +1, otherwise rank L. In every consistent case check
   the constructed dark density under every edge channel and distinguish
   nonconstant phases from E. Check 1/613 is not dyadic. General complex
   phases and all finite dyadic sequences are treated analytically.
6. **Finite handoff and budgets.** For every one of the 148 densities saved
   in group 3, embed it into the full 2L pointer and compare with E. Use a
   three-label controlled translation with codes (0,2,1), all nine source
   matrix units and both parity outcomes: 2664 branch cases. Check exact
   linearity of actual-minus-ideal, trace preservation of the branch sum,
   same/opposite parity reread, and the ideal reduced coefficients, including
   the coherence between distinct even codes. This small interface does not
   reconstruct the Stage-C writer. For K=0,2,4 and seeds t=0,1,2 use
   n_i=(i+t) mod 3 to check nine finite, uniquely addressed bath schedules,
   exhaustion and unchanged flags; K=0 still has one loading pointer and
   zero C archive capacity. Finally at L=613, m=1,2,5 and target error
   1,1/2,1/10, certify both worst-dirty and basis-blank budgets, 18 cases,
   using the exact bound (1-a)^n <= 1/(1+na), a=4/L^3. Choose the integer
   n by the resulting rational inequality; do not form huge powers or
   simulate the sufficient loading budget.

## METRO: retain the exact obligation, not a weaker replacement

The public Canon already separates the four admitted arrows from common
blocking. The registered generated relation `approx_red` is a finite zigzag
of those admitted arrows. Its definition does not itself establish the
independent completeness obligation.

| Part | Accepted public position | Closure consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A and C | `METRO-REDUCTION-ARROWS` [C] records the four arrows, exact preconditions and pointwise transport. | These parts are already covered; replaying them is not a parent closure. |
| B | `METRO-FORBIDDEN-WITNESSES` [C] discharges the five named entries under their ratified readings. Any additional registered forbidden entry still needs its exact witness. | Do not invent extra forbidden entries or describe the existing five as still unwitnessed. The already discharged five do not decide D or E. |
| D | Common q^k blocking remains undecided in the public contract. | A complete decision must retain every length-k word, padding convention, exponent-residue vector, pointwise stream, scientific decision and terminal value at the registered scope. |
| E | The generated zigzag relation is defined; a public completeness theorem for the registered class is not supplied by the new additions. | Unpublished normal-form or classification candidates cannot be counted as accepted closure evidence. |

Unpublished finite-transport and restricted-blocking candidates may be useful
future proof inputs. A result restricted to a chosen encoding, padding rule,
start domain, regular-language presentation or fixed carrier must remain at
that scope unless a separate theorem proves it decides the whole registered D.
No such candidate is silently substituted for D in this audit.

In particular, **a restricted blocking no-go is not automatically the parent's
registered negative falsifier**. Common blocking is not yet one of the four
admitted arrows. The parent's negative condition requires either a transformation
that is both allowed and forbidden, or an admitted arrow changing a frozen
decision or terminal value after exact transport. Failure of a proposed
blocking construction does not establish either condition. A complete decision
of D would have to be proved and accepted as such; the other parent obligations
would still be assessed separately.

The new circuit's common-fixed-lattice obstruction likewise concerns its own
default settings and common carrier. It is neither a METRO blocking theorem
nor an impossibility theorem for the complete physical apparatus class.

## Consequence for the v78 fold

The new public circuit result has substantive positive content: it constructs
the complete prepared and sequential conditional maps, distinguishes coherent
uncompute from retained fine paths despite equal first weights, and proves its
stated class invariance and lattice boundary. Its selected account also proves
energy and lifetime-record preservation through archival reset.

These results should be stated at their earned mathematical scope. The
existence of that construction does not identify Nature's apparatus, select
one exclusive realized outcome, establish physical terminality, or classify
all admissible physical implementations. Its negative lattice theorem does
not acquire those missing universal quantifiers. Consequently the release
can strengthen the theory without changing any O status or weakening any O
decision condition.

**Prepare the mathematical source at common ready `(q,r)=(0,1)`.** This
constant is independent of the source and target outcome. The complete

double collisions. The selected choice allows all 625 original piston

are still assumptions. The arbitrary-head merger and apparatus-only

**Use exact source recovery for virtual history reconstruction.** Given a
computable source/history function and fixed start/calendar, its complete
finite history can be regenerated from native state and counter. The proof
also gives the exact factor criterion for every origin-zero preparation
domain. This supplies no material archive, locally accessible writable
cells, physical erasure/reset or resource-cost model. An arbitrarily long
computed output is not itself evidence for physically supplied memory.

**Reject a common onset distribution as a repair of the existing first-hit
protocol.** Two supported sources have the same first LOW/HIGH function
at every phase but different required ratios, `1/6` and `1/26`. This rules
out every common onset law for the complete fixed chronological class,
including uniform onset. The accepted deterministic order and correlations
are now exact, so an occupancy count cannot silently become a trial law.

**Keep permutation scanning as a conditional alternative.** For every
nonempty occupancy set, uniform permutation counts give an unbiased first
occupied slot independently of the source. The theorem is universal and
does not use a fitted target ratio. It nevertheless requires a new order
carrier, its law, retained scan position and, for independent trials,
independently renewed orders. The primitive U has not been shown to supply
these resources. Source-dependent gap-balanced onset kernels are another
mathematical alternative documented by the proof; neither is adopted as
physical sampling evidence or selected after a measurement.

The separate [deterministic permutation profile](V80-DETERMINISTIC-PERMUTATION-PROFILE.md)
also composes the chosen native inverse with explicit enumeration of all
slot orders. It specifies every individual symbolic trial outcome and the
complete virtual record, with exact whole-cycle counts and no imported
uniform permutation measure. Its period costs `1024^2 * 1024!` native cuts;
the schedule and physical controller are not derived, and its first trial

model definition, with no new T or physical status.

**Use three fixed attenuation observables as the sharp conditional mixed
selector.** The full real/complex CP theorem extends beyond pure rational
branches. The identity channel is forced by preserving the first three

counterexample. Idempotence and complete record symmetry also admit

## 1. Source acceptance is not closure of all premises

SOURCE-ACCEPTANCE.tsv pins the actual public source revisions and reviews.
At this snapshot all selected source PRs are unmerged drafts. Their scoped
mathematical reviews do not themselves amend the Canon. The present
proposal does not claim a blind review or independent scientific execution.

For each nominated statement, a later fold must choose one honest route:

1. Retain the full explicit hypotheses and accept the resulting conditional
   implication, with complete definitions sufficient to identify its class.
2. Independently establish and admit the missing hypotheses, then state the
   corresponding stronger result with those accepted dependencies.
3. Defer the claim and remove every sentence or dependent claim that needs it.

Calling a substantive existence, convergence or uniform-success premise a
definition does not perform route 1 or 2. A reviewed consequence may be
correct conditional on a premise that has not itself been accepted.

## 4. Admission obligations for the proposed insertion

Each must be resolved at the final frozen proposal revision before the
corresponding claim is folded. PASS here means documentary/mathematical
admission at its named scope, not physical validation.

1. Match every consumed proof to its immutable Git source and exact reviewed
   revision; later source edits require a new disposition.
2. Check every theorem quantifier and initial-state domain, including the
   26-label versus 25-label distinction and arbitrary passive references.
3. Verify that all complete channels retain failed and dormant branches;
   normalize only the explicitly selected joint events.
4. Retain the complete old-memory and output-slot state, including
   correlations and the displaced waste. No deletion by reset is admitted.
5. Keep finite-system, finite-battery, finite-horizon and finite-error
   quantifiers distinct. No exact-forever claim follows from them.
6. State support size, physical diameter/radius, strength and incidence
   separately, with the actual local dimensions and layout.
7. Keep original spectral Q, rounded energy, occupation/helper account,
   conserved contact account and continuous-time generator distinct.
8. Verify the claimed unconditioned versus conditioned energy bounds;
   selection may alter an energy distribution without violating conservation.
9. Preserve the sharp-store target's reduced dephasing and the complete-source
   stationarity assumption. Source variance is necessary, not sufficient.
10. Resolve every named inherited premise by one of the three routes in
    Section 1; install the exact canonical definitions and dependency rows.
11. Check that any source declared outside the selected core supplies no
    hidden conclusion to CORE, the registry, or a downstream statement.
12. Compare the eight new rows with existing claims for duplication and
    scientific scope; preserve every prior claim and live open obligation.
13. Review the actual thematic insertion, not just its source PRs. Independent
    acceptance of a source does not automatically accept a rewritten theorem.
14. For the eventual fold, install the original proof evidence or sufficient
    self-contained proofs, generate all normative ledgers/hashes, and run the
    complete required two-architecture replay and release audit.

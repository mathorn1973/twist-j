`EXACT` requires matching the frozen conclusion, carrier and observable;
it does not borrow an implication between A and B. `CERTIFIED` requires an
exact nonempty N=5 regime satisfying all hypotheses. `EVALUABLE_AT_5`
requires the needed constants, domains and uniformities to be explicit or

hypotheses. Merely existential threshold constants do not certify that
regime. Constants appearing only in the theorem conclusion require their
exact quantified existence and uniformity, not numerical evaluation unless
the frozen target requires it. `COMPLETE` means every consumed theorem step
has been accounted for at the same scope, with no missing dependency
imported by reputation.

For a target, `ADEQUATE` requires all four favorable semantic values and
`AUDIT_INTEGRITY=PASS`. `NOT_CERTIFIED_BY_FROZEN_SOURCE` requires a complete
bounded source audit documenting a specific absent certification or target
mismatch, not merely failure to find a passage. Any unresolved target,
dependency, completeness or integrity question routes `STOP`. Documented
non-instantiation may be a valid audit result: a positive N=5 theorem is not
a prerequisite for running a correctly specified adequacy audit.

source adequacy, leaving TWIST-J action membership/comparison and the named

  `|P|<=1/1000`, and `I_E=[8*pi/5-1/1000,8*pi/5+1/1000]`.
  It has zero free incoming relative-bound weight. Its physical incoming
  state uses the incoming receiver wave operator, not a finite-time

## 4. Separate conditional preparation supplement

**NON-CANONICAL; not a premise of the proposed theorem.** The available
finite audit is single-architecture; no earned Canon claim is assigned.
Use the unchanged checkpoint rules and counting inner product of
[U-GALOIS-FIBER-CODE in Canon v104](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/blob/e6240e2689bb22aab5f02bad90b75d3692bb96d9/canon/CANON.md#L3107).
For `x=(a,b,c,d,q,r)` over `F_5`, write `X_z={x:sum(x)=z}`. At the common
origin-zero clock the first three selector bits are `(0,1,1)`. Let `E_n`
be the first `n` updates and `N_n|x>=|E_n x>` their `+1` point pushforward.
Each `E_3|X_z` is a bijection onto `X_1`; denote its preimage of `y` by
`x_z(y)`. The five-sheet prefix partitions are singletons, then
`{0,2}|{1,3,4}` at both intermediate ticks, then one merged fiber.

Choose distinct endpoints `y_+=(0,0,0,0,1,0)`, `y_-=(1,0,0,0,0,0)` and
the endpoint isometry `B|+>=|y_+>`, `B|->=|y_->`. Set
```text
b=(i,-i,i,1,i)/sqrt(5),    c=(1+2i)/sqrt(5),
V|+>=sum_z b_z |x_z(y_+)>,
V|->=sum_z conjugate(b_z) |x_z(y_-)>,    Lambda=diag(c,conjugate(c)).
```
Since `N_3 N_3*=5 I` on the endpoint range,
`S_0=N_3* B/sqrt(5)` is the uniform coherent fiber splitter. Let `D_b`
multiply plus-fiber entries by `sqrt(5)b_z` and minus-fiber entries by
their conjugates. Then `D_b` is unitary and `V=D_b S_0`; the `sqrt(5)`
factor is required because `b` is already normalized.

The middle plus amplitudes are `2i/sqrt(5),1/sqrt(5)`, the minus column
is conjugate, and different endpoint fibers cannot meet at a prefix.
Their final sums are `c,conjugate(c)`. Thus exactly
```text
V* N_n* N_n V=I_2 (n=0,1,2,3),       N_3 V=B Lambda.
```
Readout is `B*` on its range. In the fixed symmetric/antisymmetric basis,
`Lambda=(I+2i sigma_x)/sqrt(5)`. No target-coin powers are used.

The coherent splitter, correlated phase mask and preparation are supplied
operational premises. The adjoint is not an executable inverse native
tick, and arithmetic access to `J` does not supply or select these
operations. Here `B!=V`: another use needs preparation/reset and clock
accounting, not the invariant relation `N_3 V=V Lambda`. This conditional

It supplies no Born, detector or photon identification and no premise

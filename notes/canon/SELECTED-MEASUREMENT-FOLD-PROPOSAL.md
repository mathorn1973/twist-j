**PUBLIC; NON-CANONICAL; prospective only.** Public Canon v90 remains the
authority. This document proposes the disposition of
[P-QDD-SELECTED-MEASUREMENT-LAW-1](../../probes/P-QDD-SELECTED-MEASUREMENT-LAW-1/)
after its immutable proof, exact audit and public acceptance records are

not activate a release. The associated choices and build order are in
[SELECTED-THEORY-PROGRAM-2026-09-20.md](SELECTED-THEORY-PROGRAM-2026-09-20.md).

## 1. Intended Canon change

Add the explicitly selected effective measurement theory `ETH-QDD-1`. Its inputs are the
specified coherent Galois preparation, finite-dimensional CP instruments,
the stated three-attenuation preservation law for HIGH, one-outcome Born
occurrence, and externally controlled records and preparation/reset channels.
Its output is the complete ordered law and post-state accounting for every
declared finite experiment, with exact equality of the specified maps and
records. Every auxiliary carrier and its initial correlations belong to the
experiment specification.

This is a theoretical completion under stated additional assumptions. It is
not a derivation of quantum probability, physical preparation or a material
apparatus from autonomous `U`. The Canon should state that distinction at the
first introduction, not only in a distant disclaimer.

5. Review and merge that fold under the normal policy. Any activation, tag and
   release is a separately declared step with its own readback. This probe and
   these notes perform none of those authority-changing actions.

adequacy, remain distinct questions with their own evidence requirements.

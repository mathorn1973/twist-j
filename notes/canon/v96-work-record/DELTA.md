## 1. Three claims, with definitions kept out of the registry

**FIELD-CONSERVATIVE-CHAIN-LAW.** For every finite integer N>=2 on the
literal 32N-1-coordinate carrier and the specified integer C,D,K,P,S,A_f,L
and ordered matter endpoints, the total guarded G and the raw-field F are
bijections; the fixed G;A;B;F schedule and each fixed individual cut have
the stated complete inverse. Every primitive preserves full energy,
spectators, each reacting-vector sum, every actual node charge, divergence
and Gauss-defect entry, and nonnegative resources. Contacts retain occupied
old values. The four-layer support bound is two stored-block edges per
macrostep in either direction. Every finite-energy orbit is periodic from
its initial state. This is a selected mathematical architecture, not a
native-U derivation or physical energy law. Evidence: PROOF Sections 1-5,9;
#1310 and the narrow selected inputs from #1304/#1306/#1308.

**FIELD-CHAIN-FIRST-WORK.** Under that law, for every finite N>=2 and every
integer H(w)=1, the displayed zero-spectator preparation of energy 41
delivers two units first at boundary N-1 and uses them in the first receiver
R-to-AM reaction in step N. The receiver's original complete tuple returns
to readiness at N+1; this is finite transferred work followed by local
reversal. The specified equal-energy offimage source and every individual
fixed cut keep the ready receiver fixed for all nonnegative times.
Evidence: PROOF Sections 6-7; #1312's all-N induction plus the #1310
complete-state law. Finite enumerations do not establish the universal
quantifier. The charged N=3 account is a retained illustration under the
first claim, not a fourth claim.

**FIELD-LOCAL-WORK-RECORD.** On the 32N-coordinate extension by one neutral
C5 receiver coordinate with chosen constant energy 1, the accepted-event
writer Ghat_t(c,p)=(Gc,p+e(c)) has inverse
(Gc',p'-e(Gc')). Its extended layers and inverses project exactly to the
original layers. The original invariants and finite-shell recurrence remain.
From p=0 the fixed local reader p!=0 records any first accepted R-to-AM
event at j on each boundary j,...,j+7, eight boundaries spanning seven
elapsed macrosteps. In the positive chain preparation j=N and the initial
extended energy is 42; the old receiver returns at N+1 while HIT persists.
The specified offimage and cut controls remain BLANK for all time. There
is no asserted first reset at N+8, arbitrary-ready-pointer guarantee,
permanent memory, source-provenance certificate in p, or physical reading
apparatus. Evidence: PROOF Sections 4-9 and #1316.

The provisional earned scope of each is **conditional theorem candidate at
L1**, supported by exact proof and existing executed audits. An eventual T
row requires separate fold review/disposition; this proposal does not assign
T to the public registry. Any failed formula, carrier/inverse/account
identity, admissible counterexample to the quantified timing/controls, or
violated stated retention/projection refutes the relevant mathematical
clause. Hardware failure is not its falsifier.

Definitions to place once before these claims: complete carrier and literal
equality; graph and matrices; raw split and exact image admission; literal
R/AM/ZM; guarded gate; resource swaps and chosen chronology; fixed cuts;
ready preparation; C5, constant pointer energy, event predicate and reader.
The device's epsilon=0.500 J, 10 s macrostep and component architecture are
realization choices, outside these L1 definitions and outside new claim IDs.

## 5. Realization and release boundary

#1318 is an open device design and acceptance contract. Its documentation
does not qualify a capacitor, assembled cartridge, cell, complete device,
or a blinded campaign. This delta contains no MEASURED result and assigns
no physical PASS. Any later reserve theorem must state its exact assumed
loss/injection/control bounds and nonempty certified preparation set; any
hardware or campaign result needs its own actual evidence and scope.

The mathematical fold can be considered separately from hardware success.
Its remaining integration decision is review of these three exact scopes,
dependency closure and retained reproductions. Canon content application,
status award, public authority change, release-form commit, merge, tag and
immutable release require the separate authorized fold/release procedure.

## Review of the D3 variance bound

**Recommend T for `D3-FLUX-VARIANCE-BOUND`.** Fix a finite even period $L\ge2$, inverse temperature $\beta>0$, the six oriented FCC bond labels at each site, the divergence-free Gibbs ensemble over all winding sectors, and $G=5I_4-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T$. With $q(v)=v^TGv/2$ and $M_x=L^{-1}\sum_e d_eE_e$, the bound is $\beta\langle q(M_x)\rangle/(8L)<1/4$. Bond labels retain the lifted displacement even at $L=2$, where several labels connect the same endpoints. The result is proof-first. The written derivation predates the frozen computational audit. [Preregistration and derivation](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/blob/bc73a66b0f7aa3985702710c956d781beee50a19/probes/P-D3-ZWORK-VAR-BOUND-1/PREREG.md)

The normalization can be checked before contracting the covariance. Write $X=LM_x$. Four positive-$x$ directions enter and leave every vertex. Hence the angle-gradient term vanishes identically, and $\sum_e d_e^2=2L^3$. The Gaussian covariance terms for $X$ and $M_x$ are $L^3G^{-1}/\beta$ and $LG^{-1}/\beta$, respectively. The trace contraction leaves $1/4$ less a positive dual-current contribution. One positive-$x$ bond with integer dual current $(1,0,0,0)$ has squared dual length $2/5$ and positive integrated weight. This proves strictness at every finite stated $L,\beta$. The three-edge crossover in the audit is a numerical localization using truncated sums. The proposed theorem uses neither its location nor a uniqueness assumption. Fixed-flux free-energy differences, a thermodynamic-limit phase and physical coupling lie outside this statement.

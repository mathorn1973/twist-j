## What this would add to the selected theory

The accepted predecessor specifies one-system instruments, Born occurrence,
ordered histories, records and renewal as a selected effective theory.
The new candidate gives an explicit two-wing extension: tensor composition,
one marked maximally entangled source, the same five contexts at each wing,
independent uniform external setting controls, local instrument and record
laws, both marginal equalities and a full local-protocol no-message theorem.

The distinctive exact result is a separation between tests on two settings
and the complete five-setting behavior. Every CHSH restriction stays at
absolute value at most two, but

    B=2 sum_k p(LL|k,k)-sum_(k!=l) p(LL|k,l)

has Bell-local bound two and selected value 35/16. The ordered unequal-pair
sum has twenty terms. All 1024 deterministic response pairs are bounded;
convex expansion covers the entire setting-independent stochastic local
class. The proof of the Bell bound is 3c-ab<=3c-c^2<=2 for integer
intersection size c, not a numerical fit.

For these fixed effects the quantum maximum is 35/16, attained by one pure
density. The Bell-operator spectrum is 35/16 once, 5/4 four times and
-25/16 eleven times. The declared depolarizing family violates this witness
exactly for v>14/15; this is not a full locality threshold.

The theorem does not identify a physical signal or a native cause. Its
source, tensor rule, Born law, controls, setting independence and isolation
remain supplied inputs. No claim of novelty or departure from ordinary
quantum mechanics is needed for this result to make the selected program
more explicit and falsifiable.

## 1. Purpose

Public Canon v74 currently combines two logically different statements:

```text
methodological/physical frame:  physical reality is one closed whole;
model-level axiom:              the declared model is the integer J-Cayley plenum.
```

The sentence `Reality is the closed integer J-Cayley plenum` makes the second
statement read as an already adopted identity with the first. This package
proposes an explicit split:

1. The broad frame says what kind of physical description TWIST-J seeks.
2. The declared TWIST-J construction posits `J = 1 + zeta_5^2` as its
   primitive algebraic axiom.
3. Whether the complete construction physically realizes the frame is a
   separate future empirical hypothesis, not created here.
4. Whether a stronger formal theory forces a class represented by `J` is a
   separate future selection obligation, not created here.

This is compatible with `notes/AXIOM-NOT-DERIVED-2026-08-26.md`: an axiom is
not derived inside the theory that posits it, while a stronger theory may in
principle derive a representation only after its class, equivalence and
selection rule are independently frozen. Existing narrow characterisations
of five remain consequences or classifications, not evidence for A0.

## What each new claim adds

**FIELD-CONSERVATIVE-CHAIN-LAW.** The funded lift and reversal of ordered
gate composition are already INTEGER-ENERGY-FUNDED-INVOLUTION. The matter

FIELD-EISENSTEIN-RESONANCE. FIELD-SHEAR-ENERGY-BOUNDARY supplies the
generic positive-energy test. FIELD-GAUSS-CONTACT-MEMORY already requires
complete stored flux information and actual nodewise accounting.

None of those rows specifies the new C, its integral split of index 5,
the index-25 L-image, the guarded full raw-state G, the complete 32N-1
carrier, or its G;A;B;F composition with occupied neutral channels. Their
proofs therefore do not already establish this exact chain theorem. The
selected C has C^t C=[[2,-1],[-1,3]], unlike the old two-triangle matrix
whose Gram has eigenvalues 4 and 2. A different positive cell bypasses
that particular construction boundary; it does not falsify it. The
radius-two result is on the stored-block path, with zero electric-charge
current between cells. It neither strengthens FIELD-GAUSS-CONTACT-MEMORY
to arbitrary reversible currents nor weakens FIELD-CHARGED-SEPARATION-BOUND.

**FIELD-CHAIN-FIRST-WORK.** Existing retarded wave transport and reservoir
accounting already prove conservative local work and deposition in their
specified rational wave/port model. They have different state spaces,
forcing/port protocols, time updates and storage. The new result instead
proves, for every finite N>=2 and every integer H(w)=1, the exact prepared
chain's first resource arrival N-1, first paid R-to-AM reaction N and
complete original receiver return N+1, with energy 41 independent of N.
Its two-unit energy transfer is not a net creation of energy or a transported
electric charge. All cuts and the one specified equal-energy offimage
preparation are all-time controls, proved from invariant components and an
invariant image complement. They are not extrapolations of a finite census.

The return at N+1 is the return of one original 31-coordinate receiver;
the source field, matter and last channel have not all returned to their
initial states. It is neither apparatus reset nor a repeatable fresh-input
protocol. The earlier source-only native record theorem remains a different

**FIELD-LOCAL-WORK-RECORD.** Reversible record extensions and finite-bank

## 1. Current geometric setting

Put

\[
K=\mathbf Q(j)=\mathbf Q(\zeta_5),
\qquad
F=K^+=\mathbf Q(\sqrt5),
\qquad
B=\operatorname{Jac}(y^2=x^5-1),
\]

and

\[
X=B\times B.
\]

The preceding notes identify Markman's rational four-dimensional secant space

\[
\mathcal B\subset H^{\mathrm{ev}}(X,\mathbf Q)
\]

with `K` as a `Q`-vector space by

\[
v(a)=\sum_{r=0}^3\tau^r(a)p_r,
\qquad
\tau(j)=j^2,
\]

where the normalized pure spinors satisfy

\[
\tau(p_r)=p_{r+1}.
\]

W2B used

\[
\alpha=v(1),
\qquad
\beta=v(j)
\]

and proved that `alpha tensor beta` has both a non-zero `BB_1` Weil component and a non-zero `BB_0` scalar/rank component.

Markman's actual geometric mechanism is stronger than that cohomological criterion: it starts with coherent `B`-secant sheaves on `X`, applies Orlov's equivalence to their outer tensor product, and then asks for semiregularity of the resulting derived object. His general CM construction allows `q in F`; his explicit sheaf examples in Section 11.2 specialize further to the case `q in Q`.

For the present CM5 field,

\[
q=-s^2=\frac{5+\sqrt5}{2},
\qquad
s=j-j^{-1},
\]

so that explicit shortcut is not immediately available.

## 2. Perfect-complex realization is already available

At the special seed `X=B^2`, Gate E proved that every rational Hodge class is generated by divisor classes. Hence every class in the rational secant space `B`, including the W2B classes, is algebraic in the rational Chow ring.

For a smooth projective variety the rational Chern character gives the standard isomorphism

\[
K_0(X)\otimes\mathbf Q
\xrightarrow{\;ch\;}
CH^*(X)\otimes\mathbf Q.
\]

Consequently, after one common integer scaling, every rational secant class in `B` is the Chern character of a K-theory class, equivalently of a perfect complex represented by a finite complex of vector bundles.

Thus the following part of W2C closes:

\[
\boxed{
\text{rational secant class}
\Longrightarrow
\text{scaled perfect-complex realization on }X.
}
\]

This is weaker than Markman's `B`-secant-sheaf input. A virtual K-theory class or arbitrary perfect complex is not automatically a single coherent sheaf with the deformation properties required in his argument.

There is also an elementary finite construction from line bundles. If `D_1,...,D_m` are divisor classes generating the relevant rational Hodge algebra, then on a fourfold every polynomial in the `D_i` can be written as a finite rational linear combination of exponentials

\[
e^{\sum n_iD_i}=ch\!\left(\bigotimes_iL_i^{n_i}\right)
\]

because the logarithm identity truncates after degree four. After clearing denominators this gives an explicit virtual finite sum of line bundles.

So the remaining obstruction is not existence in rational K-theory.

## 3. A literal breaker for the old W2B class beta

The degree-zero term of every normalized pure spinor `p_r` is `1`. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{rank}(v(a))
=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbf Q}(a).
\]

For the W2B choice

\[
\beta=v(j)
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}(\beta)
=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbf Q}(j)
=-1.
}
\]

A coherent sheaf has non-negative integral rank. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\beta\text{ cannot be the Chern character of a coherent sheaf.}
}
\]

This is not an obstruction to the cohomological W2B result, and replacing `beta` by `-beta` preserves the non-vanishing criterion up to an overall sign. But it shows that the original pair was not the best starting point for W2C.

## 4. The anti-real cyclic class gamma

A better class is obtained from the native anti-real generator

\[
s=j-j^{-1},
\qquad
\bar s=-s.
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{
\gamma:=v(s)\in\mathcal B.
}

Since

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbf Q}(s)=0,
\]

we have

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(\gamma)=0.}
\]

The four Galois coefficients are

\[
(s,\tau(s),-s,-\tau(s)).
\]

Write

\[
t:=\tau(s)=j^2-j^{-2}.
\]

The exact cyclotomic identities are

\[
s^2=-q,
\qquad
t^2=-q',
\qquad
st=-\sqrt5,
\]

where

\[
q=\frac{5+\sqrt5}{2},
\qquad
q'=\frac{5-\sqrt5}{2},
\qquad
qq'=5.
\]

---

## 5. gamma tensor gamma still satisfies the Markman criterion

For adjacent cyclic CM types, the two ordered coefficients contributing to the corresponding singleton Weil line add to

\[
2\tau^r(s)\tau^{r+1}(s).
\]

Using `st=-sqrt5`, these four coefficients alternate between

\[
-2\sqrt5
\quad\text{and}\quad
+2\sqrt5.
\]

Hence every Weil component is non-zero:

\[
\boxed{
\Pi((\gamma\otimes\gamma)_1)_\sigma\ne0
\quad\text{for all four }\sigma.
}
\]

In particular,

\[
(\gamma\otimes\gamma)_1\notin KB_1.
\]

For opposite CM types, the coefficient sum is

\[
2s(-s)+2t(-t)
=-2(s^2+t^2)
=2(q+q')
=10.
\]

The common complementary-spinor scalar normalization is non-zero because it contains

\[
N_{F/\mathbf Q}(q)=qq'=5.
\]

Therefore the `BB_0` scalar/rank component is non-zero as well.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\gamma\otimes\gamma
\text{ satisfies the same two cohomological Markman non-vanishing conditions as W2B.}
}
\]

This is the preferred W2C pair because each input has rank zero and admits a divisor-supported sheaf ansatz.

## 6. Exact polynomial form of gamma

Let

\[
A:=\Theta_+,
\qquad
B:=\Theta_-
\]

be the two real-embedding pieces of the `F`-linear polarization. On the abelian fourfold `X` they satisfy

\[
A^3=B^3=0.
\]

Define the rational divisor classes

\[
D:=A+B,
\qquad
E:=\sqrt5\,(A-B).
\]

The four normalized pure spinors may be written

\[
\begin{aligned}
p_0&=e^{sA+tB},\\
p_1&=e^{-sA+tB},\\
p_2&=e^{-sA-tB},\\
p_3&=e^{sA-tB}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Direct exact expansion gives

\[
\boxed{
\gamma
=-5D+E
+\frac18\left(
15D^3+5D^2E-3DE^2-E^3
\right).
}
\]

There are no degree-zero, degree-four, or degree-eight terms.

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\gamma
=-2(q'A+qB)
+5\varphi^2A^2B
+5\varphi^{-2}AB^2.
}
\]

The negative class

\[
\delta:=-\gamma
\]

has first Chern class

\[
\boxed{
L:=\delta_2=5D-E=2q'A+2qB.
}
\]

Both real coefficients `2q'` and `2q` are positive, so `L` lies in the positive polarization cone. After a common integral scaling it is an ample divisor class.

## 7. A divisor-supported sheaf ansatz

Suppose first that a common integral scaling has been made so that `L` is integral and divisible by two in `Pic(X)`. Choose a smooth effective divisor

\[
i:Y\hookrightarrow X,
\qquad [Y]=L,
\]

and a line bundle `M` with

\[
2c_1(M)=L.
\]

The standard exact sequence gives

\[
ch(i_*(M|_Y))
=e^{L/2}-e^{-L/2}.
\]

Since `dim X=4`, this is exactly

\[
\boxed{
ch(i_*(M|_Y))
=L+\frac1{24}L^3.
}
\]

The target `delta=-gamma` has the same rank and the same degree-two component. Its only remaining difference is in codimension three.

Modulo the relations `A^3=B^3=0`, the exact discrepancy is

\[
\boxed{
Q
:=\frac1{24}L^3-\delta_6
=20AB(A+B).
}
\]

In the rational `D,E` coordinates this is equivalently

\[
\boxed{
Q=5D^3-DE^2.
}
\]

Thus a coherent-sheaf realization has been reduced from an arbitrary Chern-character search to a sharply stated geometry problem:

> construct an effective curve class representing a suitable positive integral multiple of `Q`, and realize the required correction by an elementary modification of the divisor-supported sheaf, with any resulting degree-eight discrepancy corrected compatibly.

The last clause matters. A curve sheaf contributes not only its leading codimension-three class but generally a codimension-four term as well. K-theoretically that term is harmless and can be corrected by point classes; realizing the whole correction by one coherent sheaf is an additional geometric condition and is not proved here.

## 8. The correction is structurally tied to the norm form

The factorization

\[
Q=20AB(A+B)
\]

shows why this term is natural. The codimension-two factor satisfies

\[
20AB=5D^2-E^2.
\]

So

\[
\boxed{
Q=(5D^2-E^2)D.
}
\]

The same norm-form factor occurs for every anti-real secant class.

Indeed let

\[
f=a+b\sqrt5\in F
\]

and consider

\[
\gamma_f:=v(sf).
\]

Its exact components are

\[
(\gamma_f)_2
=(-5a-15b)D+(a-5b)E
\]

and

\[
(\gamma_f)_6
=\frac18\Bigl(
(15a+25b)D^3
+(5a-5b)D^2E
-(3a+5b)DE^2
+(-a+b)E^3
\Bigr).
\]

For `delta_f=-gamma_f`, the analogous half-divisor ansatz produces a cubic correction divisible by

\[
\boxed{5D^2-E^2=20AB.}
\]

Hence the codimension-three correction is not an accident of the choice `f=1`. It is a structural feature of the cyclic-C4 / real-quadratic norm geometry.

## 9. Markman's q-in-Q shortcut cannot be reached by F-rescaling

Markman's explicit coherent-sheaf examples in Section 11.2 assume

\[
q\in\mathbf Q_{>0}.
\]

One might try to replace the imaginary generator `s` by `fs`, with `f in F^x`, so that the new square parameter

\[
qf^2
\]

became rational.

This is impossible for the CM5 field.

Assume

\[
qf^2=d\in\mathbf Q^\times.
\]

Taking `F/Q` norms gives

\[
5N_{F/\mathbf Q}(f)^2=d^2,
\]

because

\[
N_{F/\mathbf Q}(q)=5.
\]

Therefore

\[
\left(\frac{N(f)}d\right)^2=\frac15,
\]

which has no solution in `Q`.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
qf^2\notin\mathbf Q
\quad\text{for every }f\in F^\times.
}
\]

This is an exact square-class obstruction. The `q in Q` sheaf formulas cannot be imported into the cyclic CM5 case by a simple change of the `F`-basis.

## 10. A genus-four Jacobian platform nevertheless exists

There is a separate geometric simplification which may be useful for the next attack.

Consider

\[
C_4:\quad y^2=x^{10}-1.
\]

This is a genus-four hyperelliptic curve. Let

\[
a:(x,y)\mapsto(-x,y)
\]

and let `h` be the hyperelliptic involution.

The quotient by `a`, using `u=x^2`, is

\[
y^2=u^5-1,
\]

which is the CM5 curve defining `B`.

The quotient by `ah`, using

\[
u=x^2,
\qquad
v=xy,
\]

is

\[
v^2=u^6-u.
\]

Over `C`, setting

\[
z=u^{-1},
\qquad
w=v/u^3
\]

gives

\[
w^2=1-z^5,
\]

which becomes `B` after multiplying `w` by `i`.

The pullbacks of holomorphic differentials from these two genus-two quotients are the complementary `+1` and `-1` eigenspaces of `a` in `H^0(C_4,Omega^1)`, each of dimension two. Therefore the induced map of Jacobians is an isogeny:

\[
\boxed{
J(C_4)\sim B\times B=X
}
\]

over `C`.

So our `X` is isogenous to an explicit genus-four Jacobian, the same geometric category in which Markman constructs his concrete secant sheaves. This does not remove the cyclic-C4 square-class obstruction, but it gives a concrete curve on which to search for the required divisor/curve modification.

## 11. Semiregularity remains a separate gate

Markman's current general-CM paper defines a `B`-secant sheaf to be a coherent sheaf `G` on `X` with `ch(G) in B`. Given two such sheaves, he applies Orlov's equivalence to their outer tensor product. His algebraicity strategy then requires semiregularity of the resulting derived object.

The paper explicitly states that this semiregularity has not been established in the general construction.

Recent work of Alexander Perry extends Buchweitz-Flenner semiregularity to equivariant noncommutative settings and twisted derived categories, and explicitly notes an application to a question of Markman. This changes the available toolkit, but it does not by itself certify semiregularity of the specific cyclic-C4 object sought here.

Therefore this note does not fold semiregularity into W2C.

1. every rational CM5 secant class on the special seed has a scaled K-theory / perfect-complex realization;
2. the old class `beta=v(j)` is ruled out as the Chern character of a coherent sheaf by its rank `-1`;

4. `gamma tensor gamma` still satisfies both Markman cohomological non-vanishing conditions;

   \[
   Q=5D^3-DE^2=20AB(A+B);

8. `X=B^2` is explicitly isogenous to the genus-four Jacobian `J(y^2=x^10-1)`.

### Still open

1. prove that a suitable positive integral multiple of `Q` has an effective curve representative with the needed quotient-sheaf geometry;
2. construct one coherent `B`-secant sheaf with Chern character a non-zero multiple of `gamma` or `-gamma`;
3. construct a pair satisfying the same open cohomological criterion at sheaf level;
4. prove semiregularity after Orlov transform;
5. deduce algebraicity of the transported generic CM5 Weil class.

## 13. Verdict

W2C is **partially closed**, with a precise new boundary.

The perfect-complex problem is solved, but that alone does not supply Markman's coherent secant sheaves. The strongest current sheaf-oriented reduction is

\[
\boxed{
\gamma=v(j-j^{-1}),
\qquad
\gamma\otimes\gamma\text{ is cohomologically generic},
}
\]

and, for `delta=-gamma`,

\[
\boxed{
ch_{\rm baseline}=L+\frac1{24}L^3,
\qquad
L=5D-E,
\qquad
Q=\frac1{24}L^3-\delta_6=5D^3-DE^2.
}
\]

The next geometric target is therefore no longer `find a sheaf` in the abstract. It is:

```text
construct an effective curve in the class N(5D^3-DE^2)
for some controlled N,
then turn the divisor-supported baseline sheaf into a genuine
B-secant sheaf by an explicit elementary modification.
```

That is the correct W2C boundary after the present search.

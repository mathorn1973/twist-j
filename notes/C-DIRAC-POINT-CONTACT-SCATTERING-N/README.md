Owner: A. M. Thorn, session `dirac-point-contact-scattering-20261027`.
Public claim: [issue #1796](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1796).

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) derives the complete flux-normalized scattering matrix
of the existing four-channel point contact, its local resolvent, its pole
equation with explicit sheets, and its gap-channel phase delays. It proves
that `sin(g) != 0` excludes every spatially square-summable eigenstate of
this particular step.

The pole distinction is essential. With `c = cos(g)`, the nonzero poles
derived on the massless-outgoing, massive-gap continuation are ordinary
gap resonances for `-1 < c < 0`. For `0 < c < 1` they lie below a massive
open-band arc on a mixed sheet, and are not poles of the locally continued
all-open outgoing matrix. In particular, `g = 2 pi / 5` belongs to this
second case. Their radii do not supply a physical lifetime. The isolated
cases `c = -1, 0, 1` and the band thresholds are treated separately.

analytical preparation already exposed before it. An in-session algebraic

## What was checked

The opposition first derived a separate rigidity argument: a real normalized
gap which is multiplicative has finite image in $\{\pm1\}$; the rational
independence of the real cyclotomic periods forces at most two branch
classes. Development independently used the stabilizer of the four
nonzero coefficients in the gap polynomial. Both give $q=5$ with the
same nonzero/distinct-modulo-sign assumptions.

The coordinator read the final proofs. The following sensitive points were
checked explicitly.

- The integer extraction includes the zero residue, and simultaneous
  conjugation of numerator and denominator preserves the normalized value.
  An absolute-value denominator would change that claim.
- A nonidentity doubling involution and an arbitrary two-branch
  multiplicative reader are different classification statements.
- The endpoint zero is excluded from the latter; at $q=3$ it can produce
  the trivial character. Coincident branch labels give a zero denominator.
- The signed gap norm is
  $q((a^2-b^2)/q)$ for $A_q(b)-A_q(a)$; its absolute value is $q$.
- The shortest-length proof uses a well-defined ring map
  $\mathbb Z[\zeta_q]\to\mathbb F_q$. Its converse gives an actual
  product through cycle cancellation.
- The full relation proof satisfies all three hypotheses of the exact
  Franz lemma: symmetric integral exponents, zero total exponent, and
  equality at every nonidentity root. Primality supplies the last
  condition from one equality by Galois conjugation.
- Cycle indicator relations are primitive disjoint integer relations, so
  the quotient is free abelian with the stated rank.
- The sign-reader uniqueness concerns homogeneous rational linear
  combinations in the displayed basis. An additional independent constant
  changes the support question and is not covered.
- The additive inverse and the five/ten-state lower bounds use explicit
  separating observations. The period-five reading is not $J^n$.
- The direct Thue-Morse identity uses the shift $h$ and indices
  $0\le n<16^m$. Its existing block identity is credited; the
  digit-sum composition is a separate input map.

No substantive mathematical counterexample was found in these arguments.
This is a statement about the displayed derivations, not an exhaustive
finite audit. The strongest product-relation result depends on the named
classical independence theorem. The shortest-length and prime-five rigidity
results do not depend on that import.

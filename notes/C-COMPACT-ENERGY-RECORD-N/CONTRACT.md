## 1. Original-energy target

HOMOGENEOUS-CAPTURE-CURRENT-RECORD, the original receiver at z=100,
all 26 carrier and receiver labels, and arbitrary passive references.
Fix E0>0 and Q=E0 f(U_rec), where f(exp(-iE))=E modulo 2pi in [0,2pi).
The branch of f is not changed after an obstruction is found.

For the global occupation question fix the Canon occupation embedding
with its stated diagonal phase gauge. Allowed physical operators have
terms supported in sets of physical lattice diameter at most R, for an
arbitrary fixed finite R. Their compression to the admitted sector must
be a bounded operator; comparison error is its uniform operator norm
difference from Q. An exact extension must also preserve the code, not
merely reproduce diagonal expectations. The full physical domain and
any sums on an infinite lattice must be specified in the proof.

We seek an explicit construction or a scoped obstruction, with a
quantitative lower error bound if one can be proved. A proof about only
configuration-distance locality is not sufficient: the physical
occupation supports must be related to the target matrix elements.
An identity operator or occupation charge is not relabelled Q.

A distinct positive finite-family approximation may be supplied only
with its own exact family, reference convention, spectral-cut weight,
norm, finite-time error and locality definition. It must not be described
as a uniform approximation on the full original sector.

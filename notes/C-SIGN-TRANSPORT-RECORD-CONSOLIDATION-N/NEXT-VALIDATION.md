## Six fields to freeze in a new public probe

1. Equation: the literal total B, five ordered layers, data and clock rings,
   both input states, accounts, total tagged receiver and complete inverse.
   Freeze d,h in 1..8 for the finite audit. Prove the all-parameter theorem
   separately. Use P=2(2d+h+5), not a fitted period.
2. Code: an independently reviewed standard-library exact verifier still to
   be authored. Use polynomial coefficient arithmetic and a separately
   derived transition/shift oracle; agreement of shared gate logic alone
   is not independent confirmation. Static parsing is permitted before pin.
3. Carrier: all raw integral coordinates; declared two-input preparation for
   the readable-history theorem. The full-carrier involution/conservation
   proof must include rejection and zero/occupied cases.
4. Systematics: freeze full and intermediate observation boundaries; count
   the clock and every background site. Check both full H and positive E,
   not just a floating-point scalar. No imported mutable source probe.
5. Failure threshold: zero tolerance for any state, invariant, inverse,
   early tag, opposite sign, hold-length, expiration or recurrence mismatch.
   A disagreement or failed run is preserved. No after-the-fact reader edit.
6. Layer: L1 only. No physical time, energy, empirical occurrence or native-U
   conclusion is admitted by this audit.

## Proposed exact inventory

For each of 64 (d,h) pairs and both signs, follow at least three complete
periods P, recording every full boundary and each of the five layer boundaries.
Check the first three encoder transitions against the literal state table;
compare all subsequent pre-return rail coordinates with the shift formula;
check the exact data return table including its false-read phase; verify
clock coordinates and its local eligibility mark, the common full period,
blank initial receiver, first arrival, h-boundary hold and subsequent blank.

Apply the literal inverse layers to every saved complete state, not an
inverse inferred from the known trajectory. Separately test rejection and
noncode inputs against the total-map proof with a finite declared raw alphabet.
Fix that alphabet and all counts in the new verifier before pinning it.

Preserve these negative controls:

- Remove the clock from the reader: the later + input yields a false minus
  reading, as analytically shown in the construction.
- Remove the common initial launch high: the stated encoder table fails.
- Attach the original writer output directly to a fixed conveyor pair:
  the parity/noncode obstruction remains.
- Compare both source signs with every other initial coordinate identical;
  preselecting the outgoing high position from a is forbidden.
- Shift the receiver anchor mask or its permitted gap: the declared arrival
  or sign equation fails. Do not silently tune the decoder after this test.

## Execution and acceptance

Reserve one fresh probe, read back current authority and overlap, commit and
publish its actual scientific source set and hashes, and read back the exact
pin before the first run under the governing repository protocol. Append the
first output and run record without rewriting the frozen sources. Require the
actual architecture receipts and the unchanged expected bytes. An independent
mathematical reviewer must examine the encoder and recurrence, especially the
clock's local nature. Same-session authoring reviews are not that acceptance.

This scope closes only a declared finite occupied-region delay memory. A
stationary single-cell latch, literal-zero receiver, externally supplied
successive unknown messages, native-U realization, and physical detector
admission each require their own contract. No negative result for those
larger classes is inferred here.

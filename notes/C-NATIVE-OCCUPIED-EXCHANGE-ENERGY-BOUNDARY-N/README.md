**The original native continuation cannot receive a fresh source content
after synchronization. A complete reversible ready-content exchange
exists as an added rule, but it preserves an unselected family of relative
accounts. Selecting the square requires a different, independently admitted
contact or priced resource.**

sharp bounds, exact reference hypotheses and a full conditional contact

## The prepared receiver exchange

Write
\[
 A=a+c,\ B=b+d,\ C=a-c-2,\ D=b-d-1,\qquad k=Az.
\]
On
\[
 \mathcal H=\{A=2B,\ B\in\{2,3\},\ z\in\{1,4\}\}
\]
the two contents are
\[
 \mu=1+2kC,\qquad \lambda=3-BzD.
\]
The receiver reads \(\lambda\) from its original \((b,d,q,r)\) port.
The source reads \(\mu\) from \((a,c,q,r)\); shared \(q,r\) cannot be
omitted from this source-reader assertion.

At fixed \(A,B,q,r\),
\[
 \boxed{W:\ C'=D+k,\quad D'=C-k}
\]
swaps \((\mu,\lambda)\), preserves readiness and retains the old receiver
content in the source. It is an involution on all 500 prepared states,
and its lift commutes with the selected original \(U\).
Original \(U\) still fixes each content separately.
Its inverse remains restricted to a known preceding controller slice;
the union of both trace sheets is not a globally invertible native step.

Repeating \(W\) alternates the two contents already present. Further
independent arrivals require admitted loading or transport. A finite
port rotation supplies a complete conditional example, with explicit
additional ports. Retaining \(L\) independent old five-valued contents
requires at least \(5^L\) distinguishable environment states at fixed
current receiver; this is a capacity bound, not a thermal cost.

## The energy witness and the role of the reference

After normalizing \(f(1)>0\) to one, the original common-even coordinate
family is \(E_\kappa=N_1+\kappa N_2\), \(\kappa\ge0\).
Positive contrast requires \(\kappa>0\). The square of each balanced
integer coordinate corresponds to \(\kappa=4\); fourth powers correspond
to \(\kappa=16\).

The prepared two-block endpoints
\[
 ((2,0,-2,0),0)
 \longleftrightarrow
 ((1,1,-1,-1),(1,1,-1,-1))
\]
have accounts \(2\kappa\) and 8. An independently permitted transition
with no changing other account would select \(\kappa=4\).
There is no such isolated distinction in one zero-sum four-coordinate
block. Both endpoints preserve the source and receiver sums; their
admissibility as states does not provide an actual connecting transition.

Two stronger obstructions specify what a nonlinear law would have to change:

1. A single map on all content vectors preserving every pairwise square
   account also preserves every \(E_\kappa\). The proof uses the graph
   of minimal positive square distance, a Cartesian product of five-cycles.
2. A signal map that does not read the unchanged shared reference and
   conserves the square at the five settings
   \(\rho,\rho\pm e_1,\rho\pm e_2\) also preserves every \(E_\kappa\)
   at the central reference. Those five accounts reconstruct both counts.

The second result does not assume a linear or invertible contact.
It does assume the **same signal map** for all five preparations.
Neither theorem forbids a joint law that reads the reference.

To make that boundary concrete, write the two signal content pairs as
\(c\in\mathbb F_5^4\) and the actual reference contents as
\(\rho\in\mathbb F_5^2\). Let \(\mathsf D\rho=(\rho_1,\rho_2,\rho_1,\rho_2)\);
let \(\mathsf C\) transpose \((2,0,0,0)\) and \((1,1,1,1)\), fixing
every other point. Then
\[
 \boxed{T_\rho(c)=\mathsf D\rho+\mathsf C(c-\mathsf D\rho)}
\]
leaves the entire reference and all controls fixed and reconstructs both
signal checkpoints exactly. It is an involution on the whole common
prepared carrier, conserves \(E_4\) for every actual reference and commutes
with the common original continuation.

That carrier has 312500 states at a fixed counter, including both trace
sheets. It retains three actual cells, not just two abstract differences.
Applying \(W\) to all three cells supplies the ready exchange on this same
carrier and preserves every relative \(E_\kappa\).

The transposition was deliberately selected from a square-account shell.
It therefore establishes conditional mathematical existence and refutes
an overbroad reference no-go; it does not independently derive physical
energy, original-native admission or a rule for when the contact occurs.

## What a decisive new contact must supply

For an independently priced rest-of-apparatus account \(B\), every
admitted transition must obey
\[
 \Delta N_1+\kappa\Delta N_2+\Delta B=0.
\]
A transition with \(\Delta N_2\ne0\) determines a candidate weight;
every other admitted transition must agree. Inconsistent equations
reject the declared family. Defining the bank's compensation after
choosing \(\kappa\) would not select anything.

The same physical apparatus must account for source loading, occupied
receiver updates, old information, the actual reference, common motion,
counter and any actuation state. A relative difference alone does not
price those shared resources.

The [program synthesis in section 10](PROOF.md#10-synthesis-with-the-post-v103-contact-program)
preserves the distinct scopes of #1596, #1605, #1612, #1613, #1615,
#1617 and #1619. No equality is inferred between the integer payment 10,
the particle-channel gap \(2\sqrt{1+m^2}-2m\), and this relative account.
The exact finite \(m/\arctan m>1\) result for \(m>0\) remains; physical
existence-cost or entropy interpretations remain hypotheses.

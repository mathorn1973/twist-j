## Falsifiers and residual obligations

An incorrect Gibbs distribution, missing degeneracy, omitted cold cursor,
or incorrect ladder ground weight invalidates the quoted q bound. Replacing
a joint reference statement by matching marginals is not admissible. A
correlated or differently prepared resource requires a new joint argument.

A raw negative block, nonconserving gate, incorrect node potential, escaped
active path, second forward launch on a good history, or unlocked loader
invalidates the Hamiltonian interface. Site collisions, excess support,
range or degree, omitted rows, or a hidden qubit implementation of a
multilevel site invalidate the corresponding locality/resource claim.

Wrong preparation indices, failure of loader orthogonality, use of the old
short output tail, omission of the old completion latch, or omission of
the acceptance denominator invalidates the output estimates. Actual
full-rank readiness cannot inherit an exact logical SWAP merely because
its error is small. No old post-read storage interval is copied.

The complete mean and variance must include reverse reaction population,
all cold defects and all old rows. A small state error is not an
energy-independent bound when the capacity is large. Conditional input
energy transfer cannot be reported as the unconditional full-input
transfer without its retained tail.

This result would be overstated by claiming that a single equilibrium bath
starts the device, that the cold states or thermal contrast have been
manufactured by their own subsequent dynamics, that high aggregate hot
occupation is cost-free, or that this reaction derives from unchanged
U_rec. The new thermal factors and their product relative to the fixed
interaction remain an initial nonstationary boundary condition.

Material realization and precision, physical preparation of the temperature
contrast and low-temperature hardware, bounded small local dimensions,
physical readout, occurrence, replenishment and useful reset remain open.
The present construction is finite and reversible, with finite windows.
No merge, Canon promotion or empirical realization is performed.

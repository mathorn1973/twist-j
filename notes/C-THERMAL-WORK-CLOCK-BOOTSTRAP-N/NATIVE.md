## 2. What replaces the charged work and polarized clock assumptions

No work site is supplied in a state concentrated at the central level, and no clock site
is initially inverted or occupied with near-unit weight. Instead:

- The distinguished physical work ladder starts in its low-temperature
  Gibbs state, predominantly in its ground level.
- All original master-clock sites, added tail sites and loading-cursor
  sites start in low-temperature Gibbs states, predominantly empty.
- A hot site has one ground state and D degenerate excited states.
  Its positive-temperature Gibbs state has total excited weight D/(D+2).
- A cold entropy-receiving site has one ground state and D degenerate
  excited states. Its ground weight is made large by an explicit finite
  temperature choice.

For each label a, one local resonant transition maps

```text
hot=a, sink=0, work=0, master-entry=0, loader-entry=0
    <->
hot=0, sink=a, work=R, master-entry=1, loader-entry=1.
```

The hot gap is (R+3) epsilon. The sink receives epsilon, the work site
receives R epsilon, and the two clock excitations receive 2 epsilon.
The hot label is transferred into the sink; it is not merged with the
other labels. The interaction is specified on all raw states and retains
all failures.

The large active weight comes with a large physical degeneracy:
D=2^84. Both sites have dimension D+1. A large excited manifold can be
highly populated at positive temperature even though every individual
excited level is less populated than the ground level. This is a genuine
Gibbs distribution, not a negatively tempered qubit concealed by notation.

The original payload donor bank remains at beta_0 epsilon=ln 2.
The new hot site and cold resources have separately specified positive
temperatures. Thus there are three declared temperatures. This is not
preparation from a common equilibrium bath.

## 3. The initial condition has changed, not disappeared

The construction replaces supplied nonthermal work/clock populations by
a specified product of finite thermal resources and the admitted logical
input. It does not manufacture those temperatures, spectra, degeneracies
or initial lack of correlations.

In particular, the nearly empty cold work/clock registers are supplied
as Gibbs states. They are not claimed to have been cooled from the
moderate-temperature donor bank by this same autonomous start. The
cold-state preparation burden is represented by an explicit finite cold
temperature and the full cold hardware inventory.

The input has no coherence between different values of the additive
bare energy H_0. Every nonlogical factor is a Gibbs state of its own
onsite Hamiltonian, and the logical code has fixed bare charge. Nevertheless
the temperature contrast makes forward and reverse resonant configurations
have unequal weights. The resulting nonzero commutator with the full
coupled Hamiltonian is computed in ENVIRONMENT.md.

The fixed interaction and the specified product state coexist at the
initial time. No uncounted switching pulse is part of the model. Preparing
that product/contact condition in a material system remains an obligation.
Calling the initial factors thermal does not make their product a Gibbs
state of the complete interacting Hamiltonian.

For a fixed closed H,

\[
 [H,\rho_0]=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 e^{-itH/\hbar}\rho_0e^{itH/\hbar}=\rho_0.
\]

A stationary full input cannot become the proposed running apparatus.
If all resource temperatures were equal and the admitted full state were
proportional to exp(-beta H_0), then [H,H_0]=0 would also make it stationary.
The actual temperature contrast avoids this hypothesis and is a consumed
resource, not an exception to the statement.

## 4. Full rank, entropy and one-use scope

All finite thermal resource factors are full rank. No exact purity theorem
is claimed for the actual work or clock marginal. The stated preparation
is approximate, with an explicit joint trace-distance error. A pure
logical input, when supplied, is neither erased nor spent to produce
reset purity.

Unitarity retains every initial label, defect and reference correlation.
The entropy-bearing hot label moves to the cold sink. The hot site loses
energy while the work, sink and clocks gain it; the complete physical
Hamiltonian and its expectation are conserved. The energy of that input
is not the minimum work needed to manufacture all its thermal resources.

The hot/cold resources are finite, are depleted or correlated, and are
not maintained by an omitted infinite bath. No Markovian reset channel,
steady-state engine, reusable clock, free replenishment or indefinitely
stable record is supplied. The finite preparation and output windows
eventually close under the same reversible Hamiltonian.

## 6. What the original law supplies

Public Canon v105's homogeneous U_rec supplies the native transfer and
receiver model at its stated scope. Its charge and selected spectral
energy are not automatically the positive onsite energies assigned to
these added devices.

Under the literal spectator extension U_rec tensor I_aux, the auxiliary
marginal is unchanged, even with initial correlations. That extension
cannot charge a new work site, populate a new clock, or transfer a hot
label to a cold sink. A coupling doing so must be added and proved.

The present local Hilbert spaces, weighted gaps, degeneracies, thermal
inputs, resonant many-site interaction, guarded controls and continuous
Hamiltonian are such additions. No native encoding/intertwiner or
derivation of these couplings from J alone is provided. This is a precise
boundary of the unchanged-law comparison, not a theorem excluding every
possible future native implementation.

The mathematical read projector and conditional channel remain distinct
from a physical detector and from a law of realized events. Material
couplings, their precision and noise tolerance, acquisition of the thermal
initial state, physical readout and useful reset remain open.

## The missing source premise

A proposed positive bridge must specify an independently admitted complete
source carrier, its preparation and control resources, full source dynamics,
state equality, time convention and an accessible output interface. It must
then provide a typed dictionary to the declared cell/contact carrier and
establish the dynamics relation on its stated domain, including occupied
inputs and refusals. For a pointwise deterministic bridge a suitable form is

```text
Phi U_source = T_controlled Phi,
T_controlled(X,k) = (T_k X,k).
```

This equation requires explicit coverage and equality semantics; coverage
of the entire unbounded target carrier must not be inferred from a selected
preparation or finite sector. Other proposed interface types require their
own equally explicit contract.

The source law must independently determine the actual contact and all
field, stock, helper and interaction changes. Only then can its fixed
transition relation `Q` be tested against

```text
a_q + (c-1)b_q = 0 for every q in Q,
a_q = Delta_q H1,  b_q = Delta_q Pi.
```

## Two restricted obstructions

**The old gate alphabet cannot implement these charge-changing contacts on
the same identified carrier.** Every original field-chain primitive
preserves every actual node charge. Consequently every finite composition
does so, also with data-dependent gate choices and finite stopping times:
each realized branch is a composition of maps preserving the same invariant.
The new witnesses change `(5,-5,0)` to `(0,-5,5)` or `(5,0,-5)`. They cannot
be words of that alphabet. Additional controls are covered only if they also
preserve those charges on the complete carrier. A newly admitted coupling,
a changed dictionary or discarded helper changes the class. See the
normative [four-layer proof](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/blob/7d7f588c42422c2cf37b04d76ebc13ca55eb96dc/canon/CANON.md#shared-proof-5-four-layers-occupied-contacts-cuts-and-full-projection)
and SOURCE_SELECTION section 5.

**The original reachable native carrier cannot cover an unbounded
conserved-energy target by an onto instantaneous intertwining map.** Take
exactly the original reachable domain `D` from all time-zero preparations,
the original `U`, a target bijection `T`, and an invariant target energy `E`
with infinitely many values. If `Phi:D->target` were onto and
`Phi U=T Phi`, then `E Phi` would be a conserved real-valued point reader.
The canonical amplitude classification bounds such a reader to at most
3125 values, contradicting onto coverage of `E`. The cell target has
infinitely many energy values through its unbounded stock. This is the
restricted corollary in READOUT R7. It excludes neither a selected finite
energy sector, different admitted preparations, a history or ensemble
interface, nor an extended native carrier.

Neither obstruction proves that every broader source bridge is impossible.
The present disposition is **SOURCE BRIDGE NOT SUPPLIED**, not universal
negative closure. The new contact completion provides a more precise target
and falsifiers for future source work while preserving that boundary.

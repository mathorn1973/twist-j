# A finite prefix, energy and archive bridge

**NON-CANONICAL, L1. Conditional analytical candidate-T; independent
acceptance pending.**
Author: A. M. Thorn. License: Apache-2.0.

This construction makes one finite generator reproduce every uninterrupted
step of the complete spatial unitary up to a horizon fixed in advance.
It compares that generator with the original full-step spectral energy,
then supplies a separately funded decoder for a physical occupied archive.
The archive channel has an all-qubit, two-temperature autonomous realization.

The result is an exact finite simulation and a conditional physical output
theorem. It is not a derivation of the spatial model from the original
six-coordinate TWIST update, nor preparation of its original coherent
spatial state by Gibbs resources. The latter distinction is necessary:
the full-step energy also acts on the original receiver memory.

7. [INTERFACES.md](INTERFACES.md) reconciles the spectral and arithmetic
   results of #1771 with this different finite code and apparatus. The

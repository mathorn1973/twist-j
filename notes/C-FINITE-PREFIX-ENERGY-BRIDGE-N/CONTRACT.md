## Input model

Let U be the complete one-object-pair, one-token/pulse, occupied-receiver
unitary U_rec in Public Canon v105. It acts on the complete stated
Hilbert space H, including its 26-dimensional receiver M. Set

    Q=E0 f(U),       E0>0,
    f:unit circle -> [0,2pi),       exp(-i f(z))=z,
    f(1)=0,         Delta=2pi E0.

Thus U=exp(-iQ/E0), 0<=Q<=Delta I, and Q has no spectral mass at Delta.
This is a selected mathematical energy reading. No uniqueness, physical
selection or geometric locality of Q is assumed.

Choose before evolution an isometry A0:I -> H with finite a=dim I, a
horizon N>=1, and a resolution 0<d<Delta. A0 includes the fixed spatial
preparation and the entire admitted finite information/old-memory input.
For the fixed spatial source and arbitrary carrier label/old memory,
a=26^2 is allowed. The general proof also permits a larger finite family.
The state on I and an arbitrary passive reference R may be any density.
Compact coordinate support is not required for the theorem.

The output contract is the original M channel at one selected n in
{0,...,N}, transferred into a separate occupied 26-state archive A.
The selected time C may be coherent and correlated with I,A,R. If it is
retained, the output includes C as well as A and R. No nondisturbing copy
of every intermediate quantum state or arbitrary intervening instruments
is claimed.

- One finite space and one generator reproduce all declared prefixes on
  A0 I; no separately fitted endpoint law at each n.
- Compare the finite observable with the ORIGINAL Q. Do not substitute a
  periodic-box logarithm or infer a finite invariant spatial subspace.
- Charge projectors need not act trivially on M. Supply a complete finite
  decoder rather than assume M is a tensor factor of the compressed code.
- Count the input, selector, ready helpers, arbitrary occupied archive,
  source seed, sharp battery, workspace, Gibbs donors, row copies, cursor,
  fuel, sink and completion latch. Invalid binary strings are retained.
- The autonomous Hamiltonian is time independent, positive on its entire
  raw qubit space and uses bounded-support interactions. Hold its coupling
  scale fixed as the apparatus grows and derive the duration.
- Bound the complete output/reference error before normalizing, retain the
  common launch probability, and distinguish completion from capture.
- Preserve exact preparation obstructions and all unclosed physical
  interfaces, including native derivation and resource regeneration.

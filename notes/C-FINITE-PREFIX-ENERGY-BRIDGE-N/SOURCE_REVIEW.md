## The seventeen reasoned verdicts

### 1. Order of the complete step: PASS

The Canon uses U=U0 C, with the right factor first. Its token and pulse
sectors give exactly exp(-i beta) Rcal Wcal C. Rcal and Wcal commute
because they act on different factors; C is not moved through them.
Within W the order remains Gamma S M2. The macrostep still consists of
two synchronized microticks. No asynchronous or arbitrary-occupation
extension is inferred. Sources: C2.

### 2. Cancellation of contact square roots: PASS

Writing alpha=b_T/sqrt(2), gamma=a_P/sqrt(2), a=e1-e4 and
b=i(e2-e3), every outer product in

```text
D=5(C-I)=-2(P_alpha+P_gamma)
          +4(|gamma><alpha|-|alpha><gamma|)
```

has a factor 1/2 canceled by its coefficient. Adjoint conjugation changes
i to -i correctly. D has entries in Z[i], hence in O=Z[zeta_20], without
adjoining sqrt(2). Different contact pairs are orthogonal and their sum
is locally finite. Sources: C2.

### 3. Coin eigenvectors: PASS

For M=I+2iX, expand M tensor M as
I-4 X tensor X+2i(X tensor I+I tensor X). On both a and b, the mixed
sum vanishes and X tensor X acts as -I. Therefore M2 a=a and M2 b=b,
with no extra sign or phase. The factor i in b matches the canonical
alpha. Sources: C2 and N1768.

### 4. Integrality of Wcal D: PASS

Every output vector of a contact outer product has object part a or b.
W therefore acts on that part as Gamma S, adding only a coordinate
permutation and a unit of O. The original halves were already canceled
in D. This covers all four arrangement blocks, all labels and all contact
sites. Consequently

```text
25U=zeta^(-2)[25 Rcal Wcal+5 Rcal Wcal D]
```

is integral: use (5Rcal)(5Wcal) for the first term and (5Rcal)(Wcal D)
for the second. The statement concerns the composed macrostep, not every
individual intermediate physical factor.

### 5. Fixed receiver permutation: PASS

Tcal swaps the two 26-valued labels on y>=100 for tokens and x>=100
for pulses. It preserves position, coin, pulse bank and carrier type.
It is an involutive permutation of the fixed coordinate basis, so
conjugation by Tcal preserves integrality and denominators. The assertion
uses the specified coded C^26 memory, not arbitrary uncoded occupation.
Sources: C2.

### 6. Explicit sharp coefficient: PASS

Use a pulse input (R,r,x)=(0,10,0), object coin e1 and pulse bank 1,
with any fixed carrier and memory labels. The output (-1,10,0) with
the same internal labels has object coefficient 1/5 and the unique
same-position pulse coefficient 1/5 from V11=(1-4 exp(ik))/5.
C, Gamma and both receiver permutations are inactive. No second path
contributes. The resulting entry is exactly zeta^(-2)/25.

### 7. Minimal positive integer multiplier: PASS

The sharp entry forces d/25 into O whenever a positive integer d clears
all entries. Since O intersect Q=Z, 25 divides d. Verdict 4 supplies
sufficiency. The minimum is thus 25 for positive integer scalars in the
declared ring and fixed basis. The reviewed package does not claim a
minimum over algebraic multipliers, changed bases or separately rescaled
ports.

### 8. Finite support and adjoint: PASS

All factors and adjoints have finite row and column support: finite coins,
bounded streaming, diagonal Gamma, finite contact ports and on-site label
permutations. Identifying y and x at a mixed contact does not spread a
coefficient over all positions. Thus A_rec and its adjoint preserve finite
support, and A_rec* A_rec=A_rec A_rec*=625I is well defined.
U_rec=A_rec/25 is an automorphism on direct_sum O[1/5], with inverse
A_rec*/25. The inverse of raw A_rec is A_rec*/625 on that larger module,
not generally on the raw O-module. Configuration locality does not supply
a material register at every coefficient.

### 9. Arithmetic preparations and Hilbert extension: PASS

For nonzero finite-support a0, a_n=A_rec^n a0 and
psi_n=s 25^(-n) iota(a_n), with s=1/||iota(a0)||, give the exact normalized
trajectory. One s and one scale multiply all coherent sectors and labels.
The operator identity extends to complex Hilbert inputs, mixed states and
passive references. That does not give every such input a finite exact
O-description. The sharp spectral incoming packet need not be raw; its
capture weight remains restricted to its prescribed canonical preparation.
Sources: N1739 and C2-C3.

### 10. Completeness of free phase intervals: PASS

The one-copy eigenangle fills [atan(2),pi-atan(2)], so opposite branches
of the pair fill [-b_p,b_p], b_p=pi-2atan(2). The map
(k1,k2)->(P,q)=(k1+k2,k1-k2) is onto the momentum torus; its double
ambiguity changes both factors' signs and leaves their product unchanged.
The pulse angle fills [0,a_p], a_p=2atan(2), with both signs present.
Since a_p+b_p=pi, the full free product covers beta+[-pi,pi]. This is
about the complete operator, not every individual fixed-K fiber. Sources:
C1-C2.

### 11. Escaping Weyl sequences: PASS

For each point on the circle choose the corresponding free symbol
eigenvectors and restrict the plane wave, for N>=2, to

```text
-N<=R<=N,
N^2<=r<=N^2+2N,
-N^2-2N<=x<=-N^2.
```

After normalization, C, Gamma and Tcal are inactive on the whole required
one-step neighborhood. The residual is supported on a fixed-thickness
boundary, with squared norm O(N^(-1)) and norm O(N^(-1/2)). The support
escapes and the sequence is weakly zero. This covers endpoint phases,
needs no global compact-perturbation claim, and uses Hilbert test vectors
rather than asserting finite arithmetic descriptions for each vector.

### 12. Full spectrum and generator boundary: PASS

Every circle point is an approximate eigenvalue; unitarity excludes
other spectral points. Hence sigma(U_rec) is the full unit circle.
There is no single scalar holomorphic logarithm on a neighborhood of
that circle: its derivative would have a nonzero contour integral.
The original gap-based locality proof for H_W therefore cannot simply
be transferred. This does not exclude every self-adjoint generator or
every notion of locality; a generator may distinguish phase-degenerate
branches without being one scalar analytic function of U_rec.

### 13. Borel intertwining and calibration: PASS with explicit typing

For the stated Borel branch f, Hstar=f(U_rec) is bounded, nonnegative,
has exponential U_rec and conserves its entire spectral distribution.
The free comparison must be the fully typed U0prime=exp(-i H0prime).
Its actual canonical wave operators include the right calibration factor:
Omega_rec,+/-=Tcal(Omega_+/- tensor identities)T_+/-*.
With these definitions, Borel calculus gives the safe-window identity
below. The review found suppressed notation, not a changed energy or
dynamics, because the package explicitly invokes the canonical operators.
Sources: C3-C4.

### 14. Energy difference and spatial scope: PASS

On the common space, E_diff=E0(Hstar-H0prime). Positivity and the canonical
spectral enclosure give the two bounds below without assuming that these
operators commute. For each fixed safe-channel psi, the norm of E_diff
on its interacting asymptotic trajectory tends to zero in the appropriate
time direction. This is neither an operator-norm limit nor a waiting-time
or spatial-locality bound. Outside the safe window, branch changes by
2pi may contribute. The unchanged-contact obstruction in N1768 remains.

### 15. Token-output rows modulo five: PASS

Rcal is identity on token output. Its free contribution is 25W=5A_W;
the contact contribution is 5(Wcal D)_T. Both lie in 5O, also for
pulse-to-token input. The unit phase and Tcal preserve this divisibility.
Thus rho_2((A_rec a)_T)=0 for every raw a, unlike the old A_W's nonzero
free residue propagation. This defeats that specific inherited projection,
not all possible new projections. A unital O[1/5]->F5 homomorphism is
impossible because 5(1/5)=1 would map to 0=1. Sources: N1739.

### 16. Raw height and conditional reservoir: PASS

H_raw(a)=Tr_(Q(zeta_20)/Q) sum_i conjugate(a_i)a_i is a positive integer
for nonzero finite-support a. Cyclotomic embeddings commute with
conjugation, so each term is an absolute square. The Gram identity gives
H_raw(A_rec^n a0)=625^n H_raw(a0). Only the additional choice that this
height is stored energy, with H_raw+b conserved and b>=0, implies
b0>=(625^N-1)H_raw(a0). This is not a universal work bound. Raw
nonsurjectivity also follows: a basis vector has height 8, whereas every
nonzero image has height divisible by 625.

### 17. Open physical and native obligations: PASS

The package retains the distinction between an integer representation of
a supplied unitary model and derivation from the original six-pentit law.
Its scope remains one pair, one carrier, one receiver, coded occupied
memory and the synchronized schedule. Active native projection, input
preparation, physical selection of energy and E0, spatial behavior of
Hstar, clocks, precision, further attempts and regeneration remain open.
Static lambda labels do not become active memory by adding a Hilbert
factor. A Borel logarithm selects no empirical energy, and a coherent
record branch selects no individual outcome. Sources: C4 and N1739.

## The recommended complete tensor formulation

The following reproduces the review's substantive refinement in English.
Use A_pass for the passive auxiliary/reference factor, distinct from the
successor's archive notation:

```text
S      = ell2(Z_R x Z_r) tensor C^4,
K_T    = ell2(Z_y) tensor C^26,
P      = ell2(Z_x) tensor C^2,
K_P    = P tensor C^26,
K_0    = (S tensor K_T) oplus (S tensor K_P),
D      = C^26,
A_pass = H_aux tensor H_ref,
K      = K_0 tensor D tensor A_pass.

H_E = beta I_P + i Log_pi(V),
H0  = [(H_W+beta I_S) tensor I_K_T]
       oplus
      [H_W tensor I_K_P + I_S tensor (H_E tensor I_C^26)],
U0  = exp(-i H0).
```

The fixed one-dimensional receiver-position factor at z=100 may be
retained throughout; suppressing it is a harmless identification.
I_tok means identity on both token position and label, namely all K_T.
Restoring it adds no token energy and changes no physical premise.

P_ch,0 is the direct integral of the fiber absolutely continuous
projectors, not an assumed global ac projector. Set

```text
J_safe = (5pi/4,7pi/4),
P_safe = 1_J_safe(H0) P_ch,0,
H0prime = H0 tensor I_D tensor I_A_pass,
P_ch,0prime = P_ch,0 tensor I_D tensor I_A_pass,
P_safe_prime = P_safe tensor I_D tensor I_A_pass,
U0prime = U0 tensor I_D tensor I_A_pass = exp(-i H0prime).
```

H0 is bounded and decomposable in the same total momentum. Within each
fiber it commutes with U0 and its spectral projections, hence reduces the
fiber ac space. Thus P_ch,0 commutes with H0, P_safe is an orthogonal
projector, and H0prime preserves its range.

On pulses the asymptotic receiver calibrations are

```text
T_+ = Pi_(v<0) tensor I + Pi_(v>0) tensor J,
T_- = Pi_(v>0) tensor I + Pi_(v<0) tensor J,
```

where J swaps pulse and memory labels and the velocity projectors include
both bands. On tokens both calibrations are
T1=1_(y<100)I+1_(y>=100)J_T,mem. Identities act on all other factors.
Zero-velocity exceptional points have zero measure and leave no missing
L2 subspace. The explicit form proves [T_+/- ,H0prime]=0: token energy
is independent of its position and label; pulse velocity projections
commute with H_E, and the label exchange is energetically degenerate.
The calibrations also preserve the canonical channel and safe projector.
Commutation with U0prime alone would not establish this energy commutation
in the presence of phase aliases.

The wave operator compared with the unchanged U0prime is

```text
Omega_rec,+/-
 = s-lim_(n->+/-infinity) U_rec^(-n) U0prime^n P_ch,0prime
 = Tcal (Omega_+/- tensor I_D tensor I_A_pass) T_+/-*.

Hstar Omega_rec,+/- P_safe_prime
 = Omega_rec,+/- H0prime P_safe_prime,
Hstar=f(U_rec).
```

The right T_+/-* cannot be omitted or justified away by calling the
physical memory passive. The receiver interacts with memory; only the
added external factors and the free memory energy degeneracy are passive.
The wave intertwining extends to bounded Borel functions. On J_safe,
f(exp(-iE))=E because that interval is inside (0,2pi). The absence of
other 2pi translates in the full enclosure
[pi/2-a_p,5pi/2+a_p], a_p<3pi/4, additionally makes this window unaliased
as a scattering phase channel. These are distinct reasons.

On the common space,

```text
E_diff=E0(Hstar-H0prime),
-E0(5pi/2+a_p)I <= E_diff <= E0(3pi/2+a_p)I.
```

For a fixed sign and psi in Ran(P_safe_prime), let
r_n(eta)=U_rec^n Omega_rec,+/- eta-U0prime^n eta. The calibrated wave
limit makes r_n tend to zero for both eta=psi and eta=H0prime psi.
Intertwining and cancellation of the free terms give

```text
(Hstar-H0prime) U_rec^n Omega_rec,+/- psi
 = r_n(H0prime psi)-H0prime r_n(psi),

||E_diff U_rec^n Omega_rec,+/- psi||
 <= E0[||r_n(H0prime psi)||+||H0prime|| ||r_n(psi)||] -> 0.
```

The limit uses n->+infinity for the plus wave and n->-infinity for the
minus wave. Bounded H0prime avoids a hidden domain condition. This is a
fixed-state statement, without a uniform rate, spatial estimate or thermal
construction. It does not assert Hstar=H0prime on the interacting image.

The review classifies this refinement as explicit typing and calibration
of the existing canonical convention, not a substantive repair. Defining
a different wave operator without the right calibration would instead
change the comparison and invalidate this particular asymptotic argument.

## Results of all six falsifiers

1. **An entry of 25U_rec outside O: not confirmed.** The D factorization,
   canceled contact halves, fixed coin vectors and integral 5Rcal cover
   all blocks and sites. Tcal is a permutation. The proof is universal,
   not a sample of entries.
2. **Failure of a nonzero entry with exact denominator 25: not confirmed.**
   Verdicts 6 and 7 exhibit zeta^(-2)/25 and exclude cancellation by any
   positive integer multiplier not divisible by 25.
3. **Violation of the common coherent scale: not confirmed.** One s and
   one factor 25^(-n) multiply the complete state; A_rec* A_rec=625I
   verifies its norm. Local rational notation is not separate physical
   normalization of ports.
4. **A free circle point without the claimed escaping Weyl sequence:
   not confirmed.** Verdicts 10 and 11 cover every point, with explicit
   boxes, a valid one-step neighborhood and a vanishing residual on the
   complete space.
5. **Failure of wave intertwining or the branch on J_safe: not confirmed.**
   Full tensor typing, the canonical right calibration and Borel calculus
   prove the identity. Suppressed identities in the ZIP require no change
   to its declared canonical convention.
6. **A token-output coefficient contradicting the zero residue claim:
   not confirmed.** Every token-output row lies in 5O, including conversion
   from a pulse and receiver permutation. A nonzero complex token
   amplitude can remain while its rho_2 image is zero.

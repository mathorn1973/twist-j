## 1. Four different operations

Let O = Z[j], K = Q(j), j = zeta_5 and J = 1+j^2 = j/phi.
The following operations have distinct carriers:

| Operation | Carrier and property | What must not be identified |
|---|---|---|
| Multiplication by J | O and its quotients; J is a unit of norm one | It is not a degree-p-power geometric Frobenius |
| sigma_p(j)=j^p | K for p != 5; reduces to residue Frobenius | Its finite Galois order is not an endomorphism degree |
| pi, the p-power Frobenius | The reduction of B=Jac(y^2=x^5-1), p odd and p != 5; degree p^2 | Its semicommutation with CM is not equality with [J] |
| [p] and the formal logarithm | The completed group at the origin | Its coordinate denominators and first nonzero reduced degree matter |

The registered CM5-POLARIZATION-DICTIONARY,
CM5-HECKE-NORMALIZATION and CM5-INTEGRAL-PULLBACK-REACHABILITY are inputs at
their exact T scopes in Public Canon v117. They do not identify this fixed
Jacobian with the varying CM data of the external RH construction.

## 2. Frobenius iteration does not increase the defect valuation

For a rational prime p != 5 put sigma = sigma_p and, for r >= 1, define

\[
\delta_r=J^{p^r}-\sigma^r(J)=J^{p^r}-1-j^{2p^r}.
\]

The algebra O/pO is a product of 4/f copies of F_(p^f), where
f=ord_5(p). Reduction of sigma on each component is p-th powering.
Consequently delta_r belongs to pO. For every prime ideal P above p, normalize
v_P(p)=1. The sharper statement is

\[
v_P(\delta_r)=v_P(\delta_1)\qquad(r\geq1). \tag{L1}
\]

Proof. Put u=J^p/sigma(J). It is in 1+pO_P. Telescoping gives

\[
\frac{J^{p^r}}{\sigma^rJ}
 =\prod_{k=0}^{r-1}\sigma^k(u)^{p^{r-1-k}}.
\]

The decomposition group at every P above p is generated by sigma, since K/Q
is abelian and unramified at p. It preserves v_P. If v is in 1+pO_P, the
binomial theorem gives
v_P(v^p-1) >= v_P(v-1)+1. This inequality also holds at p=2; no equality
case of the lifting-the-exponent lemma is needed. Set t=v_P(u-1). All factors
except the final one are 1 modulo p^(t+1); the final one has difference from
1 of valuation exactly t. Multiplying them proves (L1).

The value t is finite. At the principal complex embedding |J|=phi^(-1)<1,
whereas every conjugate of J has modulus at least phi^(-1). Thus
J^(p^r) != sigma^r(J). Multiplication by the unit sigma^r(J) does not change
the valuation. This proves (L1) for every p != 5, including p=2.

At p=3 there is the exact identity

\[
\delta_1=3j^2J,
\]

so the valuation in (L1) is one. A larger Frobenius exponent alone therefore
does not produce deeper 3-adic divisibility. This is a scoped obstruction to
that iteration argument, not to the different multiplicity mechanism of the
external proof.

The arithmetic norm records the same fixed local exponent:

\[
v_p\!\left(|N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\delta_r)|\right)
 =\sum_{P\mid p} f_P\,v_P(\delta_1).
\]

## 3. Exact torsion and jet transfer on the fixed CM5 Jacobian

Assume now p is odd and p != 5. The smooth curve y^2=x^5-1 has good
reduction. Over an unramified completion containing j, the automorphism
(x,y) -> (jx,y) gives the integral O-action on its Jacobian B. Write [a] for
the endomorphism supplied by a in O. The characteristic-zero relation
Phi_5(j)=0 extends over the smooth integral model.

On the special fibre the Frobenius of the F_p-model satisfies

\[
\pi^r[a]=[\sigma^r(a)]\pi^r.
\]

This follows first by raising the coordinates of the marked automorphism
to p^r-th powers, then by its integral ring action. In particular [J] has
degree one and invertible tangent map, whereas pi has degree p^2 and zero
tangent map. These facts exclude their identification.

For p congruent to 2 or 3 modulo 5, fifth powering is a bijection on both
F_p and F_(p^2). Counting affine points by their y-coordinate gives
#C(F_(p^i))=p^i+1 for i=1,2. The genus-two Frobenius polynomial is therefore
T^4+p^2 and pi^4=[-p^2]. This classical calculation is also in
[Galbraith and Pujolas, Lemma 3.1](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~sgal018/jordi-paper.pdf).
It is separate from sigma^4=1 and N(J)=1.

On B[p^m], the O-action factors through O/p^mO. Its idempotents e_P split
the finite flat group scheme into components. Let t_P=v_P(delta_1). The
equalizer of [J]^(p^r) and [sigma^r(J)] is exactly

\[
\prod_{P\mid p}e_P B[p^{\min(m,t_P)}]. \tag{L2}
\]

On each component delta_r acts as p^(t_P) times a unit if t_P<m, and as
zero otherwise. This proves (L2) on the integral model and on both fibres.
In particular, at p=3 the equalizer is B[3] inside every B[3^m], for every r.

The same endomorphisms agree on special-fibre formal jets of total degree
less than p: their group-law difference factors through [p]=V composed with
relative Frobenius, whose coordinates start with p-th powers. They do not
agree on the full Jacobian. The nonzero element delta_r acts as a nonzero
isogeny, since multiplying it by its other conjugates gives multiplication
by a nonzero integer. An endomorphism with this property has finite kernel.

## 4. A direct formal-group identification fails at real-field inert primes

For the basis omega_j=x^(j-1) dx/y, j=1,2, the Cartier coefficient at omega_i
is the p-th root of the coefficient of x^(ip-j) in
(x^5-1)^((p-1)/2). To see the rule, rewrite omega_j as
y^(-p) x^(j-1)(x^5-1)^((p-1)/2) dx and apply Cartier to monomials.
The relevant exponents are in the polynomial's degree range. The binomial
coefficients are nonzero modulo p, so an entry is nonzero exactly when
ip is congruent to j modulo 5. Thus the ranks are

| p modulo 5 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cartier rank | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 |

For classes 2 and 3 the only nonzero entry is off the diagonal. Cartier on
invariant differentials is the pullback by Verschiebung, with its Frobenius
twist. Consequently [p]=V composed with relative Frobenius has a nonzero
homogeneous degree-p term of rank one at these primes. For the standard
Cartier/Verschiebung identification and the hyperelliptic coefficient rule,
see Rachel Pries, [The Torelli locus and Newton polygons, section 4.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00998).
The curve is over F_p here, so no semilinear iteration is being replaced by
an unjustified ordinary matrix power.

The comparison logarithm (III.85) in the
[Waterloo exposition, version 2](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/alpoge_claude_grh/zeta-paper_v2.pdf)
has the precise form

\[
L_y(X)=\sum_{e\geq0}p^{-e}X_{\rho^e(y)}^{p^{2e}}
       =X_y+\text{terms of total degree at least }p^2.
\]

For any integral formal group law with this logarithm,
[p]=L^(-1)(pL)=pX modulo (X)^(p^2). Its reduced p-series has no degree-p
term. A formal homomorphism h from the completed CM5 special fibre to this
target must satisfy h[p]=[p]h. Comparing degree p gives

\[
dh\circ dV=0
\]

with the source Frobenius twist understood. At p congruent to 2 or 3 modulo
5, h cannot be tangent-injective, even if the target dimension is larger.
In particular an invertible integral change of coordinates cannot identify
the whole CM5 formal group with this model. At p congruent to 4 modulo 5
this degree-p argument supplies no obstruction. An isogeny, quotient or other
carrier may still be usable, but requires its own valuation-sensitive map.

This does not contradict the external RH construction. Its local kernels
are not asserted to be the whole formal group of this fixed CM5 Jacobian.

## 5. Frobenius symmetry does not supply kernel integrality

In the Waterloo notation (B1)-(B4), fix an odd prime ell, even d>=2, the prescribed
logarithm L and a coefficient field containing a root zeta of order ell^d-1.
For any integer a>=2 set

\[
\beta_a=\ell^{-a}X_0^\ell X_1.
\]

The coefficients lie in Q_ell with value group Z, meeting (B1). The torus
weights at sites 0,1 are 1 and -ell, so this monomial has total weight zero,
meeting (B2). Its even-site degree is ell, odd-site degree one and total
degree ell+1>=3, meeting (B3).

In Delta_even beta, the coefficient of x_0^(ell-1)y_0 X_1 is ell^(1-a).
It has the least nonconstant total degree ell+1 in exp(Delta_even beta).
Terms of the exponential of higher order cannot change it, and substitution
of L cannot change it because L=X+O(X^(ell^2)). Hence the coefficient of
that monomial in exp(Delta_even beta) composed with L has negative valuation
1-a. Condition (B4) fails by an arbitrarily large amount. Delta_odd beta is
zero, but (B4) requires both series to be integral.

Thus residue Frobenius, the torus action and the specified logarithm do not
imply (B4) for general series. The example makes no claim that the actual
normalized theta kernel fails (B4). Establishing (B4) for that kernel is
exactly a substantive input of the intended transfer.

## 6. The complete local interface still required

| Stage | What is available here | Extra data required by the external consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Prime and orbit | O/pO and the marked CM5 action | The specified CM tower, embeddings and required local places. Inertness in a varying imaginary quadratic field is not inertness in Q(sqrt(5)). |
| Formal coordinates | The actual reduced p-series and torsion in (L2) | Integral coordinates for the actual normalized kernel, with their logarithm and torus weights. Any lost tangent direction or isogeny denominator must be retained. |
| Derivatives | The scope of a residue/jet congruence | (B4), the other hypotheses of Theorem J, and the selected admissible derivative/minor. |
| Period pairing | The local ideal of delta_r and its norm exponent | Equality with the same L_0-valued normalized period pairing, up to the specified unit/normalization, on Lambda_div and every generator of R_w. This is (Val), preceding H6(i). |
| Lattice slope | No slope estimate follows from a single norm identity | The actual lattice statements (Lat), H6(ii)-(iii), and the same map and norms used in H8. |

The smallest useful next obligation is one required place, one actual
normalized kernel, integral coordinates establishing (B4), and one admitted
generator pair for which its evaluated derivative is proved equal to the
specified normalized period pairing. Quantification over all generators,
places and words remains a further obligation.

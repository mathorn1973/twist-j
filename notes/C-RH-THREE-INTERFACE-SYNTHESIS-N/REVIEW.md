## Signed augmentation and a restriction on positive norms

**Candidate-D, retained.** The split-prime divisor change of basis is
triangular even after imposing the product cutoff. Its top band survives
the moving orientation cut, with one contributing actual row per support.
The linear asymptotic for its absolute mass follows from a divisor
hyperbola for the real quadratic ideal count and an absolutely summable
Euler multiplier. It uses no zero estimate. The reconstruction removes
one row of a finite isometry. It requires a nonempty set of sampled
primes. The resulting fourth-root characters are formal product samples.

**Candidate-D, conditioning test.** Use the orthonormal coordinates
\(E_d/\sqrt{w(d)}\) on the retained space. Write \(c\) for an actual
signal retaining the top band, and \(k\) for the evaluation vector on that
space. For any positive definite Hermitian form \(G\), direct Cauchy gives
the upper allowance \(P_G=(c^*Gc)^{1/2}(k^*G^{-1}k)^{1/2}\). If a positive
diagonal form \(W\) satisfies \(0<\alpha W\preceq G\preceq\beta W\), then

\[
 P_G\ge \sqrt{\alpha/\beta}\;P_W
 \ge \sqrt{\alpha/\beta}\;
       [D(N)-D(N/2)].
\]

Indeed inversion reverses the upper matrix order, and the second
inequality is scalar Cauchy on the absolute top-band coefficients.
Consequently an estimate \(P_G=O_\epsilon(N^{1/2+\epsilon})\) for
\(0<\epsilon<1/2\) requires \(\beta/\alpha
\gg_\epsilon N^{1-2\epsilon}\) for every such diagonal comparison, along
the scales on which the estimate is claimed. Poor conditioning is only a
necessary condition for this particular norm argument. On the full
space of section 3 of MOBIUS.md the fourth-root sample Gram is uniformly
comparable to the identity. Using its full readout, even on a projected
signal, gives a norm product at least \(\sqrt{D(N)}\,\|c\|\). Exact reconstruction
therefore leaves a genuine signed scalar estimate to prove.

The two tails have different cutoffs and different kernels. The short

window within each pair. The full Euler-unit convolution relates

positive-part test the quantifiers are every fixed accuracy and
arbitrarily late whole windows. The estimate left open here is an
independent derivation of signed cancellation from rapidity data.

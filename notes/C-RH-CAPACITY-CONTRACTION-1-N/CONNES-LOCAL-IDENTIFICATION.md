## 1. Cylinder coordinate and local Blaschke factor

For a rational prime `p`, put

```text
r = p^(-1/2),
z_p(s) = p^(s-1/2).
```

On the critical line `s=1/2+i xi`, `|z_p|=1`. The standard local ratio

```text
gamma_p(s)=(1-p^(-s))^(-1),
rho_p(s)=gamma_p(s)/gamma_p(1-s)
```

satisfies exactly

```text
rho_p(s) = z_p(s) / b_r(z_p(s)),
b_r(z)   = (z-r)/(1-r z).
```

The map `b_r` is the degree-one Blaschke factor with zero `r` and is the
transfer function of the explicit two-state orthogonal colligation already
recorded in `EULER-LOCAL-COMPLETION.md`.

## 2. Exact identification with Connes--Consani B_p

Connes--Consani use the conformal map

```text
psi(v)=1/2+(v+1)/(v-1)
```

from the disk to the left critical half-plane, and write

```text
kappa_p(v)=rho_p(psi(v)).
```

Their Proposition 3.7 defines the Blaschke product `B_p(v)` from the full pole
orbit of `kappa_p` and proves

```text
kappa_p(v) B_p(v) = -p^((v+1)/(v-1)).
```

But

```text
p^((v+1)/(v-1)) = p^(psi(v)-1/2) = z_p(psi(v)),
```

while the preceding local identity gives

```text
kappa_p(v) = z_p(psi(v))/b_r(z_p(psi(v))).
```

Therefore, identically on the disk,

```text
B_p(v) = -b_r(z_p(psi(v))).
```

Thus the degree-one local Blaschke factor on the prime cylinder and the
infinite Blaschke product used by Connes--Consani on the simply connected
Hardy disk are the same inner object seen before and after unfolding the
periodic imaginary direction. The infinite zero set in their disk is the lift
of the single cylinder zero `z_p=r` through `p^(s-1/2)=r`.

**Status:** candidate-T, exact identity obtained by combining the two displayed
source formulas.

```text

```

For a new prime `p` define

```text

N_p(s)=1-p^(s-1),

```

The Connes--Consani Sonin filtration proof uses exactly




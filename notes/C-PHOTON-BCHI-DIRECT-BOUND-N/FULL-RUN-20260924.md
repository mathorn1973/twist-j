## Scope of the successful check

from actual incidence constraints, then sum every compatible coloring.
They confirm both the entirely saturated obstruction and the unsaturated
Fibonacci obstruction. In the latter the moments are 9/325, 9/425 and
9/445 for D=5,7,9; finite values do not prove the all-D formula by
themselves.

The current-sector block forces five plaquette orientations and derives
the entire remaining space through its equality graph: L^2 free planes,
not merely four successful sample fields. Their transverse axial slice
sums vanish exactly. The loop block independently constructs the seven
walks and their incident plaquettes, checks the source weight and both
geometric bounds, and verifies the rational constants including the
expanded length/12 contact budget.

The [written proof](FULL-MEASURE.md) supplies the all-volume probability,
factorial-moment, loop-tail and dense-sector-removal arguments. Those are
not established by enumerating or sampling the full measure in this audit.
The weighted loop method and its earlier length/24 budget are explicitly
credited to the existing low-conflict path note.

Separate agents reviewed the derivations and source using shared material;
this is not blind external acceptance. Ordinary repository CI is separate
and does not run this notes audit on a second architecture.

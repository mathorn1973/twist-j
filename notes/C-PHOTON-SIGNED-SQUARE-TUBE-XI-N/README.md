This note pushes the high-contact test substantially further than a
two-strand ribbon.

Four translated copies of one base path are placed at the vertices of a unit
square cross-section and traversed with alternating signs. The reinforcing
contact density tends to one, yet the whole family remains strongly
subcritical because all strands are generated by one path.

## 1. Square-tube geometry

Take cross-section offsets

`
A=0,
B=e_b,
C=e_b+e_c,
D=e_c.
`

For a base path `P` of length `D_len` in the complementary two-coordinate
plane, traverse:

`
A forward,
B backward,
C forward,
D backward,
`

joining adjacent strands alternately at the far and near endpoints.

For contact-simple tubes the exact counts are

`
m=4D_len+4,
c=4c_P+8,
O_+=4D_len+2,
O_-=0.                                                  (1)
`

There are four reinforcing strand contacts for every base-path edge. The two
additional contacts are the opposite connector pairs at the two endpoints.

Hence

`
O_+/m=(4D_len+2)/(4D_len+4) ->1.                       (2)
`

## 2. Source weight

`

 <=2a^(4D_len+4)r^(4D_len+4c_P+10).                    (3)

The base path has only two available coordinate axes. After its first edge,
each nonbacktracking step has one straight continuation and at most two
turns.

Each turn is repeated on all four strands, so its source factor is `r^4`.

This is the exact entropy-versus-weight inequality for the tube family.

- `V` start vertices;

## 4. Why this matters

These cycles are not rescued by the sign-sensitive low-contact criterion.
Already at base length one,

`
r^(4D+2)>tau^(4D+4),
tau=73/70.
`

Thus they lie in the outstanding simple-cycle complement.

Nevertheless their Xi contribution is uniformly summable because contact
multiplicity does not create independent geometric entropy: one base path
determines four strands simultaneously.

Together with #1184 this shows two explicit regimes:

- reinforcing-contact density approaching one half, controlled;
- reinforcing-contact density approaching one, controlled.

Therefore **reinforcing-contact density itself is not the missing order
parameter** for the simple-cycle problem.

The remaining dangerous class must combine high contact weight with genuinely
large independent geometric entropy.

## 6. Boundary

This is not a covering theorem.

Still open are tubes with extra contacts, other bundle cross-sections,
arbitrary high-contact simple cycles, non-simple currents, multi-cycle
components, full `Xi_L`, `Xi_L^(2)`, P1 and the massless phase.

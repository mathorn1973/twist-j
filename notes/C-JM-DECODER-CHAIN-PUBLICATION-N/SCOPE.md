# Scope correction: one declared construction, not a unique necessary decoder

**NON-CANONICAL. Editorial scope clarification, not a new scientific claim.**
Author instruction of 2026-10-20; publication custody [issue #1696](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1696).
The linked mathematical results retain their original candidate-T/candidate-C
statuses and L1 scopes. This text does not create a Canon theorem or dictionary.

## The claim being made

The JM chain exhibits specified arithmetic readings and a specified retained-
program source, with explicit domains, codomains, equality, metric, reference,
preparation law, outputs, resource capacity, inverse and failure boundaries.
Its mathematical question is whether these declared constructions exist and
satisfy their stated contracts. Existence and the proved consequences do not
require the construction to be the unique possible decoder or to be forced
by the primitive axiom J or the original update U.

The author explicitly rejects imposing a physical-origin derivation as a
prerequisite for this construction's acceptance. Accordingly, the prior chat
framing that native physical origin is the mandatory remaining debt or the
only permissible next step is withdrawn for this lane. Such an origin theorem
or hardware realization is a separate, optional stronger question, not an
unfulfilled clause of the frozen existence results.

This is consistent with [POLICY.md, Reading-family discipline](../../POLICY.md)
and [CORE.md](../../canon/CORE.md): global decoder uniqueness is not presumed.
No change to either authority file is needed or made.

## What remains explicit rather than being declared necessary

The trace form, reference, finite preparation domain, retained digital program,
common ready stock, controlled rational operations, output composition and
comparison Born rule are named choices where the individual modules use them.
A declared model may choose these inputs. They need not be uniquely derived
from J/U to define that model or prove the conditional consequences.

The retained-pair source replaces its predecessor's unspecified conditional
fresh-preparation operation by an explicit rational reflection, addressed
swaps, retained occupied contents and an inverse. Its ready supply and program
access remain part of the declared source contract. That is a concrete
construction, not a failed construction merely because other sources or
readings are also possible.

The construction's algebraic assertions, finite audits, adopted response rule
and interpretation are distinct. Stating the choices openly is not an apology
or an implicit demand to prove uniqueness later. Conversely, allowing several
readings does not erase their assumptions or turn an adopted rule into a theorem
that selected that rule. In particular the chain uses the Born pairing in its
specified comparison models; it does not derive its selection from J/U.

## What this correction does not alter

No source domain, metric, equivalence relation, probability formula, frozen
falsifier, threshold, negative result, preregistration or scientific output
is changed. Scale loss in a bare normalized state, the opposite-seam mixture
collision, REDRAW versus HOLD, dirty-readiness controls, finite stock and
retention of old contents all remain part of the respective contracts.

An existence result for one construction is not completeness of all reading
families. Nonuniqueness is not a falsifier here because uniqueness is not
claimed. If inequivalent outputs are later asserted in one shared physical
context, the ordinary repository requirements for equality, selection,
occurrence and scope still apply. This note does not weaken those requirements.

Nor does this wording assert that the constructed source is already a measured
laboratory device, that Nature uniquely chooses it, or that all of TWIST-J has
been empirically validated. Those stronger claims are not required here and
are not being made. No registered physical-owner obligation is closed, moved
or silently replaced by the JM existence question.

## How to read the archived qualifications

Statements in archived reports such as "not derived from native U" and
"physical realization not supplied" state the limit of what their evidence
establishes. Read them as exclusions of stronger assertions, not as mandatory
prerequisites that invalidate the stated mathematical construction.

Prospective phrases in the preceding chat and handoff text calling native
origin "the next physical debt" are superseded, as a direction for this lane,
by the author's clarification recorded here. Existing public proof bodies and
pins are retained unchanged; this addendum is explicit rather than a silent
rewrite. Separately scoped future work remains possible, but is not demanded
in order to count the present existence result.

## Review standard for the present lane

Review the declared carriers and maps, exact identities, positive forms,
normalization, retained information, preparation-memory contract, occupied-port
continuation, inverse, capacity and evidence. Check every claimed conclusion
against its supplied premises and stated scope. Do not reject the existence

not also been reconstructed as a native-U or laboratory mechanism.

Publication and ordinary CI do not promote the scientific status. A separate
review/fold remains the procedure for any Canon change. This scope correction

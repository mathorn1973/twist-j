## Fifteen independent review obligations

1. Check finite analyticity, the precise fixed-observable permanent-record
   hypothesis, and the quantitative spectral recurrence bound, including
   a passive reference. Do not apply the theorem to a changed Hamiltonian,
   an infinite reservoir or only a finite promised interval.
2. Verify the clock-marginal versus joint-state distinction and the exact
   scope of no-information-without-disturbance. Orthogonal classical
   labels and arbitrary unknown quantum states have different contracts.
3. Check the copy/uncompute ready-code condition, its reference action and
   the probabilistic-instrument counterexample. A recorded coarse outcome
   must not be substituted for the coherent archive/reference output.
4. Check classical and quantum storage dimension bounds, finite reservoir
   entropy-capacity bounds, approximation conventions and their preparation
   assumptions. Do not infer a universal numerical heat cost without the
   stated bath, work, spectrum and correlation hypotheses.
5. Verify the full seven-qubit export SWAP and entire S-qubit reset SWAP on
   arbitrary raw states, including old occupied slots and all correlations.
   Every rejected or dormant run must have the same resource allocation.
6. Prove the N-use joint-channel identity for arbitrary correlated admitted
   inputs and the passive reference. Confirm exactly which thermal factors
   become independent and why the next information input need not do so.
7. Audit the driven piecewise Hamiltonian, same-spectrum SWAP pulse phases,
   contact durations, sign and magnitude of switching work, transient
   storage and energy conservation during the contacts. An ideal timing
   prescription is an explicit resource premise, not a finite closed clock.
8. Verify retained accepted-state invariance under the isolated output's
   d n_L Hamiltonian and the maintained isolation assumption. Export must
   not inherit the old unperturbed recurrence or its original invariant
   history after the intervention.
9. Check the outer three-qubit thermal launch, exact shared-temperature
   activation probability, virtual node zero, instruction ordering,
   padding, export/reset indices, completion edge and full raw positivity.
   No extra supplied pure controller is permitted in this construction.
10. Verify the entire inventory: N+1 S-qubit banks, N seven-qubit slots,
    outer fuel/sink/latch and every outer cursor; all hot/cold/zero-gap
    factors, 15(N+1) admitted input qubits and their entropy. The final
    reset and next-use information input must not be omitted.
11. Check the noncircular enlarged temperature choice, outer cold mixture,
    exact single-slot accepted map, complete-tape channel and every
    conditional normalization bound. A common uncertain outer launch
    must not be represented as independent success events.
12. Verify source-effect transport into the spent cartridge and the single
    export-before-reset contribution to its fixed-location test. Compare
    record-only reading with global-completion-qualified reading, and
    preserve the original unwanted-band margin.
13. Check exact rounded conservation, the fuel-inclusive C_data budget,
    all residual counterterms, all-raw unconditioned drift, actual initial
    expectation, full-generator mean/variance and capacity. The old device
    Hamiltonian occurs through an instruction matrix, not as an additional
    continuously running term in the outer law.
14. Verify the exact finite holding window, inner hardware replacement and
    full outer return at 3T_hold. State the growing macro support/degree
    honestly. Neither the old locality theorem nor permanent storage may
    be inferred for the new autonomous controller.
15. Confirm the proof manifest, five-file addition, unchanged preceding
    seventeen files, final CI/tree pins and public-content scope. Check
    that no spent bank, controller, old value or reset resource is silently
    restored or discarded. Same-session checks and CI are not independent
    mathematical acceptance.

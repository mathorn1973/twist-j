# Consequences of the closed numerical account and next decoder contracts

**NON-CANONICAL / L1 analysis and proposed work contracts / 2026-10-13.**
Original work Apache-2.0, A. M. Thorn.

This document combines the actual saved-output audit with the current
theoretical drafts. It proposes new work; it reports no new solver,
apparatus, physical experiment or Canon promotion.

## 1. Decisions available now

The unchanged full-pulse DP5/N384 attempt has a valid saved account and
all six precision radii <=1/1000. Four selected sine-versus-linear
discrepancies are strictly above that threshold, for both angle signs.
The current pulse recipe therefore excludes agreement within 1/1000 on
those selected witnesses under its fixed compensated model.
A finer enclosure cannot turn those positive lower bounds into agreement.
One remaining pair is undecided; settling it is not needed for the
already resolved two-sign conclusion.

There is also a concrete limit on resolving pair (1,2) with this same
retained-center account. Its saved center upper endpoint plus the saved
thermal radius is exactly 9632247108013357/9223372036854775808,
about 0.001044330324, already greater than 1/1000. Even a zero numerical
radius would not certify WITHIN_TARGET from these unchanged K3 intervals.
This is a limitation of the retained-center/tail enclosure, not a lower
bound on the actual discrepancy or a universal impossibility. A separate
thermal-tail treatment/domain change would need its own explicit contract.

The inherited local enclosures and restored coherence rectangles remain
premises: audit A did not independently recompute MPFR preparation,
per-cell quintics/nodes or final restoration. A valid conditional numerical
account is stronger than an unfinished prefix and weaker than independent
propagation certification or a physical apparatus result.

The bracket/pentad successor has already solved the exact minimum of the
prescribed external evaluator context: five labels for T, SO and GL;
four consecutive forward bits suffice without extra time/parity input.
Those four bits are theta_n through theta_(n+3), including three future
values. The five labels have no autonomous next-label map. This result
must not be rerun as another minimum search.

The reader-context T1, ternary contact/archive T2 and complete-product
T3 address different carriers. A commuting checkpoint symmetry does not
provide a raw-source reader, an archive or a reversible global update.
The existing selected effective ETH-QDD dictionary is complete at status D;
independent physical admission and realization remain open.

## 2. One common contract comes first

For the first decoder leg, freeze a finite-horizon **signed classical-port
response and energy-balance model**, with its physical interpretation
explicitly provisional. List the source/preparation domain, source-receiver
boundary, carrier, permitted contacts, actual clock input, units/reference,
observables, equality or error norm, all occupied helper registers, horizon,
continuation and reset owner. Record which premises are definitions,
proved mathematics, candidate constructions or open physical assumptions.

This is a manageable first leg without an ion experiment. The coherent
QDD-source branch remains a separately typed problem: preserving an
unknown complete quantum state/reference while extracting an informative
record cannot be silently assumed. A successful classical analogue would
validate its declared implementation, not independently identify U with nature.

The contract must specify the source/contact class before matching the
desired output. If admitted physical readings disagree, require an
independent selection rule or physical equivalence. Otherwise that physical
conclusion stays open.

P, O and R0 are small analytical lanes; B is a bounded numerical-certification
successor design. None requires a new full trajectory campaign. B would close
one specific remaining dependency, not the unarchived per-cell enclosure gap.
Its first execution requires its own complete public contract/source pin;
the closed DP5 identifier and first outcome are not reused.

For P, the straightforward forward-window construction has a four-bit
shift register initialized once on 0110, receiving a fresh theta_(n+4)
at step n. Its readout is the proved five-class function. This is a
driven construction with an exposed look-ahead port; its internal-state
minimum is not asserted. T1's existing 23-state observer instead reads the
current bit theta_n and has its own raw-(q,r) contract. Keep the two inputs
and readout types distinct.

O should consume the existing signed-port observability candidate only
when its exact source domain is selected. One origin port has a dark
source kernel; four signed first-cut ports and four squared deposits are
different information channels. More waiting or fitting cannot remove
an established kernel. Physical source-data qualification and calibration
remain separate from a mathematical inverse.

## 4. Serial assembly and the first prediction

1. Freeze P, then prove its actual driven updater D. Check transient
   checkpoints as well as the synchronized active union.
2. Combine D with R0 into a complete finite-H apparatus map. Off-preparation
   helper inputs must have an explicitly different projection or a separately
   declared invariant carrier, as required by T3. Retain the driver, old
   context, pointer and occupied-memory information in the inverse.
3. Compose the resulting typed apparatus with T2 only after the
   source/contact/field bridge has been specified on a common finite domain.
   The ternary last-gain context is not the five-valued time context.
   An indefinitely complete arbitrary contact history requires growing
   capacity; a fixed finite apparatus must declare a finite horizon or
   bounded history claim.
4. Join that construction with O. Freeze one- and two-step signed responses
   on held-out preparations and a complete energy/work boundary account.
   Establish one independent discriminating prediction with its joint
   uncertainty budget before opening validation outcomes.
5. Only then extend the same contract to persistent events, occupied
   continuation, renewal and a specified occurrence/ensemble law. A
   reservoir threshold batch can emit zero or several events; selecting
   exactly one is an additional rule.

The missing native operation T remains an explicit target equation:
the actual induced difference map of an admitted contact must equal T
on its declared reachable fibers. Longer words from the same five native
generators cannot implement it. Counter-dependent relabeling and arbitrary
invariant-label permutations do not select the physical contact class.

The physical dictionary may be an explicit testable effective hypothesis.
Its successful held-out prediction establishes compatibility of that
dictionary and implementation on the frozen domain. It does not close
unrelated photon, particle, cosmological or geometric selection gates.

## 5. Resource and publication decisions

The original full-pulse attempt, audit source and theoretical proofs remain
unchanged. All actual first outcomes are preserved. The exact supporting
CI gate is distinct from numerical propagation and from notes-only audits.
All current new claims remain NON-CANONICAL; later review/fold is separate.

A new pulse-design task is justified by the resolved discrepancy if the
common decoder contract actually needs this ion-model architecture.
Before any large campaign, freeze its control family, target, held-out
criterion and a short screening budget. A smaller step or larger cutoff
alone addresses enclosure width, not the already resolved model response.
The currently deferred ion experiment is not required for the classical
port/dictionary work above.

Five contexts supply no probability distribution, entropy value,
temperature or heat price. A physical energy/work account must include
clock/control/reference and detector/reset ports under its own hypotheses.
Keep finite response prediction, event semantics and L6 measure as distinct
claims with named gate owners.

## Mathematical and account checks

* **Actual carried curve.** Conditional on a unitary full-space propagator
  and valid local enclosures, variation of constants gives the preceding
  error plus the actual polynomial defect integral and outgoing stored-state
  jump. The next polynomial starts at the stored endpoint. Accumulated
  certificates may exceed the exact sum through outward rounding; no reset,
  normalization or projected-unitarity premise is introduced.
* **Exact binary64 norms.** The endpoint decoder ignores only the sign bit,
  rejects exponent 2047, restores the normal hidden significand bit and
  retains subnormal significands. With denominator 2^2148, a normal squared
  term has numerator shifted by 2(exponent-1), while a subnormal squared
  term has shift zero. The bucket formula therefore sums the actual dyadic
  squares exactly, including squares that underflow in binary64 arithmetic.
  Terminal interval checks are linked to final endpoints by byte equality.
* **Gram minimum.** For the two nonnegative bounds A and B, a radius
  satisfying 2r>=min(A,B) is equivalent to (2r>=A) OR (2r>=B). The OR in
  audit_pair is correct. Each affine-square-root comparison also checks
  the nonnegative difference before squaring. The branch bounds retain the
  actual stored norms and exact unrenormalized thermal weights.
* **Thermal and interval account.** The omitted sine contribution is charged
  once as TAIL/2. Rational rectangle extrema, center bounds, arithmetic
  halfwidth, total radius and outward clipped final intervals are checked
  separately. Precision uses total<=1/1000; discrepancy requires a strict
  lower endpoint>1/1000, or upper endpoint<=1/1000 for within-target status.
  A valid wide account can resolve discrepancy while failing precision.
* **Completeness.** The six label cases have active-segment counts
  32,16,16,32,16,16. Fifteen batches per case give 90 tasks, 1920 active
  task-segments, 5280 inactive carries, 737280 cells and 90*81=7290 snapshots.
  Final code checks the complete canonical prefixes, initial basis vectors,
  cumulative certificate fields, terminal file sizes and terminal equality,
  worker coverage and the complete lifecycle/progress inventories.

## Remaining inherited and unavailable scope

The proof is conditional on the declared unitary propagator and valid local
enclosures. This review does not independently rederive every inherited
norm-ball operation, analytic infinite tail, between-node remainder or MPFR
force-model preparation. PROOF_REVIEW.md identifies those dependencies.

Saved boundaries omit per-cell quintic coefficients, internal stages and
Gauss-node evaluations. Their actual incoming polynomial, intermediate
arithmetic bounds, defect integral and continuity cannot be independently
reconstructed from these receipts. Active intermediate endpoint norms are
not all recomputed by default; the optional all-snapshot check has a broader
explicit scope. The displacement/restoration intervals and force model are
not recomputed from endpoint bytes. Exact terminal norms do not supply any
of these missing checks.

Inventory, source manifests and stream receipts are mutually checked saved
records. They do not authenticate the original machine or execution, prove
crash durability, or demonstrate that a supplied directory was closed by an
independent observer. The copied executable is rehashed only when explicitly
supplied. The auditor's PASS concerns its declared consistency checks and
exact aggregation, not independent propagation validation.

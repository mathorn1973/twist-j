## Falsifiers and remaining obligations

An incorrect recurrence, reused correlated tree input, unretained dirty
state, or unfunded energy-changing gate invalidates the preparation claim.
A hidden clean parity wire, routing head or row would invalidate its input
account. A local gate leaving the buffered complete sectors invalidates
telescoping. Replacing the actual incomplete-sector word by an unproved
global identity invalidates the full-rank-tail account.

A negative raw-space block, noncommuting onsite term, site collision,
excess support/range/degree, or omitted ladder copy invalidates the
Hamiltonian or locality claim. A generic cross-energy input cannot be
covered by silently discarding its free evolution. The admitted logical
code remains an input promise, not a source of consumable reset purity.

Wrong history indices, an unfinished final-row acceptance flag, omitted
old latch, incorrect normalization denominator or missing preparation
error invalidates the endpoint-channel comparison. Actual noisy readiness
does not inherit an exact SWAP or exact record identity merely because the
error is small. The old post-read storage interval is not imported into
the new Hamiltonian.

The common-temperature bank itself, the nearly sharp charged work
distribution, polarized master-cursor distribution, logical input, product
boundary and exact physical couplings remain supplied. Work and cursor
states commute with their bare energies but the combined input is not
stationary under the full interacting Hamiltonian. This construction
prepares neither those bootstrap resources from equilibrium nor a closed
cycle that renews them.

A finite full-rank closed resource cannot become exactly pure by a
unitary; a stationary total input cannot start preparation under its fixed
Hamiltonian. These are scoped obstructions, not a prohibition of all
future native couplings, external resources, or conditioned instruments.

Material implementation, generation of the common bath and residual
bootstrap, bounded local dimension, practical gate counts, precision/noise,
physical readout, occurrence and useful replenishment/reset remain open.
The new law is not derived from unmodified U_rec.

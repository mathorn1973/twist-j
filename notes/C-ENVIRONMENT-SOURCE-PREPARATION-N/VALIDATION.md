## Falsifiers and residual obligations

A negative raw-space block, incorrect source/control graph, active-path
escape, wrong diagonal or nonconserving primitive would invalidate the
claimed Hamiltonian. A site collision, excess range/support/valence or
unaccounted hardware would invalidate its physical-locality account.

The environmental claim fails if any specified inactive input can launch,
if q is normalized away in unconditioned throughput, if the source is
claimed pure with unit weight, or if the full interaction energy/covariance
is omitted. Preparation fails at the stated scope if the explicit good
packet is outside the source-ready subspace or its complete-state bound
confuses a norm distance with a bad weight. An invalid readiness predicate
or premature accepted compiler prefix invalidates the corresponding
read interface.

The interpretation would exceed its proof if local Gibbs marginals were
called full-H equilibrium, if stored mean energy were called the minimum
work of making every initial state, or if a mathematical projection or
partial trace were presented as an already implemented instrument.

The initial environment Gibbs states, pure cold vacuum, ready data fuel,
work/latch, product contact condition and precise local couplings remain
supplied. Full-H stationarity cannot generate the specified nonstationary
input under H-conserving closed evolution. This is not a no-go theorem for
all future native couplings or every local state-preparation protocol.

Material realization, noise and calibration, manufacture of the input
environment and clean apparatus, physical readout, occurrence,
replenishment and useful reset remain open. No passive reference or old
information is physically erased to hide these obligations.

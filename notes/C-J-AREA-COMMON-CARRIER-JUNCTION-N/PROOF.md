## 1. A residue label map is not a dynamical embedding

The explicit arithmetic injection

    iota_Z:Z^2 -> O^2, (p,q) -> (p*1_O,q*1_O)

is a valid map. Its composite with rho is (p mod5,q mod5). It neither
retains the integer reference/bank/control state nor intertwines the
integer motion with T_A. In fact

    T_A iota_Z(1,0) = (1+j^2,0) not in iota_Z(Z^2).

The basis 1,j,j^2,j^3 is Z-linearly independent, so 1+j^2 is not an
integer. Thus the proposed constant-coordinate image is not invariant
under T_A. This refutes that particular literal identification; a larger
integer coefficient carrier or a different reading is outside the test.

Likewise, rho(1)=rho(j)=1. No function of rho(alpha) alone can recover
both literal algebraic values 1 and j. The selected character t->j^t has
domain F5 and is a new representation of an additive label; it is not an
inverse to rho on cyclotomic scalars. The source #1583 already distinguishes
these objects. This note records the consequence for a proposed junction,
not a correction of #1583 or a new character classification.

An alternating form also needs two vectors. A polygonal path on Z^2 is a
sequence of points, not a single pair of O^2 vectors. A common implementation
must specify which actual point/edge/reference supplies each vector at
each cut. Equality of separately assigned scalar areas does not supply
that specification or the commuting transition diagrams.

## 2. Original U cannot be identified with a global permutation

Let F:S->S be a bijection and suppose pi:S->Omega_N is onto with

    pi F = U pi.                                             (1)

Because F and pi are onto, pi(F(S))=Omega_N. But (1) also gives
pi(F(S))=U(Omega_N). This is impossible: no state with native n=0
belongs to U(Omega_N), since U increases n by one. Therefore this exact
surjective-factor specification cannot describe a global permutation.

This is a narrow elementary obstruction to (1), not a no-go for initialized
forward simulation, partial projections, an image inverse, an enlarged
clock/historical carrier or a separately admitted contact. The adopted
KERNEL-Z6-SYNCHRONIZATION theorem additionally records merging of initial
checkpoint sheets; invertibility of each g_i is not invertibility of
the state-selected update. That theorem is inherited, not re-executed here.

A B-state permutation may therefore not be relabelled U merely because its
constituent letters have inverses. The joint certificate must identify its
actual inverse domain and the exact status of its native projection.
The contract does not impose (1) on every future implementation, so this
argument cannot yield COMPLETE_CLASS_NEGATIVE.

## 3. Reference preparation needs both funding and retained information

Use the declared integer comparison cost H_int. Keeping q,p and all other
resources fixed, changing the blank reference (0,0) to (1,-1) raises its
cost from 0 to 2. Conservation requires a decrease of 2 in a named donor
or bank. Starting a calculation at (1,-1) simply supplies these two units;
it is not a derivation of their preparation. This is an account in the
chosen integer units, not a universal or physical preparation bound.

The map that overwrites every occupied (u,v) with (1,-1), keeping all
other fields fixed, is noninjective. Funding that reset from the scalar
bank alone still need not repair it. Take these two complete B states:

    s1=(0,0,1,0,2,+1,+1,0),
    s2=(0,0,0,1,2,+1,+1,0).

Both have E_B=3. A purported reset that sets the references to (1,-1),
deducts their cost increase from b_B, and changes nothing else sends both to

    (0,0,1,-1,1,+1,+1,0).

It conserves the scalar total but loses the distinction s1!=s2. Hence
it has no inverse. This is an exact new contract test of that naive
preparation extension, NOT a counterexample to U_* (which has no such
reset) or a re-proof/closure of the owned CONTACT-RECORD reset boundary.

A legitimate once-only loading from a declared blank subdomain can have
an inverse on its image. A universal occupied-state reset must instead
retain the displaced information somewhere in the complete state. The
location, initialization, cost and later accessibility of that state are
obligations, not free notation.

## 4. Occupied reference reading is different from cold reading

For an actual Q displacement the references do not move. For an actual
P displacement they both change by q*dp; a refusal has dp=0. Therefore,
directly from the declared elementary updates, for the mathematical sum
A=sum q_i dp_i and arbitrary initial references,

    u=u0+A, v=v0+A,
    u^2-v^2=(u0^2-v0^2)+2(u0-v0)A.                          (2)

Equation (2) is already exposed in the source PREREG; it is used as a
local interface requirement, not claimed as independent confirmation of
the author's all-history theorem. It distinguishes the baseline term,
the reference gap and the new path increment. With (u0,v0)=(1,-1), the
special normalization divides by four. With arbitrary occupied references,
the same formula is not a universal incremental-area reader.

For u0=v0=0, A=+1 and A=-1 both give the ordered squared record (1,1).
The signed references are different and a richer reader could distinguish
them. These are not equal complete states and this is not a no-go for all
readers. It rejects only extending the cold squared-cost reader to arbitrary
unprepared references without a baseline/gap contract.

Preserving the accumulated sum in u,v also does not preserve a separate
archive of every previous sum. An accepted continuation with opposite
increment changes that sum back. Any claim of historical persistence must
name a different retained record and its updating law.

## 5. The zero-bank test rejects a nominal Weyl word

Take the literal admitted B state

    s=(0,0,1,-1,0,+1,+1,0).

Its current c requests Q+ and h=+1. The candidate q=1 raises H_int by
1, while b_B=0. By definition the attempt is refused. The actual state is

    U_*(s)=(0,0,1,-1,0,-1,+1,1).

Thus dq=dp=0; the executed Weyl displacement is I. A nominal compiler
that assigns X to this attempted Q+ assigns a different operator:
I e_0=e_0 but X e_0=e_1. It is false at this one raw state.

The energy account remains 2+0=2. The refusal still changes sigma and c;
it is not identity on the complete machine. Conversely its Weyl displacement
is identity. Keeping these two statements distinct is essential for both
the inverse and the phase calculation. This is a substitution in the
declared law, not an execution or full inverse audit of #1585.

## 6. Fivefold character return leaves a paid record

Compute an accepted positive square directly on the chosen Weyl basis,
with chronological operators X,Z,X^-1,Z^-1. For every k in F5,

    e_k -> e_(k+1) -> j^(k+1)e_(k+1)
        -> j^(k+1)e_k -> j e_k.

Its literal product is jI. For the oppositely oriented sequence
X,Z^-1,X^-1,Z the same basis calculation gives j^-1 I. This is an
operator calculation, not an assignment W:=j^A. Five repetitions of
either executed square give I because j^5=1.

On the integer comparison, CONDITIONALLY ON ACCEPTANCE of every step,
the positive square returns q,p to zero and adds 1 to both references:
only its first P step has q=1; its last P step has q=0. Five such squares
from (1,-1) therefore end at (6,4). The ordered reference costs are
(36,16), the new reference cost is 52 rather than 2, and a conserved
H_int+b_B account requires bank decrease 50. The negative five-square
word instead gives (-4,-6) and ordered costs (16,36). The algebraic
operator is I for both, whereas the ordered reference records differ.

These conditional endpoint substitutions do not verify that a particular
initial bank funds every intermediate step, nor prove the source's sharp
budget threshold. The advertised B=52 example and general threshold stay
with R2. This note needs only the conditional distinction: identity of the
character is strictly weaker than identity of the reference and energy
state. A finite character cannot encode unrestricted integer winding.

Other closures must likewise stay separate. In the chosen finite label
action diag(2,3), the fourth power is I modulo 5. D:e_k->e_(3k) also
has fourth power I. A fifth-order central phase does not give either of
these maps period five. The area covariance advertised for T_A^5 does
not make T_A^5 the identity on O^2: its second coordinate multiplies by
phi^5, with phi>1. The B controller returns after four microsteps, while
the original native counter increases. Matching the word 'return' across
these different types supplies no clock or full-state equality.

## 7. An internal program is a resource, not an incoming source contact

The two source preparations s_(B,+) and s_(B,-) in #1585 differ in eta.
That bit selects the order/sign of the P requests and is part of the
complete state. They are therefore not the same complete apparatus
preparation if eta is assigned to its controller. This does NOT deny
that U_* is internally autonomous after preparation: its program is
retained in its state, rather than fetched from an external list.

To claim a source-to-receiver experiment with a common complete apparatus,
one must either show how the actual source loads eta through a permitted
contact, or explicitly type eta as source data and supply its native
encoding, loading, inverse and cost. No such map from an original native
source into this integer controller is supplied by merely juxtaposing
the two PRs. Choosing eta to match the desired output is not that map.

The reader's denial of direct eta access is a meaningful declared port
restriction, not a claim that eta is absent from the full state. A negative
statement about coarse energy histories cannot be extended to readers
given eta or the full ordered reference. Native theta_n, integer c and
program eta are different typed resources, even when each takes finitely
many values at a particular stage.

## 8. A phase representation is not a measured phase record

Literal operators jI and j^-1 I differ. Under equivalence of nonzero
vectors up to arbitrary nonzero scalar, however, both act as identity on
rays. A claim of observable relative phase must specify the additional
reference, controlled operation, observable and post-state equality that
make that relative distinction accessible. An ordered integer record can
mathematically distinguish orientations without implementing such a phase
measurement. The two statements have different codomains.

This observation does not reject an existing registered physical dictionary;
it prevents this particular L1 junction from quietly supplying a stronger
experimental conclusion. No physical measurement impossibility is proved.

## 9. Decision

The above witnesses reject specific shortcuts and constrain a future joint
certificate. They do not exhibit an admitted common S, a complete native
generator class, a source-independent loading interface, a joint integer
cost or a compatible global inverse. The existing typed input maps do
not grant any of these missing permissions.

Therefore the overall decision is **STOP_UNTYPED**, with the analytical
contract and necessary conditions delivered. It is not
COMPLETE_CLASS_NEGATIVE and not an independent acceptance of #1583/#1585.
There is no known conflict in ownership because N stops at the junction:
compiling W_b/Ucal, the rational H_B, or the inherited integer-chain law
is explicitly left to its existing owner.

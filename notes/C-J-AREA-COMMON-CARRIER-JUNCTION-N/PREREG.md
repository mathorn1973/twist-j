## 5. Failure conditions and decision rule

Zero tolerance, logical rather than statistical tests:

1. PREPARATION: compare the complete receiver and controller before source
   contact. A sign-dependent reference/program counted as common ready,
   or a fixed-reference overwrite with no retained inverse data, fails.
2. OCCUPANCY: require a total law on occupied references and a reader whose
   domain includes exactly the claimed initial-reference classes. Missing
   baseline, branch flag or archive state is not filled by an oracle.
3. FUNDING: every accepted and refused microstep, preparation and controller
   transition must have the declared integer account. Use the source's
   zero-bank first-Q attempt as a literal negative control for nominal words.
4. FIVEFOLD: distinguish central character return, reference reset, complete
   state return, T_A^5 area covariance, and the finite controller period.
   A phase equality alone must never certify equality of any larger state.
5. ACTUAL ORDER: construct the Weyl product from executed displacements,
   with I on a refused displacement; deriving it by setting W=j^A is invalid.
6. CONTROLLER: include eta, sigma, c, native n, schedule and any fetched
   program. A different eta is a different full preparation unless an
   explicit source-loading interface accounts for it. No external oracle.
7. NATIVE/INVERSE: require named maps and commuting diagrams. A finite
   checkpoint is not Omega_N; invertible letters do not invert selected U.
8. SELECTION/COMPLETENESS: an arbitrary embedding, cost or character does not
   constitute a complete independently admitted native class.

One exact violation refutes only the specified proposed identification.
Missing domains/permissions are admission STOP, not a scientific falsifier
of every possible native implementation. Record each failure at its exact
scope; do not move the contract after the pin.

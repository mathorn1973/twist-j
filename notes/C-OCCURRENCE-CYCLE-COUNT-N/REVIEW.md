## Counting: why the applicable conclusion is STOP

The native full state includes a counter with value n+k after k positive
steps. Hence it has no finite full-state cycles. This argument does not
rule out all finite factors; the required autonomous, history-preserving
factor is simply not supplied for this proposed test.

#1334 supplies a complex vector/density update and a complete formal
instrument, but expressly leaves the realized record and actual law
undefined. A finite formal return does not fill that interface. Counting
basis indices or inverse-tail visits would introduce a new actual-state
interpretation or trial rule. The proposed frozen input also lacks the
independent phase preparation domains and complete finite-preparation
descriptor needed for the requested comparison.

The occurrence preregistration therefore correctly stops before counting.
It distinguishes all starts of one preparation from all starts of a
different phase, permits nonunique microscopic preparation classes, keeps
the conditioned remaining state under the same law, and requires full
ordered trial histories rather than single-symbol marginals. Neither an
unknown readiness promise nor absence of a completed trial is silently
turned into a successful event. No new reservoir, reset, selected branch,
finite clock quotient or substitute machine repairs the missing contract.

uniform input to uniform output but also transports a point mass to a point
mass. Time frequencies, uniform state counting and the law of the first
trial are kept distinct. Reversibility permits a record of an existing
classical value and is not a general sampling-impossibility theorem.

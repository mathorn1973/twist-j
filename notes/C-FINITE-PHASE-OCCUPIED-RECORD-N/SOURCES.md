## 4. Meaning of the negative and positive results

The primary proof exhibits exact whole-step spectral degeneracy of
different readout responses: two finite preparations differ only in
relative bank phase, have the same complete spectral law for
\(U_{\rm rec}\), and give different one-step receiver weights. It
therefore separates this phase information from the entire nominated
spectral-energy distribution, not merely from a common mean.

Its reducing-sector proof deliberately removes the conversion contact.
The simple \(3{:}4\) active-contact calibration separately restores
genuine contact interference under the same operator, but its dark/bright pair has
different second spectral moments. That failed equal-spectrum extension
is retained as a precise boundary, not relabeled as success.

The separate
[analytical supplement](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1775#issuecomment-6399077081)
uses a different finite source pair built from the skew-adjoint
polynomial \(U^2-U^{-2}\). Its three-step original-receiver contrast
and equal full spectral laws are proved in ACTIVE_EQUAL_SPECTRUM.
This is explicitly result-exposed analytical work beyond the simple
contact pair. It changes neither the frozen primary experiment nor the
failed claim about that simple pair.

The finite receiver-only flag requires one excluded old-memory direction.
The actual old data may fill the other 25 dimensions, including all
reference correlations. The impossibility with arbitrary full support
is an indication-capacity result; the full coherent exchange itself
remains valid on all 26 labels.

The complete second-step calculation gives both an uninterrupted output
and a different output for a protocol with an inserted receiver
instrument. This distinction prevents a branch decomposition from being
misread as an already realized classical history.

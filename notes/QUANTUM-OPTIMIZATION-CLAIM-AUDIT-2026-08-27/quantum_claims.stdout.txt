VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The four numerical assertions in the challenge text are false as
stated. 20! is 61 orders of magnitude SMALLER than the atom count,
not larger. Exact optimization of the named 20-node instance is a
seconds-to-minutes job on one core using a 1962 algorithm, and it
returns a PROVEN optimum, which no annealer or sampler does. Even a
perfect fault-tolerant Grover search loses to that algorithm from 21
nodes upward. The Atari claim is directionally right but for the
wrong reason: 128 bytes of RAM stops it, not the age of the universe,
which it beats by four orders of magnitude.

This settles arithmetic only. It does not touch whether the Ford
Otosan or Pattison deployments occurred; see AUDIT.md, which finds
those real but benchmarked against the incumbent process rather than
against a tuned classical solver.

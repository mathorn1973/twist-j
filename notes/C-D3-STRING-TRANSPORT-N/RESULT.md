## Decisive positive result

A five-state integer link field on the fixed D3 graph admits an explicit
reversible local update whose straight unit-flux strings translate exactly:

    T^m f = tau_(+/- 2m b1) f,       for every integer m.

This equality covers EVERY link coordinate, not merely the location of a
charge maximum. Finite even-length strings are neutral pairs with endpoint
charges -1,+1 and selected energy H_def=2. A single endpoint also translates,
with H_def=1, when its half-infinite flux tail is retained. The latter is an
infinite-domain theorem, not a finite numerical simulation or a compactly
supported isolated charged state.

The link alphabet {-2,-1,0,1,2}, charge n=G^T f, energy H_def=sum n^2, and
the even/odd matching schedule are NEW premises. They replace neither the
old photon energy nor native U. The direction and sublattice fix the speed
and chirality, so this is not isotropic free motion, a rest-mass law, or an
identified electron/proton/neutron.

The contact is derived within its one-edge class. For d=n_a-n_b,
Delta H_def=2delta(delta-d); the only possible moves are delta=0,d.
The selected law executes d when f_e+d stays in the alphabet. It is a full
involution, exchanges the endpoint charges, and gives exact local charge
and energy transport. Energy conservation does not by itself select the
alphabet, energy, or the choice to execute every admitted branch.

## What prevents this from already being the requested physics

1. The complete charge multiset is conserved. No charged pair is generated
   from a neutral state; every neutral configuration is pointwise fixed.
   There is NO neutral positive-energy propagating photon sector here.
2. A divergence-free background loop can block a hop while leaving exactly
   the same charges and H_def. Charge labels alone therefore do not determine
   the dynamics. The complete link field, not a hidden particle register,
   carries the relevant additional state.
3. The old wave-energy identification A=x=v=0, E=f gives F_wave=sum f_e^2.
   A unit string of length L has F_wave=L, while H_def=2. Adding any positive
   coefficient kappa of that term gives 2+kappa L. A one-link endpoint
   extension preserves H_def but increases F_wave by one. The naive joint
   energy is not conserved. This is a restricted failed bridge, not a general
   theorem against deconfined charge or alternative field energies.
4. Bounded integer links are a different topology/carrier from the old dense
   Z[phi] marked field. This does not overturn the earlier continuous
   one-electric-coordinate obstruction. Joining the two theories is not done.

The known string-endpoint idea motivated this work. No equivalence with
Levin/Wen's quantum rotor model or with actual spin ice was proved.
The contribution is the exact chosen integer mechanism, its all-time
translation certificate, and its explicit incompatibility with the naive
old-energy sum. It is not a claim of novelty for string excitations in general.

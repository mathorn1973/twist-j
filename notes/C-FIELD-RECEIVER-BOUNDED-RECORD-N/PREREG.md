## Frozen carrier, law, preparation and reader

PROOF.md defines the complete conditional theorem. For every integer N>=2,
each cell stores three ordered reacting Z4 vectors, three spectator Z4
vectors, six raw field coordinates and a nonnegative integer resource.
All three reacting vectors occupy the same actual electric vertex 0.
The N-1 separately stored nonnegative neutral channels give 32N-1 literal
coordinates. Equality is full-coordinate equality, with no phase quotient.

The inherited field maps, reaction G and contacts A,B are exactly #1310's.
Let u=(1,0,0,0), v=(0,-1,0,0), t=(0,-1,1,0) and
M0=(u,v,t)=AM, M1=(v,t,u), M2=(t,u,v). Add one receiver-only gate K:
M0 -> M1 -> M2 -> M0, fixing every other matter triple and every other
coordinate. The designated terminal receiver is an architectural role.
K is applied nowhere else. The new chronological law is
G; K; A; B; F, with inverse F^-1; B; A; K^-1; G.
This is an explicitly changed, five-layer law with a stored autonomous
phase/timing mechanism, not the unchanged predecessor law.

The source starts (R,0,PLw,0), with integer H(w)=1; all intermediate cells
start (ZM,0,0,0); the receiver starts (R,0,0,0); all channels start zero.
The single fixed reader returns 1 precisely when the receiver's matter
triple is M0, M1 or M2, otherwise 0. It reads only that complete cell's
stored coordinates and has no N, step number or external history input.
The claim is an event bit, not source-vector or payload recovery.

The predicted first arrival is N-1, first paid reaction and first read 1
are N. The exact readable boundary states are N,N+1,N+2; the first reset
is N+3. Retention means this first episode of three complete macrosteps.
The full closed formula through N+3, total energy 41, all individual
cut-j variants and their all-time fixed downstream state belong to the
same theorem. Each cut replaces BOTH contacts by identity and retains q_j.
The new gate preserves full energy, actual charges, Gauss defects and
reacting-vector sums for every legal background; it has inverse K^2.

No additional coordinate or initial energy is used, but the selected new
operation, fixed receiver role and extra layer are resources. Three steps
is a chosen construction, not a minimality or optimality claim. This is not
permanent or perturbation-protected memory, arbitrary-background event
detection, a translation-homogeneous law, native U, or a law derived from J.
Finite-energy recurrence remains. Later reappearance is not excluded.
The old-law obstruction applies to a stateless receiver-cell-only reader:
the full target cell resets at N+1, while the last channel contains 2.

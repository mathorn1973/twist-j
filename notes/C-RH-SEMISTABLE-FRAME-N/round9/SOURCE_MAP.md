## Classical local representation theory

- Atobe, Kondo and Yasuda, *Local newforms for the general linear groups over a non-archimedean local field*: [original paper, arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.09070), [published version](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2022.17). Theorems 1.1 / 2.1-2.2 give the distinguished tuple, weight equal to the conductor, one-dimensional newforms, and vanishing at lower total weight. The base field has characteristic zero. Proposition 5.2 supplies the compact-invariant Mackey formula used in the spherical case.
- Zelevinsky, *Induced representations of reductive p-adic groups II. On irreducible representations of GL(n)*, Ann. Sci. ENS 13 (1980), 165-210: [primary PDF](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1980_4_13_2_165_0.pdf), DOI 10.24033/asens.1379. Example 3.2 identifies segment subrepresentations on unramified GL_1 lines as determinant characters. Theorem 4.2 is the pairwise-unlinked irreducibility criterion.
- Reeder, *Old Forms on GL_n*, American Journal of Mathematics 113 (1991), 911-930, [DOI 10.2307/2374790](https://doi.org/10.2307/2374790), Theorem 1, is the classical generic oldform comparison. The nongeneric rule in this note does not depend on extending Reeder's genericity hypothesis; it uses Atobe-Kondo-Yasuda and the separately proved spherical interval count.

The Satake interval expression and its application to the actual Hida level are deductions in PROOF.md. They are not advertised as new general representation theory or as claims already formalized in the external Lean files.

## Global periods

Beuzart-Plessis and Chaudouard, *The global Gan-Gross-Prasad conjecture for unitary groups II. From Eisenstein series to Bessel periods*, Forum of Mathematics, Pi 13 (2025), e16, [published primary article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/forum-of-mathematics-pi/article/global-gangrossprasad-conjecture-for-unitary-groups-ii-from-eisenstein-series-to-bessel-periods/2E9DFB9FC7A671B5530E47E3A37D1E7E), DOI 10.1017/fmp.2025.8.

Theorem 1.3.3.1 quantifies over the covered Hermitian forms and cuspidal constituents with a discrete Hermitian weak base-change parameter. Its vanishing contrapositive is used conditionally. Theorem 1.3.6.1 requires everywhere local temperedness and gives squared moduli for factorizable tests. Neither statement alone supplies a rank lower bound for the source's selected finite-dimensional period maps.

## The local-to-global bridge

This was reviewed separately from the finite tests. The seed's absolute
fold satisfies the same fold datum and multiplicity-integrability
hypothesis. Its L2 bound and Cauchy-Schwarz permit absolute unfolding
against any L2 row.

The unfolding then disintegrates through L=N(A)H(F), whose fiber is [N].
Absolute convergence is constant along the fibers. The absolute value of
the Fourier coefficient descends to L backslash G, so its ambient
almost-everywhere vanishing passes through the Haar quotient measure.
No arbitrary representative of an L2_loc class is evaluated on a
lower-dimensional orbit.

For equation (26), psi is explicitly a reference word character psi_*.
The established rational conjugacies identify the full Fourier kernels
for all words. The full PMat construction and one fixed SIso realization
are retained. If any of those realization or absolute-fold hypotheses is
not supplied, the implication for the actual P_u cannot be claimed.

The alternative use of PerLaw is limited to its bounded continuous RowFun
representatives and an actual representative/spanning bridge, with the
star convention in its first argument. Signed square integrability alone
does not meet the absolute-unfolding requirement.

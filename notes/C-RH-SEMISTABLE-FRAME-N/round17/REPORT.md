## 1. The result and its boundary

This round establishes a new common image-preserving algebra for the actual
uncut Poincare maps. It is generated by multiplication by finite determinant
characters and the generic finite Hecke corner at either one of the two level
primes. The two kinds of operators generally do not commute. Their exact
intertwiners are checked on the source's column laws, seed functions and fold,
not inferred from a resemblance between abstract matrices.

Under the stronger counting suppliers already used by the source, the
concentration excess is exponentially small on the range of **every orthogonal
projection in this algebra**. The error does not grow with the number of
determinant classes or Hecke labels. Source and target proportions in every
simple type of this algebra also agree to a uniformly exponentially small
relative error, including types of very small weight.

Every nonzero full word has a nonzero period image in every finite ambient
double class. In fact its relative kernel there is smaller than
`exp(-a f L)` for each fixed positive `a`, eventually under the stated strong
family suppliers. This is relative near-injectivity, not integer injectivity.

The remaining maximizing subspace lies in a **multiplicity space**, on the
other tensor factor from the algebra just controlled. An explicit tensor
construction satisfies the abstract new symmetries, exact mass balance,
constant projected kernel diagonals and the sharp pairwise frame bounds,
while retaining the old square-root concentration defect. Thus these new
conclusions do not by themselves prove the required all-subspace estimate.
This is a counterexample to an inference, not a realization of the
automorphic family or a refutation of RH.

## 3. Exact common action and support

Write

\[
 P_u:E_u\longrightarrow F,\qquad
 D=\dim F>0,\quad R=\sum_u\dim E_u>0,
\]

and let

\[
 \Phi_P(W)=\frac1R\sum_u\dim P_u^{-1}(W)-\frac{\dim W}{D},
 \qquad \epsilon(P)=\max_{W\le F}\Phi_P(W).
\]

Let `G=U(V)` and `T=U(1)`. For a finite unitary character of the norm-one
idele torus that is trivial on `T(F)` and on `det K2`, set

\[
 (M_\chi f)(g)=\chi(\det g_f)f(g),\qquad
 (S_{u,\chi}T_0)(h)=\chi(\det h_f)T_0(h).
\]

**Candidate-D.** The actual seed and fold give

\[
 \boxed{M_\chi P_u=\chi(\det x_u)P_uS_{u,\chi}.}       \tag{17.1}
\]

Indeed a seed point has finite part `n h x_u k`. The nilradical has
determinant one, the level factor has trivial character, and every rational
summation factor has trivial character. The left and right column covariance
laws are preserved. All multipliers are unitary. The target multiplier
commutes with the finite and infinite row projections, and therefore
preserves the actual full row space. Absorbing the displayed scalar into
the source action makes (17.1) an exact intertwiner of representations.

**Candidate-D.** The full finite double-class set is the finite abelian group

\[
 \mathcal C=G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_{F,f})/K_2
 \simeq T(\mathbb A_{F,f})/(T(F)\det K_2).                \tag{17.2}
\]

The determinant has a rational section on a nonisotropic Hermitian line.
The derived special unitary group is simply connected and almost simple,
and is noncompact at infinity in the source signature. Strong approximation
then proves injectivity in (17.2). In the actual self-dual, finite-unramified,
split-level setting, `det K2` is precisely the integral norm-one subgroup
with residue one at the selected level factors. The source audit also proves
period support in these classes directly from nilradical approximation and
the seed; this does not need a new interpretation of a cross-Gram bound.

Let `C=|mathcal C|`. **Candidate-D.** Every full word has exactly

\[
 \dim E_{u,c}=\frac{\dim E_u}{C}\qquad(c\in\mathcal C).  \tag{17.3}
\]

Haar measure on the compact smaller-group adelic quotient pushes uniformly
to the determinant classes. The original level has trivial rational
stabilizers, so all its class coordinates have equal Haar mass and identical
coefficient-fiber dimension. Arbitrary right translations need not normalize
the level for this measure argument.

## 4. Where noncommutativity enters

Let `p_alpha` be the generic central projectors of the finite Hecke corner
at one selected prime, with all `f` split factors retained. The other level
prime remains unchanged. **Candidate-D.** Conjugation by `M_chi` twists their
finite representation labels by `chi_v o det`, up to the harmless inverse
dictated by the right-action convention. Each projector and each multiplier
preserves every individual full period image.

The size of this permutation is explicit. Put

\[
 A=\prod_{v\mid g_0g_1}\mathbb F_{q_v}^{\times},\qquad
 H=\operatorname{red}(\mu(E)).
\]

Every character of `A/H` extends to a finite global norm-one character.
The only integral rational obstruction is the one simultaneous image of the
roots of unity. A fixed joint two-prime label must have unchanged scalar central
character, hence its stabilizer is killed by `k`. Joint two-prime label
orbits consequently have size divisible by

\[
 T_0=|(A/H)^k|=\frac{|A^k|}{|A^k\cap H|}
 \ \ge\ \frac1{|\mu(E)|}
             \prod_{v\mid g_0g_1}\frac{q_v-1}{\gcd(q_v-1,k)}. \tag{17.4}
\]

This is a genuine action on Hecke labels, while every word image remains
invariant. It is not the missing order-five action permuting words in the
reviewer's R15 discussion. No such word action is asserted.

Choose one level prime for the counting argument and write `mathscr A` for
the finite-dimensional star algebra generated by its projectors and **all**
determinant multipliers. With `X=widehat(mathcal C)`, it is a represented
quotient of the ordinary crossed product of the label functions by `X`.
For an orbit `O`, let `H_alpha` be the label stabilizer in `X`. Since `X`
is abelian, the simple types are indexed by `beta=(O,theta)` with
`theta` a character of `H_alpha`, and their matrix size is `s_beta=|O|`.

**Candidate-D.** On the same abstract algebra for source and target,

\[
 F=\bigoplus_\beta \mathbb C^{s_\beta}\otimes M_\beta,
 \qquad
 E_u=\bigoplus_\beta \mathbb C^{s_\beta}\otimes N_{u,\beta},
 \qquad
 P_u=\bigoplus_\beta I_{s_\beta}\otimes p_{u,\beta}.     \tag{17.5}
\]

Zero target multiplicities are initially allowed. Matrix units can also be
constructed directly: choose unitary twists from a base label to each orbit
label and use `U_alpha p_base U_beta^*`. No coherent selection of extensions
of local characters is needed for that construction. The global character
group itself acts coherently in the crossed-product description.

## 5. Exponential control on the actual algebra

Put `r=k+m-1`, the ambient group rank, and keep `D` for the dimension of the
row space. Under the refined geometric counting suppliers, the diagonal
projection kernel `K` satisfies the following stronger fact than a scalar
dimension estimate:

\[
 \int |K(x)-k_0|\,d\mu(x)
 \le B_0:=3e^{-c_1rf\log r}D_*+2e^{-r^2f}D,
 \qquad k_0\mu(\text{quotient})=D_*.                    \tag{17.6}
\]

**Candidate-D.** This follows by retaining the absolute-integral estimate
inside `moderate_of_near_far`, then adding the omitted constant mass and
the positive deep-region kernel. The constants `3` and `2` use the source's
specialization `A2=1`. It is a deduction from the cited proof and premises,
not a new Lean theorem. The proof and exact source lines are in OPPOSITION.md.

The same bound applies to each admissible one-prime replacement, with
`D_*` and `D` replaced by its expected and actual dimensions. Partitioning
its integral by determinant classes, or by stabilizer-character classes,
and summing the replacements costs **no number-of-classes factor**.
The regular column model gives the matching source proportions exactly.

Let `w_beta` be those common source proportions. Explicitly, for
`beta=(O,theta)` and a representative `alpha` of `O`,
`w_beta=|O| a_alpha/|H_alpha|`, where `a_alpha` is its round 8 regular
generic-corner weight. Put

\[
 q_\beta=\frac{s_\beta\dim M_\beta}{D},\quad
 b_\beta=\frac{s_\beta\sum_u\operatorname{rank}p_{u,\beta}}R,
 \quad \kappa=1-\sum_\beta b_\beta.
\]

**Candidate-D.** One obtains

\[
 \operatorname{TV}(w,q)\le B_0/D,
\]

and the exact restricted maximum

\[
 \boxed{
 \max_{Q=Q^*=Q^2\in\mathscr A}\Phi_P(\operatorname{ran}Q)
 =\kappa+\sum_\beta(b_\beta-q_\beta)_+
 \le\kappa+B_0/D.}                                    \tag{17.7}
\]

In a simple matrix block a projection has the same fractional rank on every
copy of the irreducible representation in source, kernel and target. Its
contribution is linear in that fractional rank. The maximum is therefore
attained by selecting whole simple types, which proves (17.7). This includes
noncentral projections and arbitrary directions in the matrix factors.

The bound is `O(exp(-x^(eps/8)))` under the source family suppliers and their
fixed constants. The exponential number of labels is not multiplied into
it. This quantitative conclusion still uses the source's analytic kernel
estimates; the new algebra organizes and extends their scope.

There is also a uniform **relative** assertion. If
`b1=3 exp(-c1 r f log r)` and `b2=2 exp(-r^2 f)<1`, then every positive-weight
stabilizer sector has relative count error at most

\[
 |\mathcal C|\frac{b_1+b_2}{1-b_2}.                     \tag{17.8}
\]

The source weight cancels before this estimate. Since
`log|mathcal C|=O(fL)` and `k log k/L` tends to infinity, (17.8) is still
uniformly exponential. Thus every simple type of positive source weight
occurs in the target for sufficiently large parameters. This says nothing
by itself about nonvanishing of every automorphic constituent or every
period in that simple type.

## 6. Actual nonvanishing in every ambient component

The sharper diagonal estimate before the `LinkS` weakening is

\[
 \|A_u^*A_u-I\|_{HS}^2\le e_F\dim E_u,
 \qquad e_F=2C_0/\Gamma+2e^{-kf\log k}.
\]

At the two level primes, the smaller scalar lifts shift determinant classes
by `k`th powers and intertwine every word. If `B` is the image of the integral
residue units in `mathcal C`, these shifts give

\[
 \frac{\dim\ker(P_u|_{E_{u,c}})}{\dim E_{u,c}}
 \le e_F[\mathcal C:B^k].                              \tag{17.9}
\]

**Candidate-D.** The norm-one class number is at most `2^f h_E`, and
`[mathcal C:B^k]` is at most that number times `k^(2f)`.
The actual parameter bounds give `log h_E=O(fL)`, `log k=O(L)`,
`m` tending uniformly to infinity, and
`log Gamma=CGam m^5 f L`. Consequently the right side of (17.9) is at most
`exp(-a fL)` for every fixed `a>0`, eventually. Every nonzero full word is
therefore visible in **every** ambient finite component. The separate
integer condition for zero kernel, `e_F dim E_u/|B^k|<1`, has not been proved.

## 7. The exact remaining obligation

The largest maximizing subspace is invariant under every operator in the
common image-preserving star algebra `mathscr A`, including its adjoints.
In (17.5) it therefore has the form

\[
 W=\bigoplus_\beta\mathbb C^{s_\beta}\otimes W_\beta,
 \qquad W_\beta\le M_\beta.                            \tag{17.10}
\]

Its orthogonal projection belongs to the **commutant** of `mathscr A`.
Within one simple block, (17.7) controls projections `Q tensor I_M`, whereas
(17.10) uses `I_s tensor P_W`. Their common projections are only zero and
identity. Substituting one for the other would be the missing, invalid step.

Let `e_beta` be the normalized reduced-image defect of the actual maps
`p_{u,beta}` on `M_beta`, using their total image rank in the source
normalization. Terms with zero image rank carry zero weight. The earlier
exact block budget gives

\[
 \epsilon(P)\le
 \kappa+\operatorname{TV}(w,q)
 +\underbrace{\sum_\beta\min(q_\beta,b_\beta)e_\beta}
                 _{E_{\rm mult}}.                     \tag{17.11}
\]

The first two terms are now exponentially controlled for this actual
noncommutative algebra. **O:** prove a sufficiently small bound on
`E_mult` for the actual `p_{u,beta}`, uniformly on every subspace of each
`M_beta`. Local or blockwise nonvanishing, exact balance, and automorphic
multiplicity one do not give that lower rank bound.

For the source/target cut to the arithmetic `B_k`, let `alpha>0` be the
retained source fraction, retain positive target dimension, and put
`a=max(source loss,target loss)`. The sharp round 15
comparison gives only

\[
 \epsilon(B_k)\le
 \frac{\kappa+\operatorname{TV}(w,q)+E_{\rm mult}+a}{\alpha}. \tag{17.12}
\]

It does not turn the restricted statement (17.7) into an all-subspace
statement after a noncommuting cut.

additional scientific computation was needed for this symbolic deduction.

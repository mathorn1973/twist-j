The source representation of #1804 already handles a whole finite word
on its fixed system: Gamma(V_q)...Gamma(V_1)=Gamma(V_q...V_1). The
one-shot controller there does not autonomously execute that word; at
twice its nominated time it executes a free phase instead. Here a new
finite stage program supplies the missing autonomous schedule, while
the whole-word identity prevents an artificial factor N in source error.

The active archive is a new carrier component. Its projectors are part
of every complete energy eigenspace, and every writer is a full unitary.
It is not introduced as an uncounted passive reference. The new joint
target is a coherent sequential record, with old information retained.

The zero-energy archive assignment concerns E_M in this note. It does
not give those labels the original full-step branch energy Q of #1774
or make a zero-energy dictionary into the original spatial state. The
stationary thermal preparation boundary remains: stationary inputs and
conserving operations do not supply the independent coherent beta_M.

The energy account and time generator remain explicit distinct operators.
Both are conserved by the new H. Simply adding E_M to that generator
would change its target by a free phase. Conversely merely declaring
H=E_M would remove the displayed clock and record interactions. Neither
identification is made by this proof.

This is a concrete common finite contract and construction with a
specified retained-record window. It does not claim that the original
three apparatuses already were one apparatus, or that their remaining
physical obligations disappear after composition.

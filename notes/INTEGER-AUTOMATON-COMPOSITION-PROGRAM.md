# Composition program for declared integer automata

**NON-CANONICAL / construction plan.** This note schedules work; it creates
no claim, status, gate, probability law or permission for a formal run. The
existing 25 H/O owners and their full decision clauses remain unchanged.
The native record and the declared-automaton theorems in Canon section 2
are different mathematical results. This program does not derive the latter
automaton from native U.

## One reference law

Keep the current research entrypoint `field_coupled_step` and its exact
inverse as one reference law. A reference manifest must bind the complete
state schema, every imported update module, gate order, inverse, matter/field/
contact/radiation energy, charge, boundary conditions and preparation code.
Record any change to these as a new law. A different initial configuration
under identical bytes is a new preparation. A passive reader never supplies
a missing transition, energy payment or saved record.

The reaction path R18 <-> A20 <-> P14 is selected architecture: activation
costs two and the next reaction releases six. Its quadratic energy does not
by itself exclude a direct 18 -> 14 channel. The one-triangle field shell
3 -> 1 can pay the activation; its six admitted phases all enable that gate.
The triangular norm-three identity is independent of any proposed barrier
25 from fifth-root ramification. Existing trajectory records remain research
evidence, not new canonical computational claims in this fold.

Maintain separate construction and derivation accounts. The construction
account asks what a fixed closed law can do. The derivation account lists
which coordinates, carriers, gates and readings are selected by TWIST-J
and which are added choices. Success in the first account does not settle
the second.

## Complete the present finite classification

The proposed radius-two input family has 666 routing representatives, eight
high-carrier phases/polarities and 21*20 distinct ordered port placements:
666*8*21*20 = 2,237,760 preparations. This is a target inventory, not a claim
that its complete classification has run. The reduction concerns routing
before activation; representatives need not have equal energies or products.
Its admission and completeness proof must be frozen before a formal census.

Build one functional graph of exact reduced states. Each state has a unique
nonterminal successor or a first terminal outcome: activation, exit of any
part of the complete state from the declared region, or a competing reaction.
Memoize exact states rather than treating each preparation as an unrelated
trajectory. Mark cycles as permanent nonactivation only while the reduced
sector hypotheses hold; an exit or competing reaction is not such a cycle.
Propagate outcome and distance backward through predecessor trees. Output
all preparation-to-outcome classes, maximum times within each class, cycle
lengths and delaying/routing mechanisms. Do not infer a preparation measure
from a search biased toward long waits.

Verify representatives under the full frozen law, including phase order,
field, flux memories and radiation, then audit products separately. Distinct
energetic representatives of the same routing class require their own product
checks. The finite quotient theorem bounds a first event under its frozen
sector assumptions; it supplies neither this census nor a decay distribution.

## Two cells and two interacting systems

The first field-extension test is two triangles sharing an edge. Directly
reusing the current field energy loses positive definiteness: the Canon
zero mode has zero divergence and survives Gauss admission. A candidate
replacement must declare an exactly invertible whole step, preserve Gauss
and a positive coercive energy, and state the complete macrostep dependency
radius. Locality of each gate alone does not give a one-edge macrostep bound.

Then prepare two separated systems under one unchanged law. Require source
emission, propagation, actual receiver absorption, a receiver state change
and persistent local readback. Include the energy and record cost of
preparation and rearming explicitly. Numerical longevity alone is not an
all-time certificate; a certificate must cover the complete factor and all
internal substeps, with permanently separated future support envelopes.

## Behavioral particle and record contract

For each proposed particle-like structure declare its preparation class,
full local state, persistence certificate, allowed perturbations, transport
or scattering experiment and local capture/record interface. Exact periodic
orbits establish persistence on those trajectories; robustness requires a
separate quantified perturbation class. A pair of distinguishable charged
ports in a neutral local factor is not yet a pair of independently escaping
charged objects. Zero field enforces zero charge density node by node, and
the bridge-flux energy bound limits separation at fixed finite energy in
the present geometry and boundary conditions.

A useful composition milestone is a traveling structure that changes a local
receiver into a permanent distinguishable state under the same law. The
preparation, event, archive and restoration of readiness must then share a
single state/update contract. This still does not close QDD-INSTRUMENT-APPARATUS
or QDD-TERMINAL-EVENT-SEMANTICS without their complete physical conditions.
Radiation energy cells alone do not close PHOTON-MASSLESS-PHASE.

## Scaling with a declared resource

Seek a defined preparation family with T(r) tending to infinity under one
law, declaring what grows: support, carrier count, energy or actual memory.
Separate travel distance from delay after first interaction. Merely enlarging
cold neutral coefficients within the finite four-phase routing sector does
not create unbounded internal arithmetic history at fixed ports and carriers.
Only after such a family is established should a specified preparation
measure be used to ask about a lifetime distribution. A longest found wait
and an exponential decay law are separate claims.

An object is a triple (C,f,g) with f:X->C surjective, g:C->MatterData_QDD, and D=g o f. A morphism h:(C,f,g)->(C',f',g') satisfies f'=h o f and g=g' o h.

    D(x)=D(y) iff q(x)=q(y).

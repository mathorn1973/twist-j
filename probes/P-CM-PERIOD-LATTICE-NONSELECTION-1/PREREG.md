### S1. Integral period subgroup

For every integral alternating form,

```text
Per_Z(Omega)
 = gcd(w01,w02,w03,w12,w13,w23) Z.
```

Proof. The six basis bivectors evaluate to the six displayed coefficients, so
the image subgroup is generated by those integers. Bezout gives the positive
gcd as an attained value.

### S2. CM primitive period

For the public CM pencil,

```text
Per_Z(Omega_(a,b))=gcd(a,b) Z.
```

The six coefficients are `(a,b,-b,a,b,a)`. If `d` divides `a,b`, then `d^2`
divides `q(a,b)`. Hence on the unimodular locus `q(a,b)=+-1` one has
`gcd(a,b)=1` and therefore

```text
Per_Z(Omega_(a,b))=Z.
```

The positive primitive generator is exactly one for every integral
unimodular/Pell form.

### S3. Pullback invariance

For every `A in GL_4(Z)`,

```text
Per_Z(A^* Omega)=Per_Z(Omega).
```

Proof. `Lambda^2 A` is an automorphism of the lattice `Lambda^2 L`, so

```text
(A^* Omega)(Lambda^2 L)
 = Omega((Lambda^2 A)(Lambda^2 L))
 = Omega(Lambda^2 L).
```

Consequently the public Pell shift and the public J-pullback preserve the
primitive period subgroup. In particular one J-pullback sends the subgroup
`Z` of a unimodular form to the same subgroup `Z`. No multiplier `phi^-2`
occurs in this L1 primitive-period invariant.

### S4. Unit-period cell nonselection from Omega_1 alone

Let the matrix of `Omega_1` be the public alternating matrix. For
`v=e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3`, define the integral symplectic transvection

```text
T_v(x)=x+Omega_1(x,v)v.
```

Each `T_v` lies in `Sp(Omega_1,Z)`. On `Lambda^2 L`, the common fixed
sublattice of the four displayed transvections is exactly

```text
Z Pi,
Pi=(1,0,1,0,0,1).
```

The generator is primitive and

```text
Omega_1(Pi)=2.
```

Therefore no bivector `C` with `Omega_1(C)=1` is fixed by this four-element
family. A selector that depends only on the unmarked form and is natural under
all integral symplectic automorphisms would have to return a fixed bivector
when the input form is fixed. Such a selector cannot return a unit-period
bivector.

This is nonselection of an individual lattice cell. It does not deny the
canonical image subgroup `Per_Z(Omega_1)=Z`.

### S5. Root-of-unity full-pencil control

Multiplication by `zeta_5` fixes every form in the public CM pencil by the
registered root-of-unity kernel. Its exterior-square fixed rational space is

```text
C(a,b)=(b,a,b,a,a,b),  a,b in Q.
```

For such a bivector,

```text
Pf_biv(C(a,b))
 = b^2-a^2+ab
 = -(a^2-ab-b^2).
```

In four dimensions a rational bivector is decomposable exactly when its
bivector Pfaffian vanishes. The equation

```text
a^2-ab-b^2=0
```

has no nonzero rational solution. Indeed, after clearing denominators and
dividing by the gcd, a nonzero coprime integer solution would satisfy
`a|b^2` and `b|a^2`, forcing `|a|=|b|=1`; the four sign pairs are not zeros.
Cases with `a=0` or `b=0` are immediate.

Hence the only decomposable `zeta_5`-fixed rational bivector is zero. Thus a
selector of one nonzero decomposable rank-two cell that is natural under the
full root-of-unity kernel cannot be obtained from the CM pencil alone.

This clause does not exclude invariant nondecomposable algebraic two-classes,
orbits of cells, or a later selector using additional typed structure.

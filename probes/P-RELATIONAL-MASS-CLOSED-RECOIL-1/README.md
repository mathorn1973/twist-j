This separate probe extends accepted stable binding to a conditional closed
recoil comparison. It derives a finite-clock operational mass ratio for
two stable composites, a free covariant center reading, and a precise
nonuniqueness theorem for physical law selection from unrestricted native
counter readings. It does not claim physical mass has been derived from U.

common boost law. A competing law agrees on the complete h=10 free
calibration but predicts weak ratio 1. This is the explicit discriminator.

Run from the repository root:

    python3 probes/P-RELATIONAL-MASS-CLOSED-RECOIL-1/verify.py

The exact stdout must match EXPECTED.txt, with exit 0 and empty stderr.
The output labels the reciprocal ratio A/B=2/9. PREREG.md, PROOF.md,
SOURCE.md, FIXTURES.md, verify.py and FROZEN.sha256 were publicly pinned
and read back before the first execution. RUN.md records that execution;
RESULT.md and REVIEW.md delimit its meaning.

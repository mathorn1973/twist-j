# The delayed two-edge receiver

**Candidate C, finite native H3 computation, L1.** A natural source contact changed a receiver two horizontal spacings away in one of 170 matched emission trials. The first difference was ten ticks after emission. The actual incoming edge refused a transfer that the comparison admitted. The radiation difference survived the next complete tick unchanged. Neither arm wrote matter at that receiver. [Preregistration](PREREG.md) · [Exact records](EXPECTED.txt) · [Replay](RUN.md).

## All natural emissions

Each distance uses the same 170 emissions, drawn from the first 128 ticks of all 85 starts. Seventeen starts emitted and 16 emitted repeatedly. There were 94 westward and 76 eastward launches. Sources followed the original trajectory without interruption. In a separate comparison for each emission, only that source B2 was undone, after which the whole network evolved natively in both arms.

All 30 event-distance arrivals were isolated first local differences. The first differing rail was CORR after C. At every such last edge the gap was nonzero in both arms. The actual arm refused transport, the comparison arm transported, and the signed contact phase was 0 minus +2 fifth-turns, or −4π/5. All arrivals came from the westward launch cohort and approached in the original travel direction. Per-direction yields were 29/94 west and 0/76 east at distance 1, and 1/94 west and 0/76 east at distance 2. No first arrival in this sample transmitted a nonzero actual CORR movement into the observed recipient. A signal here means a recipient's dependence on a source event under the registered comparison.

The 29 one-edge arrivals comprise four at age 1 and 25 delayed. Their ages and counts were 1 (4), 3 (1), 11 (3), 31 (3), 35 (1), 41 (4), 51 (4), 61 (9). Every actual arrival B selected B2 locally and the comparison was idle. All 29 material and radiation contrasts survived the next C and B. None retained the original actual material write, the ordered matter pair, or the same signed CORR difference. Several single-source comparisons locate the same live receiver transition. Grouping by (rank, absolute recipient tick, site) gives **13 distinct live writes on nine ranks** for those 29 attributions. For example, for raw 0 the separate removals at source ticks 11, 21, 31, 41, 61 and 71 each change the receiver at absolute tick 72. This multiplicity rules out reading 29 as a count of separate delivered pulses on the live circuit.

## Exact distance-two witness

On raw 0, the B2 at A=(1,3) on tick 81 first affects (0,3) at 82 C. At (7,3), two spacings west round the torus, the live and comparison cells agree through 90 B. At 91 C the donor is (0,3). Its actual CORR value (8,3,4,5) and the zero recipient have different parity. The comparison donor (6,2,2,4) and its zero recipient have equal parity. Thus the actual recipient remains zero and the comparison becomes (2,2,−1,2) in the (1,j,j²,j³) basis. The entire ordered field pair (CLK,CORR) from the arrival is retained at both 92 C and 92 B. At 91 B both tags are zero and all four observed matter values remain zero.

Here the matter half-gap u at 91 C is zero in both arms. B1 requires u≠0. For B2 a nonzero field half-gap v would require h(u)=h(J)h(v)>0 in the chosen embedding, which fails at u=0. A nonintegral gap also leaves the cell idle. This explains why that radiation contrast alone cannot produce a local matter write on this cut. The evidence identifies the last edge and the first local change at each observed site. Whether the 82 B material write is necessary for the 91 C result remains open. Other intervening contacts can matter.

## Signed phase and scope

Here −4π/5 is the actual-minus-comparison angular change of the **ordered incoming edge gap at one contact**. The earlier [fixed-port experiment](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/pull/2016) used a separate observable over 64 ticks and found both signs of the net angle at the same separation. These two observables answer different questions.

Delayed observation added 25 one-edge responses and a two-edge response. These witnesses reject the preregistered no-additional-arrivals and one-edge-only propositions on this cohort. All 29 one-edge responses reject universal exact retention of the material write. Distances three and four delivered no detectable change on any of the four recipient rails within 64 ticks per event. The finite samples and the asymmetric background leave long-time rates and any distance law open.

A focused follow-up would fund a nonzero matter pair at (7,3) through redistribution of existing stock and use an equal-account comparison. Register the stock and guards before replay, then require an actual local B write caused by the two-edge signal, the resulting word held through both next cuts, and full-account and inverse checks on all 85 starts. A missing write or immediate reversal would fail that test.

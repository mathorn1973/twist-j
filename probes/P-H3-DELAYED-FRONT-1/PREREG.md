## Tests and limits

Test the propositions that delayed observation adds no arrivals beyond the first cone, that a natural event cannot reach beyond one edge in the window, and that every isolated material response retains its literal word through both next cuts. A single counterexample refutes the corresponding proposition on this fixture. Check how frequently the first recipient difference is a literal CORR movement rather than a suppressed movement and whether the same distance admits both signs of the relative angular increment. No monotonicity or quantitative distance law is assumed.

Compare the early subset and the complete rank-to-emission list with the preceding transcript. Independently compare the integer Python and C++ event/length projections, including nulls, matching directions, tags, local phase and persistence at C and B. Any mismatched bit, account, inverse or projected result invalidates the associated evidence. Output includes the individual rows so that conditioned counts can be reconstructed from raw witnesses. The circuit has unequal local backgrounds, recurrent contacts, finite size and fixed observation horizons; the spatial sequence is not a sample from a uniform medium.

## Prior information and hypotheses

During design a pre-pin exploratory run used raw 0 only and recipes 0 through 247. At the first local difference following a westward source B2, it observed six isolated westward incoming contacts and no actual matter write. The six came from baseline emission 81 and five mode-42 emissions, all without a receiver B. The complete original raw-0 trajectory and the unfunded 91 C stock across ranks also informed the design. These observations are outside prospective evidence. The present census includes the disclosed input, all other 84 inputs, arrivals in either orientation, later direct material conversions, and additional bank and preimage families. No positive prior result selects a recipe. Record a null outcome in each family when it occurs.

The primary question is existence: at a fixed receiver two horizontal spacings west of the source, does deleting just one natural source event change the incoming CORR and cause an actual material write whose literal ordered matter pair survives both cuts of the following tick? The stronger endpoint requires that work coincide with the first isolated local difference after a westward emission. Stronger still, the seed preserves the six raw clock words. Test these endpoints separately for each construction family. A changed word or lost contrast at either next cut falsifies retention for that work. An all-zero census for some endpoint bounds the result to these stocks, ranks, emissions and windows.

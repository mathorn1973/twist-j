# PREREG: P-NATIVE-LINEAR-JHODGE-STEP-CLASS-1

FORMAL PUBLIC PROBE. Action layer L1. Issue #1065.
Base: Public Canon v89 main c85497cfc5dd60dcfc6b84d3e3086c361ffe0e35.

Let Gamma_lin be the complete subgroup of GL_6(F_5) generated by the exact
linear parts A_n,B_n,C_n,D_n,E_n of the five declared checkpoint generators.
It is rebuilt by breadth-first closure from the identity under right
multiplication by generators a,b,c,d,e, with no word-length cutoff.

On the marked integral Hodge carrier let C_4 be the A4 coordinate five-cycle,
M_h=I+C_4^2, and L_h=Lambda^2(M_h) mod 5. This is the reduction of the
actual fixed-J Hodge step, not the auxiliary cycle Lambda^2 C_4.

Frozen decisions:

G1 Construct L_h exactly, verify invertibility, compute its exact order, and
compute r_L(k)=rank((L_h+I)^k) for k=1,...,6. Verify (L_h+I)^6=0. The
resulting rank sequence is derived by the pinned verifier and is not supplied
as an expected constant.

G2 Re-enumerate Gamma_lin completely and verify closure and inverse membership.
For every g with (g+I)^6=0, compute the same six-term rank sequence. Since
these matrices are (-1)-unipotent over F_5, equality of the full rank
sequences is equivalent to equality of Jordan block partitions and hence
GL_6(F_5)-similarity.

G3 STEP-SEAM iff at least one native word linear part is similar to L_h.
Otherwise STEP-DIFFER. Print the exact candidate count and a stable shortest
BFS witness if positive. Print every realized native (-1)-unipotent type,
count and shortest word in either case.

G4 As a control only, build A_h=Lambda^2 C_4 and compare the rank sequence of
(A_h-I)^k with that of (L_h+I)^k. This comparison is not a proof premise.

G5 Check the named natural order-ten controls -B_n C_n and -C_n B_n.

G6 Enumerate the fired linear subgroup <B_n,D_n,E_n> and report any direct
L_h-class candidate there separately.

Scientific outcomes STEP-SEAM and STEP-DIFFER are both accepted. A negative
result excludes every direct bridge in which one finite native WORD LINEAR
PART is conjugate to the mod-five reduction of the actual fixed-J Hodge step.
It does not exclude affine, nonlinear, history-dependent, time-dependent
gauge or counter-assisted bridges.

verify.py uses only exact standard-library F_5 arithmetic. No floating point,
randomness, external package, network input, sampling or word cutoff is
admitted. Runtime/integrity failure is STOP.

No physical dimension/time, observer, decoder, occurrence law, selector
equivalence, measure, SI, Canon promotion or L2-L6 lift is claimed. The author

# Proof: minimal rational closure of the fixed-J Hodge chart

K is rational integral and K^2=5I. Therefore over F=Q(sqrt5), sigma exchanges P_+ and P_-. Since L is rational, sigma(E_+)=E_-.

The exact matrix certificates give dim E_+=dim E_-=4 and dim(E_++E_-)=6, hence dim(E_+ intersect E_-)=2. T is rational, has rank 2, and im(T)_F is contained in both charts. Therefore
E_+ intersect E_-=im(T)_F.

If v is rational and v in E_+, then sigma(v)=v lies in E_-, so v lies in im(T)_F. Since T is rational, v lies in im(T)_Q. Conversely im(T)_Q lies in E_+. Hence E_+ intersect W_Q=im(T)_Q.

R=I-T is complementary to T. The verifier gives dim(E_+ intersect im(R)_F)=2. Any rational vector there would also lie in im(T)_Q, hence is zero. Thus the visible periodic plane is genuinely Q(sqrt5)-defined.

Let U be a Q-subspace with E_+ subset U_F. Because U_F is Galois-stable it also contains E_-. Therefore U_F contains E_++E_-=W_F, so U=W_Q and dim_Q U=6. This is stronger than requiring U to be L-invariant.

Finally Lambda^2 A4 is a free rank-6 Z-lattice. M is integral unimodular, so L=Lambda^2 M preserves it. This supplies the upper bound, proving minimal integral rank 6 in the preregistered sense.

Reducing the same integral K modulo 5 gives Kbar^2=0 and rank 3, the already accepted A3 ramified consistency. No native checkpoint identification follows.

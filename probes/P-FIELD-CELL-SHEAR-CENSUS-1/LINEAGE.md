   order `(a,b,c,d)`. The plus-sign case is related by
   `diag(1,-1,1,-1)`. Neither sign convention changes a physical model.
2. Reversing a shared edge changes both incidences in its row and leaves
   its Gram product unchanged. Reversing an entire face gives the

   accepted as an oriented cell.

from the unchanged F source response. On every common-ready branch,
(a,b)=(alpha_A z_A,alpha_B z_B). Equality is signed rational equality.

source is returned as None, not as an accepted zero source. For unknown A
acceptance, nonzero b0 implies acceptance, while b0=0 remains unknown.
With no acceptance/context at all, the zero record returns alpha_A=alpha_B=None
and z_A=z_B=None. It is not silently marked accepted.

With the retained d, z_B=eta(d). If z_B=0 the B cost is zero and the ready
contact accepts; otherwise r=0 or r=R0 z_B identifies refusal/acceptance.

when d is present; alpha_A still needs its sign at a zero record. If a sign
and d are both provided they must agree. No claim of maximally informative
reading is made outside these expressly specified contexts.

NON-CANONICAL / conditional L1 proof. A. M. Thorn.
Scope frozen before the new program runs; numerical source targets exposed.

## 1. Why a raw tuple cannot certify its intended origin

Let x=(1,0,0,0) be the same Python tuple supplied to a function in two calls.
One caller describes x as four source residues; the other as a rational port
vector. With the same other arguments and execution state, a deterministic
value/type predicate returns the same answer. It cannot accept the second
and reject the first from their unrecorded descriptions. Restricting all
rational integer-valued vectors would alter the numerical carrier, not solve
origin detection. The new contract distinguishes object TYPES. A tagged
record still does not authenticate acquisition: an external caller may
construct another record with identical fields. No security or native-gate
conclusion follows from the tag.

There is no c0/c1 term. No original native head, wave coordinate or energy
appears. The new wrapper evaluates just these formulas and their declared

finite audit supplements rather than replaces this direct dependence proof.
The chronological prefix reader is separately the literal slot schedule in
SOURCE_PROOF, not an inferred reconstruction from initial preparations.

## 3. Partial information, zero and refusal

On first-period endpoints the initially positive contact sign flips exactly
on acceptance, so alpha=(1-sigma)/2. If alpha=1 the above product recovers
z, including z=0. If alpha=0 the product is zero for every refused source;
its value cannot identify that source and the reader returns None.
Without a sign, nonzero b0 forces alpha_A=1; at zero b0 both accepted zero
and refused nonzero sources occur in the declared family, e.g. at capacity
1/5, h_A=0 versus h_A=3 with the same zero B source. Their classical
records agree. Therefore an unknown zero cannot be assigned acceptance.

For B, retained d gives z_B=eta(d) independently of acceptance. With r=b2-D2 a,
the family equation is r=alpha_B R0 z_B. If z_B is nonzero, invertibility of
R0 distinguishes r=0 from r=R0 z_B. If z_B=0, the exact contact increment is
zero; the store stays within its carrier and the contact accepts. Hence
omitting sigma_B while retaining d leaves this specified reading unchanged.
This does not remove the B sign from the reversible state transition.
Contradictory records are rejected where the declared equations test them;
record-local checks are not a recognizer for the entire physical/state history.

Static AST comparison in MIGRATION.md finds 70 preexisting top-level
engine functions/classes unchanged. The changed original nodes are only

## 5. What is and is not closed

SOURCE_PROOF already supplies the conditional universal algebra. The new
proof resolves the record-access and unknown-context ambiguity, not a new
physical mechanism. The executable audit must still pass; no prose proof
predeclares its result. A local/CI replay does not confirm physical adequacy,
native compilation, finite-bit rational storage, unlimited reuse, reset or
one-outcome occurrence. Those facts cannot be obtained by renaming a wrapper.

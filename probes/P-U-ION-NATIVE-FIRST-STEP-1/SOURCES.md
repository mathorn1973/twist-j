## S2. Dependence on independently fixed optical controls

B. C. Sawyer and K. R. Brown, *A Wavelength-Insensitive, Multispecies
Entangling Gate for Group-2 Atomic Ions*, Physical Review A **103**, 022427
(2021). DOI [10.1103/PhysRevA.103.022427](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.103.022427).
Read [arXiv:2010.04526v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.04526v1),
9 October 2020. This theoretical source is cited by S1.

Appendix A, Eqs. (7)-(10), expresses Stark shifts through intensity,
polarization, atomic matrix elements and optical frequencies. Appendix
C.2, Eqs. (36)-(39), separates stationary shifts and the travelling-wave
amplitude with its geometric-mean dependence. These support the common
scalar-intensity law, with fixed level coefficients. Appendix C.3,
Eqs. (44)-(49), gives the effective force approximations and closed-loop
phase. The discussion after Eq. (52) distinguishes closure of the selected
mode from spectator-mode closure. These equations justify neither an
arbitrary independently assignable spatial coupling vector nor an error
bound for the new word.

## S3. Ringbauer: individual carrier addressing and its limits

M. Ringbauer et al., *A universal qudit quantum processor with trapped
ions*, Nature Physics **18**, 1053-1057 (2022).
DOI [10.1038/s41567-022-01658-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01658-0).
Read [arXiv:2109.06903v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06903v1),
14 September 2021, especially Supplement SI and SII.

The `40Ca+` processor uses resolved 729 nm transitions between S and D
Zeeman levels. Supplement SI describes focused-beam individual addressing
with crossed AODs and controllable beam phases; reconfiguration is finite.
It discusses crosstalk and demonstrates addressed entanglement in three-
and ten-ion strings. Its entangling operation is MS, not the LS force used
here. The source supports the physical individual-addressing principle;
the present ideal carrier matrices on all seventeen ions remain explicit
model premises. The optical addressing-range discussion is not a measured
seventeen-ion fidelity certificate. Supplement SII treats level-dependent
spectator Stark shifts and phase compensation; these do not disappear
automatically when LS phases are refocused. No MS gate, arbitrary local
unitary or reported crosstalk number is imported as a primitive or error
bound for this construction.

## S4. Many-ion/multimode force and phase accounting

P. A. Kamenskikh et al., *Analysis of the action of conventional trapped-ion
entangling gates in qudit space*,
[arXiv:2602.21886v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21886v1), 25 February 2026.
DOI [10.48550/arXiv.2602.21886](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.21886).

Section II.2, Eqs. (14)-(21), separates diagonal AC shifts, the linear
sum of mode/ion/level forces, and local versus entangling phases. It also
describes fixed ratios of level-dependent force amplitudes. Appendix A,
Eqs. (50)-(51), exposes the state- and mode-dependent residual displacement.
This supports the form of the enlarged effective force and the requirement
to control every significantly driven mode. Selecting only one harmonic
COM mode is an adopted truncation, not a multimode error certificate. The
paper is a theoretical preprint and did not test the present device or
projective refocusing word. Its separate control proposals are not imported.

## S5. Scope check: Meth's alternative encoding and gate

M. Meth et al., *Simulating 2D lattice gauge theories on a qudit quantum
computer*, [arXiv:2310.12110v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.12110v2),
29 April 2024. DOI [10.48550/arXiv.2310.12110](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2310.12110).

Appendix A documents individual optical addressing and a sideband
controlled-rotation implementation. For dimensions above two, its code
uses D levels and auxiliary S states, differing from the present code.
Its gate is not imported into MODEL.md. Appendix G.1 discusses spectator
protection and buffer ions; the disjoint-level hiding used for qutrits
does not directly extend to ququints. The reported D-state lifetime and
finite coherence illustrate why a long ideal word is not automatically
a practical device. None of that apparatus's measured error rates is
assigned to the current seventeen-ion sequence.

## Derived construction versus physical realization

The 500-loop projective refocusing identity, its scalar treatment of all
spectators, the controlled native-step synthesis, pulse bounds and symbolic
energy table are this probe's mathematical work. Selecting `lambda_e` to
produce a prescribed phase is control synthesis, not an independently
predicted occurrence of that phase in nature. The spectral profile and
geometry must be determined independently of the native table.

The combined seventeen-ion geometry, six nonzero pair coefficients,
admissible intensity/rate bounds, powers, switching time, preparation,
inherited mode distribution and accumulated physical error remain
uncertified numerical device inputs. A finite incident optical-energy
expression is not a construction of a finite quantum work reservoir.
Neither the source combination nor an exact verifier closes those inputs
by adoption or derives this architecture from TWIST-J.

## Native law and common inputs

All 85 ranks are identified by their exact ready state. Start one tick before each ready state with bit 1. All 32 cells on the periodic 8 by 4 lattice carry four elements (R0,R2,CLK,CORR) of Z[j], j=exp(2πi/5). At bit b, C pairs the first three rails vertically and CORR horizontally on the matching starting at parity b. On a same-parity pair it keeps the midpoint m and rotates the half-difference u to ρu with ρ=j². Other pairs stay fixed. B acts locally on the integral matter and radiation half-gaps (u,v), preserving both midpoints. B1 sends them to (Ju,v/J) if u≠0 and h(v)=h(J)h(u); B2 sends them to (u/J,Jv) if v≠0 and h(u)=h(J)h(v), where h(z)=z conjugate(z) and J=1+ρ. Other cells stay fixed. The next bit is 1−b. No measurement directs a transition.

## Decisions, sampling and evidence

Report all 85 starts at each length. The same event can appear at more than one distance, and repeated emissions can come from the same input. The exact finite counts describe this stock and these horizons. The first-cone arrival cohorts must be nested; a broken chain falsifies the proposed attribution. A single vanished matter response falsifies universal retention at its tested length. Compare conditional retention fractions only for nonempty denominators. Test whether moving a matched initial pulse yields a nonincreasing arrival count or selects a unique signed phase, recording counterexamples. Distinguish persistent contrast from unchanged words, an inhibited collision from a physical write, and signed endpoint displacement from a count of rotations. No power law, continuum limit or late-time periodicity is inferred from four sites.

The primary verifier emits JSON Lines with pooled and direction-resolved data, per-rank counts, trajectory and full-evidence digests, and detailed fixed-port word witnesses for ranks 4 and 44. Calling paired.run directly retains all witnesses. Independently match all 18 prepared-arrival and response fields from audit.cpp for each (rank,distance,comparison). The C++ projection does not independently measure signed phase or the natural-emission counterfactual. Synthetic phase calibration includes the signed pentagram, a branch crossing shorter than one turn and a singular chord. Freeze code and protocol before launching decorated trajectories.

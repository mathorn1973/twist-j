# Route-length audit of natural and prepared H3 pulses

**Finite L1 result.** The natural experiment found four first-cone contrasts within one horizontal contact. In each case the recorded B2 emission blocked transport that occurred in the comparison arm. No first-cone arrival involved actual nonzero CORR transport. The recipient made a local matter write in all four cases. The matter contrast survived the next contact in all four cases, and the original word pair survived in zero. The fixed-port experiment detected prepared CORR contrasts at all four distances, with no matter hit at first arrival. Moving the preparation neither reduced arrival counts monotonically nor selected a unique signed net phase. [Preregistration](PREREG.md) · [Execution record](RUN.md).

The four westward arrivals compare a refused actual edge with an admitted comparison edge. All four gaps were nonzero in both arms. Actual-minus-comparison local contact phase was −2 fifth-turns in all four cases, because the actual gap was fixed and the comparison gap rotated by +2 fifth-turns. This measures the phase of each ordered contact gap in the specified embedding. The sign is independent of compass direction.

At each of the four recipients the actual B changed the matter pair and produced a contrast relative to the arm with the source B2 undone. Both CORR and matter remained distinct at the recipient after both C and B of the next tick in 4/4 cases. The original signed CORR difference persisted in 0/4, and the literal matter pairs were retained in 0/4. These are responses to suppressed transport at the earliest contact. Later, reflected or off-front natural effects were not counted.

# Proof: digit plus carry reconstructs the absolute counter exponent

**L1 arithmetic only.**

## 1. One-step complement

Let m=nu_2(n+1). In binary, n ends in exactly m consecutive ones before the
increment to n+1. Adding one erases those m ones and creates one new one.
This includes n=2^m-1, where the new one is a fresh leading bit. Therefore

    s_2(n+1)-s_2(n)=1-m=1-nu_2(n+1).

Thus the public SQRT-PHI-DIGIT-LIFT exponent increment and the chronological
carry length satisfy

    delta_n + kappa_n = 1

at every step.

## 2. Cumulative identity

Define C(0)=0 and C(n)=sum_(m=1)^n nu_2(m). Then

    C(n+1)-C(n)=nu_2(n+1).

Define H(n)=n-s_2(n). Section 1 gives

    H(n+1)-H(n)
      =1-[s_2(n+1)-s_2(n)]
      =nu_2(n+1).

Since H(0)=C(0)=0, induction yields

    C(n)=n-s_2(n).

Additivity of the 2-adic valuation on products independently gives

    C(n)=nu_2(product_(m=1)^n m)=nu_2(n!).

Hence s_2(n)+nu_2(n!)=n.

## 3. Uniqueness

Suppose an integer sequence H has H(0)=0 and complements the public digit
increment to one at every step. Then

    H(n+1)-H(n)
      =1-[s_2(n+1)-s_2(n)]
      =nu_2(n+1).

The same base value and increments force H=C.

In the free infinite cyclic group generated by r, equality of powers is
equality of integer exponents. Therefore the normalized multiplicative
condition

    r^s_2(n) Z_n=r^n

uniquely forces Z_n=r^C(n).

## 4. Two distinct specializations

At the ramified finite place, SQRT-PHI-DIGIT-LIFT has
Y_n^epsilon=r_epsilon^s_2(n), where r_epsilon has order eight. The exponent
identity gives

    Y_n^epsilon r_epsilon^C(n)=r_epsilon^n.

This is still a finite C8 record and cannot acquire archimedean growth.

Separately, in characteristic zero choose the positive real root rho^2=phi.
There is no assertion that rho and either r_epsilon are one carrier. They are
two specializations of the same free exponent identity. Put

    D_n=rho^s_2(n),  K_n=rho^C(n).

Then D_n K_n=rho^n, and rho^4=phi^2 gives

    (D_n K_n)^4=phi^(2n),
    (D_n K_n)^(-4)=phi^(-2n).

These are the hyperbolic powers of the accepted fixed-J Hodge loxodrome.

If a_H=phi^2 zeta_10 is imported only as the already accepted algebraic
loxodromic identity, then immediately

    a_H^n=(D_n K_n)^4 zeta_10^n.

The torsion factor is not produced by the carry complement.

## 5. Information boundary

The finite ramified digit state has at most eight values. The accepted
finite-reader theorem therefore excludes it, by itself, from representing a
nonzero hyperbolic orbit for all n. By contrast the completed exponent
s_2(n)+C(n)=n is injective on N_0 and has infinite image.

This proves resource sufficiency at the exponent-accounting level only. It
does not select a physical decoder or identify a finite-place root with the
positive real root. The open physical clock/gravity bridge is unchanged.

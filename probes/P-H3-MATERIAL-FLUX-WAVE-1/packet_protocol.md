```

           * (32+n*(rho-rho^-1)) * v[s]   for -15 <= n <= 15

```

Call this the left launch. The right launch is the literal reflection $x\mapsto1-x$, which preserves both matching sets, exchanges cell components and sends $n\mapsto-n$. The complete initial ledgers agree. The exact seed approximates an eigenvector of the staggered symbol derivative $G$ with positive eigenvalue. The algebraic seed uses $(688+162\phi)/1000$ as an approximation to $\sqrt{\nu}$ in both embeddings.

The matching bit is $t\bmod2$ after tick $t$, and the forward C uses $(t-1)\bmod2$. Inversion restores the all-zero rails and the phase, whose old matching bits are recovered in reverse order. The coefficientwise parity guard and C rotation are implemented over the integral basis. The initial $2^{80}$ factor pays up to one consumed factor of two per tick. Audit all forward ledgers and inverse steps through restoration of the full initial state. In each launch cross-check the first two forward C steps and first two reverse C steps against the pinned complete-torus transport, one of each matching phase in each direction. Refused gates, ledger mismatch, implementation disagreement or inverse failure are integrity stops.

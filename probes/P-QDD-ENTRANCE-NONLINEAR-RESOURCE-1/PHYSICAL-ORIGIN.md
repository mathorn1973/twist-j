## 1. Exact location of the missing influence

The native port factor (z,r) has its own closed evolution. The four
prepared endpoints have one common value of that factor. Unchanged U
therefore cannot create the desired difference between their r values,
at any duration. An arbitrarily elaborate classical causal controller does not repair
this if both its observations and its admitted port-affecting actions
factor through the same pair and its preparation is source-independent.
This causal restriction is an explicit complete mathematical class;
it is not a statement about every possible physical apparatus.

Information is not absent from the checkpoint. At counter three q=1-h,
and at every stable cut

    h_n=(-1)^(n-3)(z_n-q-r).

Thus the required entrance can be localized to the two port coordinates
q,r and the already specified counter and common trace. It preserves
q+r and moves information already encoded in that sum into the relative
port coordinate. It need not read four separate source coordinates.
Neither this reduction nor the presence of the information physically
implements its transfer.

The commandable native word b,d,b,e implements constant T_2 exactly.
All constant paired displacements follow by powers. This distinguishes
availability in an algebra of independently commanded words from actual
execution under the native selector. The desired conditioned choice
still needs a source-sensitive path into the action.

## 2. Native nonlinearity is sufficient with added affine routing

The stronger construction in NATIVE-ROUTING.md uses no work register and
no supplied nonlinear arithmetic gate. Two actual native selector steps,
at counters three and four with their actual bits 0 and 1, are interposed
with two explicit invertible affine maps A and B. Their exact action is

    F_1 B F_0 A Y_h = N_(3,2) C_3 Y_h,  h=1,2,3,4.

The freely evolved map N_(3,2) on this sheet is b squared, hence identity.
Every intermediate code map is injective and has phase zero, so the same
identity holds on all code amplitudes and sixteen matrix units, with no
retained work state. No unitary extension of global U is inferred.

This is a genuine resource reduction: the native selector can supply the
needed nonlinear label dependence once affine routing exposes the label
to that selector. It prevents overreading the low-degree endpoint
obstruction as a ban on affine controls combined with U. Two selector
steps are minimal in the explicit no-auxiliary affine-routing class:
zero steps remain affine and one step fails the HIGH collinearity test.
This is a discrete operation count at a specified launch phase, not a
physical time bound or a theorem about every apparatus.

The new physical burden is the availability of A and B, together with
their timing and carrier correspondence. They are target-synthesized
members of a generally defined affine family, not native words or
interactions already admitted by the Canon. Source motion during this
entrance need not match its free path; agreement with the freely evolved
source and the prescribed displaced-port entrance target is at completion.
The completed-block convention does not describe the
physical duration of either affine operation. No physical origin can be
claimed by calling these maps mere coordinate changes: they act on the
state.

## 3. A second sufficient arithmetic construction

The clean construction uses ordinary reversible field additions,
multiply-add operations and three initialized work registers. It never
treats C_n or the LOW projector as a primitive. It computes the fixed
five-value response, applies a balanced shift, and reverses all temporary
fine-label information. Full coherent equality survives, including an
untouched reference. The sixteen matrix units are the comparison domain.

Native ticks can occur between its completed blocks. The paired changes
of q and r must be completed before another native tick. A physical
process of nonzero duration inside that block is still missing; its
absence must not be hidden behind the discrete-cut convention.

The label response needs degree three on the code, and the declared
five-value extension needs degree four. This does not require nonlinear
quantum evolution or a three-body physical Hamiltonian. Several simple
interactions can compose to higher label degree. Conversely, a supplied
quadratic arithmetic interaction is not proved to be a native law.
Source-sensitive routing into the already nonlinear native selector,
general interference and differently encoded apparatuses remain outside
the proved low-degree endpoint obstruction.

Even the conservation of q+r and covariance under paired translations
leave exactly 3125 mathematical point-permutation laws. The required
law is one member. These symmetries do not select it physically.
The sum is not identified with physical charge or energy in this work.

## 4. Physical admission remains the missing input

The current public CORE and the v89 physical contract do not identify a
material carrier for q,r, an available source-sensitive interaction,
its physical time law or its complete environment. No existing accepted
theorem fills those fields. The native architecture is also explicitly
not derived uniquely from J. The present result cannot infer an absent
physical law from the algebraic axiom or from successful synthesis.

The preexisting external twenty-mode optical construction in #1038
addresses a different code-transfer channel. A prepared optical device
realizing that matrix is not evidence that native U supplies this entrance.
Likewise, external quantum control theory has sufficient generic gate
families, but importing them is a new conditional implementation model.
For example, Brennen, O'Leary and Bullock give exact universality from
connected one-qudit transition controls and a specified two-qudit
interaction in [Criteria for Exact Qudit Universality,
arXiv:quant-ph/0407223v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0407223v1).
That result supplies neither a TWIST-J dictionary for those degrees nor
a native-counter clock identification. No device or experiment is
claimed here, and their universality theorem is not a frozen audit input.

The next physical witness must supply, from independent evidence:

1. The actual q/r carrier, the code preparation and the shared reference.
2. An available interaction family and source-sensitive coupling, with
   all permitted controls, calibrated independently of this output target.
3. A nonzero-duration law including native evolution, synchronization,
   controller/environment state and decoupling after the entrance.
4. Whole-state agreement with N_(n,d)C_n on all code amplitudes, including
   clean or explicitly retained auxiliaries and an untouched reference.
5. An independent consequence outside the target, with the named layer
   gates and the scope/completeness required by the entrance contract.

Writing down a target-controlled Hamiltonian, selecting a matching matrix
or relabelling a reversible arithmetic primitive does not supply these
items. If such an interaction is adopted as a new architecture premise,
it must be declared as such. The present work does not make that choice.

## Frozen source, target, and finite-cylindrical ansatz

Use the public branch maps on `F_5^6`

```text
F_eps(psi) = g_(z_6(psi) + 2 eps mod 5)(psi),  eps in {0,1},
```

and the two-sided Thue-Morse subshift clock. The public one-sided
distinguished orbit is generated by `theta(n) = popcount(n) mod 2`.

For a driver window of length `L`, a cursor `c in {0,...,L-1}`, and a
J-orbit length `ell`, the exact constraint graph has nodes

```text
(w,k),  w a length-L Thue-Morse factor,  k in Z/ell Z.
```

Every length-`L+1` factor `v` gives the edge

```text
(v[:L],k) -> (v[1:],k+1),  labelled v[c],
```

a global assignment `x` is feasible exactly when

```text
x(target) = F_(v[c])(x(source))
```

on every edge and is total on every weak component. If the component root-seed
counts are `n_1,...,n_r`, the exact global solution count is
`n_1 * ... * n_r`, not their sum. It is positive exactly when every component
has a total consistent assignment. If one or more parameter triples are
positive, the verifier chooses the lexicographically first positive triple
and prints one deterministic root-seed witness for every one of its weak
components.

The zero residue is fixed by multiplication by J at every lambda-depth.
Therefore any finite-cylindrical cut restricts to the pure-word graph
`ell = 1`. Pure-word infeasibility for `(L,c)` excludes that window and
cursor at every lambda-depth. The verifier derives the generic zero-context
transition `(w,0) -> (tail(w),M_J 0)` and checks, for every E03 pair, that
`M_J 0 = 0` and that projection away from the zero coordinate gives exactly
the pure-word nodes and labelled edges. The all-depth lift is the displayed
restriction proof; no repeated finite `ell` check is presented as an
extrapolation.

### S02 FACTORS

For every `L = 1..40`, the verifier compares the exact factor sets, not
only their cardinalities, from Thue-Morse prefixes of lengths `512`, `2^16`,
and `2^18`. Their pinned cardinalities for `L = 4..17` are

```text
10, 12, 16, 20, 22, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48.
```

The length-512 prefix is a completeness certificate for this range, not an
empirical cutoff. Write `mu(0)=01`, `mu(1)=10`. Since `L <= 40 < 64`, every
length-`L` factor of the two-sided Thue-Morse language meets at most two
consecutive level-6 substitution blocks and is contained in `mu^6(ab)` for a
legal digram `ab`. All four legal digrams `00,01,10,11` occur in the
length-8 prefix `01101001`. Hence every such factor occurs in
`mu^6(01101001)`, which is exactly the length-512 prefix of the fixed point.
The reverse inclusion is immediate because that prefix belongs to the
language. Every finite-prefix factor window includes both endpoints.

## Falsifier map

A basis or factor mismatch fires S01 or S02. Failure to reproduce a pinned
engine value fires E01. Any positive global solution product in E02 through
E04 fires the finite-cylinder falsifier. The verifier selects the
lexicographically first positive parameter triple and prints one deterministic
root-seed witness for every weak component of that triple. All other positive
triples remain counted in the merged failure. A nonzero component count
without a complete component family is not a cut witness. A zero-residue structural mismatch
fires E05. A reversal mismatch fires E06. A primary component not
forward-reachable from its root fires C01. A wrong, disconnected, or vacuous
periodic control fires C02. A literal-affine, Thue-Morse, independent graph,
tree-residual, reachability, component-vector, or independent-control mismatch
fires C03. Every fired result is retained and merged; no threshold moves after
the pin.

## Pre-pin development disclosure

An incubation bundle, its x86_64 outputs, and local Windows confirmation runs
were inspected before this public preregistration. Gates E02, E03, most of
E04, and all displayed candidate conclusions were therefore seen before the
public pin. This public run is a pinned confirmation, not a blind discovery.

The incubation verifier also contained three wall-clock-dependent coverage
branches, printed a nondeterministic elapsed-time line, omitted the final
factor window of each finite prefix, compared prefix factor counts rather
than exact factor sets, and double-counted the 105 E02 cases inside its
reported total of 654. Those defects are removed before this pin.

A subsequent static pre-pin review found four more defects: component counts
were summed and a local positive could be misreported as a global cut; the
fixed-zero audit repeated one depth-independent identity under an unused
`ell` loop; the proposed relational route reused the primary graph, tables,
reachability, and propagation while allocating a full state-domain set at
every node; and equality of two long finite-prefix factor sets alone did not
prove completeness for the two-sided language. The accepted verifier instead
uses the global product with complete component witnesses, one structural
zero-context projection audited over E03, the separate linear-memory C03
route, and the level-6 substitution lemma with the exact length-512 factor
certificate frozen above.

The scientific range and zero threshold are not narrowed: the accepted public
surface deterministically runs all 522 pure-word pairs and all 27 depth
triples and reports 549 distinct candidate triples.

**Question and operational comparator.** Can an unchanged H3 source give a nonzero, signed material phase *increment* during equal-duration holds at radii \(r\) and \(2r\), with independently measured flight time? With two physical unit charges, \(U(r)=qp\,\alpha\hbar c/r\) and \(\exp(-iUT/\hbar)\) give \(\varphi_r-\varphi_{2r}=-qp\alpha\) at \(T=2r/c\). Fix the order near minus far. A candidate direct Coulomb read needs a nonzero hold contrast, reversal with each independently varied charge sign, and a common angular value across radii for a fixed source and instrument. A nonzero first write alone does not satisfy the test. The signed lifts on an odd anchor are available separately. The mixed-column read checks finite phase relations modulo one turn in both embeddings; it supplies no generic phase lift. Source orientation, bias and matter signs are operational preparation codes. A physical comparison also requires a source assignment and work-to-action calibration derived or measured independently. Use no numerical fit and no assigned value of alpha in the dynamics. [Physical normalization](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/RevModPhys.97.025002.pdf)

**Carrier, preparations, observables, systematics and falsifiers.** The linked protocols and all code in this directory are frozen prior to the first gate.

1. [Two loaded receivers on one native route](native_read.md). Fixed source amplitude and separately compensated launch, matched null and free-path pairs, flight, terminal and postarrival hold phases, work and changes to the field. An undefined phase or a nondetection is retained.
2. [Biased anchored receivers and autonomous emitter](anchored.md). Compare near/far on spatially separated paths, four independently crossed sign choices, an independent flight pilot, actual versus nominal far receipt, a held source with dynamic stock, and possible orbit closure. A nonresponsive probe, a compensated-only sign response or a transient-only read fails the respective stronger interpretation. The complete-word prediction on anchored matter can be falsified at any substep. Check full accounts and both local and composed reversals.
3. [Phase and work controls](quadrature.md). Test global sign equivariance, local fixed-reference source reversal and a two-contact native readout of equal-energy material phase. Compare Hermitian and scalar work separately.
4. [Scalar work and flight clocks](d3_read.md). On the adopted scalar \(D_3\) operator, evaluate static probe work and independently record first arrival and the tangent characteristic. Two box sizes, ten point pairs per box, with three pairs of inequivalent directions at equal radius. Check unit source balance and positive shortest-path amplitudes. A product of scalar work and elapsed ticks uses a disclosed action unit. The quantized lift of [#1943](https://github.com/mathorn1973/twist-j/issues/1943) is not part of the native tests.

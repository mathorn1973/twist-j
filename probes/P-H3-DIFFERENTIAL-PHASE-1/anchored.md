# Signed phase and elapsed branch counts on anchored matter

Use the first cyclotomic embedding, \(\rho=j^2=\exp(4\pi i/5)\), \(L=1-\rho\), \(J=1+\rho\), \(\Delta=J^{-1}-J\). Keep a single antipodal matter pair \((d,-d)\) at each receiver, with no other material in its column. The nonzero residue \(d\bmod 2\) blocks each C contact into vertical vacuum on both matter rails. B1 and B2 multiply the material gap by \(J\) and \(J^{-1}\). Both are units modulo two. Induction gives the literal word on every post-B slice
\[
 g_t=d J^{N_t},\qquad N_t=\#B1-\#B2 .
\]
The chosen embedding gives \(J=2\cos(2\pi/5)e^{2\pi i/5}\). Thus a simultaneous relative read at two identically prepared anchors and every time difference of that read have phase \((2\pi/5)\Delta N\) modulo \(2\pi\). The integer record retains the signed lift as well. This exact resolution excludes a direct single-window angle of magnitude strictly between zero and \(2\pi/5\) on that readout. An event frequency averaged over windows or a material gap mixed by C can have different resolution. Complete accounts are computed before and after both substeps. The source sign is \(q=\pm1\) and \(p=\pm1\) is the orientation of a fixed local CLK bias. They are preparation labels.

**Simultaneous spaced impulse arms.** Use a 240 by 4 native torus with matching bit zero and three sectors beginning at \(0,80,160\). The first two launch horizontally at their local coordinate zero and read at \(r\) and \(2r\). The third holds a free anchor at \(r\). All targets are on row zero. Set \(d=L^8\), \(b=(J+J^{-1})d\), and prepare one target CLK word \(pb\) with its opposite at local coordinate 50. The odd bias stays fixed before a signal. All sectors carry banks of opposite CORR word pairs \(s_i=2^i L^{8-i}\Delta,\ i=1,\ldots,8\), initially at \(30+2i,31+2i\) on row zero. Apply \(q\) to the words. Relocate only the positive slot of the selected pair to the sector's launch site. The reference retains both members in its bank. The fixed-amplitude arm selects \(i=8\) at all distances. A separate compensated arm selects each target's own distance \(i\), so the leading \(L/2\) transport produces \(q\Delta d\) there. Its gap is \(pJ^{pq}d\) and the first B should be B1 for \(pq=1\), B2 for \(pq=-1\). This is one way of preparing opposite phase shifts with the same receiver geometry. The unaltered-amplitude arm tests sensitivity to the distance calibration. All stock is present in each state and the banks are outside the observation cones.

Run \(r=1,2,3,4\), four sign pairs and both drive arms. Independently time the fixed impulse with only the radiation banks and launches present. Score actual arrival, first B, signed branch counts at every tick, reference stability, near-minus-far after \(2r\) and after \(4r\), and their difference during the common subsequent \(2r\)-tick hold. Report transient nonzero phase, zero endpoint, integer windings and forbidden writes as they occur. Check sparse native evolution against dense sentinels, every substep inverse, complete accounts, and final reversal. The two arms can coexist without cross-sector propagation in the measured interval.

**Autonomous point emitter.** Now omit the injected packet. Prepare a zero-center matter pair of gap \(\Delta\) at a fixed even source site, with CLK/CORR \(q\Delta(c+J),q\Delta(c-J)\), \(c=1+2\rho+2\rho^2\). Initialize once, then evolve every site by H3. At its first B the source becomes radiatively eligible to send a word to its left at tick two. The source material remains spatially pinned and exchanges its full local Hermitian stock with its field. For the receiver choose \(d=L\rho J\), \(b=(J+J^{-1})d\). The undisturbed one-bond leading word is \(q\Delta d\). Predict opposite first material phases for opposite \(pq\). Use the same source at all separations; there is no range calibration in this test. Keep every subsequent source event and all reflected radiation.

Use sector origin plus 20 for each nominal source and place the near and far receivers to its left at their specified distances. In the third sector move the actual source 38 sites right to supply an equal-stock null. Balance the source's radiation sum by an isolated CLK at local \(x=65,y=2\), and the probe bias by a separate CLK there at \(y=3\). They have no horizontal propagation from those columns. Evolve 32 ticks. Measure first receipt by separate source-only pilot runs, report \(N_r-N_{2r}\) at source emission, nominal fast-front times \(1+2r,1+4r\), and tick 32. Also score the change during \(2r\) ticks following **actual** first receipt at the farther probe when it exists. A missing receipt has an undefined elapsed-receipt measurement. Record every source B, every change on its left CORR neighbor across their matched edge, and the first divergence between full source-site words in the two coupled sectors. The latter compares different load geometries; different source trajectories are allowed.

**Exact recurrence of the isolated emitter.** In a separate 24 by 24 vacuum, use the same isolated \(q=+1\) emitter at (12,12), with no spectators. Stop at the first exact return of the full field with its matching bit, or at 340 ticks. Inspect and record the union of occupied sites/rails at both substeps. Exact return proves periodicity by autonomy. If the union stays strictly inside the box with a one-bond margin, locality also proves the same orbit on the infinite grid. Without such a return only the finite observation is claimed. The counts and support determine whether any farther resting receiver can be reached. No Coulomb behavior or action unit is assigned to the circuit.

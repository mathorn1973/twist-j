# Native same-carrier differential matter phase

This finite L1 test compares two matter half-gaps at distances $r$ and $2r$ in one native forward history from a single local horizontal CORR launch. The nearer receiver can modify the carrier before it reaches the farther receiver. A matched free-path history measures that difference. This test extends the earlier local and remote H3 phase-read fixture to simultaneous matter-phase measurements.

## Exact apparatus, signs and matched histories

Use the complete 128 by 4 torus at initial matching bit zero. Let $\rho=j^2$, $J=1+\rho$, $L=1-\rho$ and $u=128L^8$. In a loaded history, the two receiver columns are $x=r,2r$, for $r=1,2,3,4$, with observed sites on row zero. Two same-history reference material columns are $x=68,73$ for the near and far receivers, respectively. In every row of each column prepare matter $(p u,-p u)$, with probe sign $p=\pm1$. All CLK is zero. Uniformity keeps each column's material still under vertical C until B changes it. A reference column is outside the radiation causal cone through all scored cuts and co-evolves under the same native matching and B.

Prepare stocked CORR pairs $\pm s_d$ on row zero for $d=1,2,4,6,8$, where $s_d=2^{d+8}L^{8-d}J$. Pair $i$ in the displayed order occupies horizontal slots $36+2i,37+2i$ before relocation. Each history has all these words. In a local-launch arm multiply both words of the selected pair by the independently chosen source sign $s=\pm1$ and relocate its first word to $(0,0)$. The other pairs remain parked. Select $d=1$ in the main arm for every $r$, so this emitted source word has identical unsigned amplitude and shape across all distances. Select $d=2r$ in the secondary arm. There the geometric leading word at the far receiver would be $2uJ$ times the source sign if the intervening carrier remains free. The main arm has that same analytic clean calibration at distance one only. This is an amplitude-calibrated secondary test, with no tuning from the measured result. Source and probe signs here are exact signed native words. They do not encode the earlier Hermitian-account signs or a physical charge assignment.

For every sign pair, radius and drive mode also run the following complete equal-stock histories through the same clocks.

- **Parked source.** Keep the selected first word in its original slot with its chosen sign. The receiver and reference columns are unchanged. This supplies the matter-phase null under equal total stock.
- **Free path.** Keep the single local source and relocate the two receiving columns to $x=82,91$. Keep the two references fixed. This gives the unperturbed CORR at the original sites under equal full stock. The matter words at the original observation sites are zero, so their phase is undefined.

The parked coordinates are separated from the receiver and reference causal cones for the maximum horizon of 16 ticks. The same complete account tuple, including all parked words, is required across all 96 histories. Sign reversal of any selected radiation pair preserves stock and cancels its linear sum. Reversing all material words also preserves stock and leaves its linear sum zero. The native parity gates, quadratic B guards and sign-equivariant maps give a reason to predict even phase after normalizing the measured gap to its own signed reference. An odd response is tested separately.

## Time windows, exact phase and backreaction

Record every post-C and post-B cut at both receivers up to $4r$. Score the simultaneous near/far endpoints at $2r$ and $4r$ and their common $2r$-tick increment. The first endpoint is the earliest geometric far arrival. Report actual first nonzero field at each receiver and retain a suppressed or delayed far arrival as observed. Neither the farther receiver nor the reference overwrites the earlier state in the same run.

At each cut read the actual matter half-gap $g=(r0-r2)/2$ and the simultaneous reference gap $g_0$. The complex normalized gap is $g\overline{g_0}/h(g_0)$. Use its exact numerator to compare phase, since the denominator is positive in each embedding. At an endpoint multiply each loaded phase numerator by the conjugate of its parked-source counterpart to remove the null phase. For near and far endpoint words $A,B$, the differential is $A\overline B$. The phase increment between the two endpoints uses the later exact word times the conjugate of the earlier. A zero gap yields an undefined phase, distinct from a nonzero gap with zero phase increment. For each complex word report or derive real/imaginary signs in both embeddings with exact arithmetic in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$.

For the finite phase-only $1/r$ check use $A\overline{B^2}$ at each endpoint and on the interval increment. Its being positive real tests $\arg A=2\arg B$ modulo one turn in both embeddings. Require both angles to be defined and nonzero for nondegenerate support. The relation alone gives no absolute unwrapping or physical law. Across the independent source/probe sign grid test evenness and separately test nontrivial oddness by conjugating the reversed-sign phase and excluding equal angles. Record inactive, zero and even responses.

At every cut also record pre/post B CORR, the B tag and the Hermitian and scalar matter transfer. At each endpoint report loaded minus free CORR and the exact loaded/free ratio when the free word is nonzero. Record first departure from the free CORR at each receiver before B. The control comparison bounds changes in arrival amplitude, phase or timing associated with interposed loading. The endpoint observable reads the literal gap and does not substitute a B event count.

Use sparse exact matching on pairs with at least one nonzero input and the pinned B at each site with matter. Absent coordinates mean zero.

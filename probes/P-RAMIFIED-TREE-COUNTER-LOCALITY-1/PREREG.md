## 1. Equation and carrier

```text
O = Z[j], Phi_5(j)=0, J=1+j^2, beta=1-j, P=beta O.
T vertices = {a+P^k : a in O, k >= 0}.
Edges = immediate proper inclusions within this displayed family.
A(a+P^k) = a+1+P^k; M(a+P^k) = Ja+P^k.
```

The root is O. The tree uses only powers of the prime above five, not arbitrary
maximal subideals. Depth is recoverable from the root; no external spatial
coordinate is used to define incidence. Representatives do not label distinct
vertices if their cosets agree. Put v_P(0)=infinity and v_P(beta)=1.
The completion uses boundary metric d_P(x,y)=5^(-v_P(x-y)); it is distinct
from graph distance between finite vertices and from positional carry depth.

Five universal theorem targets, proved in PROOF:

1. `RAMIFIED-COSET-TREE`: the intrinsic rooted five-child tree, its Galois
   invariance and distance
   d_T(a+P^k,b+P^l)=k+l-2 min(k,l,v_P(a-b)). A and M are depth-preserving
   tree automorphisms. Every A displacement at depth k is 2k; an M displacement
   is 2(k-min(k,v_P(a))). There is no assertion that beta multiplication is
   motion to the current vertex's child.
2. `RAMIFIED-COUNTER-ORBIT`: P^k intersect Z=5^ceil(k/4) Z. Every A orbit at
   depth k has length 5^ceil(k/4); there are 5^(k-ceil(k/4)) such orbits. The
   natural zero-counter orbit occupies fraction 5^(-floor(3k/4)). Its closure
   is the embedded rank-one Z_5 module in the rank-four module O_P, not a
   physical dimension or a radial path claim.
3. `RAMIFIED-AFFINE-ADDRESS-TRANSITIVITY`: the group generated by A and M
   contains all translations by O and is transitive on O and each tree level.
   This follows from M^r A M^(-r)=translation by J^r and Z[J]=O. Address
   reachability supplies neither a fired native operation nor local transport.
4. `RAMIFIED-TREE-TRANSPORT-RADIUS`: for the literal data carrier below, exact
   A transport at depth k needs R*tau >= 2k. The same witness works for M at
   a unit address. No fixed finite R,tau implement it at all depths. R=0
   cannot implement nontrivial transport. No matching routing upper bound.
5. `RAMIFIED-TREE-TRANSPORT-CAPACITY`: under the additional finite total cell
   alphabet assumption below, q^(5^(K-1)) <= Q^(b_R*tau),
   b_R=(5^R-1)/4. At fixed R,Q,q>=2 this is exponential latency in K for
   simultaneous transport of the independent depth-K leaves. For q=Q it
   gives tau >= ceil(5^(K-1)/b_R). No capacity bound is claimed with
   unbounded information stored at a cell.

## 2. Literal transport model and equality

Use T or its truncation through depth K. At every vertex is a cell containing
a distinguished data symbol in an alphabet of size q>=2, and optionally local
workspace/control. Every assignment of the specified input data is admissible
with the same auxiliary initialization. In the radius theorem it suffices
that two allowed configurations differ only in one source data symbol. In
the capacity theorem every assignment of all 5^(K-1) leaf symbols in a chosen
first-level subtree is admissible, with every other initial cell fixed.

A deterministic microstep may depend on time and vertex but each new cell
state depends only on previous cell states in its graph ball of radius R.
The predetermined rule/schedule is the same for all compared inputs; no
input-dependent nonlocal scheduler, oracle or auxiliary initialization is
allowed. Output is the data component at the literal target cell after the
same fixed tau steps, with exact equality

```text
data_final(A(v)) = data_initial(v)
```

for every admitted input (respectively with M). Auxiliaries need not reset;
the updates need not be reversible. No distance-changing input/output codec
or global readout is part of this model.

For the capacity theorem the entire state of each cell, including workspace,
controller and phase, has at most Q states, a bound uniform in K and time.
For A choose the first-level subtree over residue 0; every one of its depth-K
leaves moves outside it. For M choose the subtree over residue 1, mapped to
residue 2. The inside R-boundary has at most b_R vertices. Its tau-step
state transcript has at most Q^(b_R*tau) possibilities; exact distinct
outside leaf outputs require at least q^(5^(K-1)). This is an information
count, without an entropy measure or a thermodynamic interpretation.

## 4. Systematics, nulls and exclusions

- Natural scalar-counter inclusion is one specified map; no census of every
  F(n,psi) is asserted. Module rank is not physical spatial dimension.
- Every integer increment has graph displacement 2k. It is not a rare carry
  effect; boundary ultrametric distance remains 1. Neither +1 nor J is radial.
- Affine transitivity is a positive control against the false inference that
  the counter orbit count bars algebraic access to other branches.
- Shared constant auxiliaries and full independent-data inputs are explicit
  resources. The native Omega=N_0 x F_5^6 does not supply this carrier.
- Radius and bandwidth are separate costs. The graph lower bound is not a
  claim of O(k) total routing; the finite-alphabet bound can be much larger.
- Completion of one decoded operation over multiple existing U ticks remains
  possible in principle. Variable return times, different encodings/graphs,
  correlated subcarriers and history readers are outside the frozen class.
- No theorem that all local physical interpretations need a new law. The
  physical bridge is NOT DERIVED, not universally disproved. No open owner,
  cross-layer gate, decoder, arrow of time, relativity or spacetime claim moves.

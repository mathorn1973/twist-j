## Complete preparation and reader class

All arithmetic is in F5 with representatives 0,1,2,3,4. Let x,y range
independently over all 25 pairs in F5^2. The entire four-coordinate piston
block P=(p1,p4,p1p,p4p) is the source block. The two-coordinate fibre
F=(q,r) is the receiver block. The initial counter is zero and every
preparation is exactly

    E(x,y)=(0, P0+v x, F0+w y),
    P0 in F5^4, v in F5^4\{0},
    F0 in F5^2, w in F5^2\{0}.

Offsets P0,F0 are common to all input pairs. All piston variation is the
one source line v x; its transverse degrees of freedom are common. The
receiver initially depends only on y and contains no copy of x. There
is no additional register, input-dependent launch time, intervention,
external control word, hidden auxiliary state, or independent seed.

Choose fixed affine readers on their own blocks:

    X(P)=A.P+a,  A in (F5^4)^*, a in F5,
    Y(F)=B.F+b,  B in (F5^2)^*, b in F5.

The SAME readers decode the initial preparation and the output:

    A.v=1, a=-A.P0,    B.w=1, b=-B.F0.

They have no access to the other block, the original x or y, history,
or a counter-dependent branch. Each candidate uses one fixed time
t in {1,2,3}, common to all 25 pairs. Advance exactly the native U from
n=0 for t steps and require

    X(P_t(x,y))=x,    Y(F_t(x,y))=y+x

for every pair. Equality of these outputs alone never identifies the
complete checkpoint: every actual six-coordinate state, counter and
selected generator must also be retained in the scientific evidence.

This finite parameter class is complete as stated: all P0,v,A,F0,w,B,t
satisfying the displayed conditions, with a,b forced. It has
438750000000 parameter tuples when t is included; equivalent descriptions
are allowed and are not silently removed. The task is existence or exact
nonexistence in this class, not in every nonlinear or differently owned
native encoding/reader class.

The count is 3*625*624*125*25*24*5: P0 has 625 choices; v has 624;
for each nonzero v the equation A.v=1 has 125 solutions; F0 has 25;
w has 24; and B.w=1 has five solutions. The constants a,b are then fixed.

## Actual update and exact projection reduction

At step n, theta_n=s_2(n) mod2, z=sum(P)+q+r, and generator index is
z+2 theta_n modulo five in order a,b,c,d,e. For this three-step test the
driver bits are exactly 0,1,1. They are computed from n, not selected to
achieve the target. Full generator formulas are part of the pinned source
and will be retyped independently by primary and reviewer implementations.

Put kappa=sum(P). The exact projected generator formulas to be proved
from the full native formulas are

    a: (k,q,r)->( k, q, r),
    b: (k,q,r)->(-k,-q,-r),
    c: (k,q,r)->(1-k,1-q,-r),
    d: (k,q,r)->(-k,1-q,1-r),
    e: (k,q,r)->(-k,2-q,1-r).

The selector also depends only on this triple and n. Thus for a proposed
preparation its fibre history depends on the source line only through
k0=sum(P0) and s=sum(v). The complete necessary receiver class is

    k0,s in F5; F0 in F5^2; w in F5^2\{0}; B.w=1;
    b=-B.F0; t in {1,2,3}.

There are 75000 such receiver configurations before choosing t. Every
configuration is the projection of an admitted source preparation and
initial source reader: take P0=(k0,0,0,0), v=(s,0,0,0) and
A=(s^-1,0,0,0) when s!=0; for s=0 take v=(1,-1,0,0), A=(1,0,0,0).
Set a=-A.P0. Hence the reduction does not discard any source-sum class.

For a negative decision it suffices to prove that no member of this
complete necessary receiver class realizes Y(F_t)=y+x. This excludes
every full candidate irrespective of source-output retention. The source
retention requirement is retained in the class, not weakened because the
receiver condition is easier to test. A receiver survivor alone would not
certify a positive full candidate; it would invalidate the proposed
receiver obstruction and require explicit source-retention analysis.

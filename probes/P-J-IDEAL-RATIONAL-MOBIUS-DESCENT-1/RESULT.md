## Exact result

Let
\[
 F=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5),\qquad O_F=\mathbb Z[\varphi],\qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For every rational prime, put
\[
 e_p=\#\operatorname{Fix}
 \left([A]:\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)\to\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)\right)
\]
and define
\[
 \chi_A(p)=e_p-1,
\]
then extend completely multiplicatively. The fixed points are exactly the
finite slopes satisfying \(t^2-t-1=0\). Quadratic reciprocity, with direct
checks at 2 and 5, gives
\[
 \boxed{\chi_A=\chi_5}.
\]

Let \(a_F(n)\) be the number of nonzero integral ideals of norm \(n\).
The split, inert, and ramified prime-power counts give
\[
 \boxed{a_F=1*\chi_A=1*\chi_5}.
\]

Define the ideal Möbius descent
\[
 b(n)=\sum_{N\mathfrak a=n}\mu_F(\mathfrak a).
\]
Its prime-local factors are
\[
 B_p(T)=
 \begin{cases}
  (1-T)^2,&p\text{ split},\\
  1-T^2,&p\text{ inert},\\
  1-T,&p=5.
 \end{cases}
\]
Multiplication by the local character series gives \(1-T\) in all three
cases. Consequently, coefficient by coefficient for every positive integer,
\[
 \boxed{\mu=b*\chi_A=b*\chi_5}.
\]

If
\[
 S_5(m)=\sum_{d\le m}\chi_5(d),
\]
then
\[
 S_5(m)=
 \begin{cases}
  1,&m\equiv1\pmod5,\\
  -1,&m\equiv3\pmod5,\\
  0,&m\equiv0,2,4\pmod5.
 \end{cases}
\]
Summing the descent identity gives both exact readings
\[
 \boxed{
 M(N)=\sum_{a\le N}b(a)
 S_5\!\left(\left\lfloor\frac Na\right\rfloor\right)}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{
 M(N)=
 \sum_{\substack{a\le N\\\lfloor N/a\rfloor\equiv1\ (5)}}b(a)
 -
 \sum_{\substack{a\le N\\\lfloor N/a\rfloor\equiv3\ (5)}}b(a)}.
\]

These are algebraic and incidence identities. No zeta or L-function
factorization, convergence statement, zero location, or Mertens bound is an
input.

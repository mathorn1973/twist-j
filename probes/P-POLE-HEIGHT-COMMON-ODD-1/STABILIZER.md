## 1. Chronological words and the exact transporter criterion

Let O=Z[j], Phi_5(j)=0, p=j^2, u=1-j, B=1-p and X=1+j-2j^3. Write

    I_t=(tB,-tB,u,u,X,2),
    G_t=(u+tB,u-tB,0,0,X,2),       t in {-2,-1,0,1,2}.

The primitive contact C_e, for one fixed slot pair e, includes its parity
refusal branch. It is a bijection with C_e^5=identity and inverse C_e^4.
Let Gamma be the group of transformations generated by these contacts.
No linearity between different input states is assumed.

Use a right action z.w: the word ab applies a first and b second. All
word products below are chronological. Suppose a word w0 has been verified
to send both I_-1 to G_-1 and I_+1 to G_+1 with their complete helpers.
The supplied 38-contact word is a candidate for this role, with its exact
endpoint assertions audited separately. Its reported minimality is not a
premise here.

Define the ordered odd pair and its pointwise stabilizer by

    g=(G_-1,G_+1),
    K={k in Gamma : g.k=g}.

Equality is componentwise: exchanging the two odd states is not pointwise
stabilization. Define the residual even triple and its desired endpoint by

    r=(I_-2.w0,I_0.w0,I_2.w0),
    h=(G_-2,G_0,G_2).

There exists a common contact word loading all five inputs if and only if

    h belongs to r.K.

For sufficiency, choose k in K with r.k=h; then w0 k loads every input.
For necessity, if w loads every input, set k=w0^(-1) w. On the odd pair it
first returns to (I_-1,I_+1) and then applies w, so k is in K. On r the
adjacent w0 w0^(-1) cancels, giving r.k=h. This equivalence concerns the
full stabilizer, not the finite subgroup sampled by the search.

## 2. Closed excursions give explicit stabilizer elements

Choose paths U_a and U_b from g to ordered odd-pair states a and b. If

    g.U_a=a,       a.C_e=b,       g.U_b=b,

then the chronological Schreier loop

    L=U_a C_e U_b^(-1)

returns g exactly. Its inverse is

    L^(-1)=U_b C_e^4 U_a^(-1).

The first loop goes from g to a, across the edge to b, and back to g;
the second follows the same route backwards. Both fix each odd input,
including every helper, although intermediate odd states may move.
Their actions on the even inputs need not be identity.

An idle contact at a is the special case b=a, U_b=U_a. It gives the
conjugated loop U_a C_e U_a^(-1). For paths of at most four forward
contacts, the displayed elementary lengths are bounded by

    length(L)<=4+1+4*4=21,
    length(L^(-1))<=4+4+4*4=24.

The second bound uses the compact inverse expression above. Expanding
the inverse of an already expanded 21-letter word into 84 forward letters
would describe the same transformation but waste its known cancellations.
In every case C_e^4 is charged as four forward contacts when an elementary
word length is reported.

These identities apply to the complete gated contact because its inverse
identity is global, including refused contacts. Reducing adjacent powers
of the same C_e modulo five is therefore valid on every input, not merely
on the odd pair used to discover a loop.

## 3. Why this exceeds stepwise-idle control

Let S_idle contain the primitive edges that individually fix g. A word
made only from S_idle leaves the odd pair at g after every elementary
step. This is a restricted control alphabet. A closed excursion need only
return the odd pair at its end; it can visit other odd states first.
Consequently a failure or obstruction proved for stepwise-idle words does
not automatically exclude the larger stabilizer K.

The finite search collection includes the twelve primitive idle edges
and selected closed excursions. Their inverse directions are supplied
explicitly. Every retained macro must pass componentwise odd-pair closure
and inverse checks before it is used on the even triple. A proposed word
is finally replayed on all five original inputs. Odd restoration is
required at every macro boundary, while temporary odd motion inside a
macro is allowed and fully accounted for.

The initial w0 and the chosen macro sequence form one fixed program for
all five inputs. The input label cannot select a different program.
Different internal parity decisions remain part of the contact law.

## 4. A finite collection is a subgroup, not a completeness result

With a transversal of the entire odd-pair orbit and every generator edge,
Schreier's construction generates its full stabilizer. The bounded
excursion tree in this probe is different: depth at most four, at most
2000 retained odd-pair states, and a deterministic selection of at most
32 undirected macros, twelve idle primitives and at most twenty selected
loops. Their at most 64 directions generate some K0 contained in K. Neither the depth
cutoff nor the selection proves K0=K.

In particular, a loop must not be discarded merely because it acts as
identity on the odd orbit. The kernel of that restricted action may be
exactly what moves the even inputs. Likewise two words agreeing on the
current even triple can disagree after another macro has moved that
triple. Safe equality reduction uses established global word identities
or a faithful action on all relevant carriers. Target-based macro scoring
and bounded selection are heuristics, not group-completeness arguments.

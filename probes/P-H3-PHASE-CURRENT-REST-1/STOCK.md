# Minimum stock for a signed state and for an active collision

This is a post-run algebraic consequence of the public H3 accounts. It compares a minimum over all complete states with a local collision threshold. Neither quantity measures inertial or rest mass.

Let H=A+Bφ be the full Hermitian account, ν=B and Q=2A+B its real-subfield trace. All inequalities refer to the two ordered real embeddings. A nonzero H3 state has strictly positive embedded sums of squares. Consequently Q+√5ν and Q−√5ν are both positive, and Q≡ν modulo two. At |ν|=1 this requires Q≥3. A single nonzero matter rail J has h(J)=2−φ, ν=−1 and Q=3. Replacing it by 1/J gives h(1/J)=1+φ, ν=+1 and Q=3. Both words are odd. With every other rail zero each preparation is stationary for either matching. This minimum allows the linear accounts to vary.

For an arbitrary nonzero integer word z let q(z) be the real trace of h(z). Its field norm N(h(z)) is a positive integer, so q(z)≥2 by the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality at the two real embeddings. Equality forces h(z)=1. If B is active its two half-gaps d,f are integral and nonzero. Their squared norms differ by the factor θ=h(J) or its inverse. They cannot both have q=2. Hence q(d)+q(f)≥5. With the local material and radiation centers m,b, the cell stock is

```text
Q_cell = 2q(m)+2q(b)+2q(d)+2q(f) ≥ 10.
```

The bound is attained. The single cell (1,−1,J,−J) in an otherwise empty field survives its first C unchanged and executes B1 at total Q=10. With material gap J and radiation gap 1 the corresponding B2 example also has Q=10. Thus the smallest state carrying unit ν and the smallest possible active B have different, sharply determined resource requirements. No effective particle Hamiltonian, momentum observable or calibration enters this argument.

## Phase and current in the same apparatus

Use the 8 by 4 torus, initial matching zero and five receiving sites (r,0), r=0,...,4. Write ρ=j², θ=h(J), h(a)=a·conjugate(a). The two matter rails contain a center plus and minus a half-difference. The five receiving centers are c with half-differences 2ρ^r. The five sites (r,1) have center −c and the same signal half-difference 2ρ^k. The five copies and their phase origins are supplied at launch. The data code k ranges over the five fifth roots. At all eight columns in rows zero and one set CLK=2J, CORR=−2J. Unspecified coordinates vanish. Test c=ρ², −ρ² and 1. In addition run each data/center pair with all ten common matter rotations ±ρ^s. For every run require that all ten input matter rail contacts at the five receivers are admitted, exactly reader k executes material-changing B1, and the others execute B0. Check the full H value 100+128θ, Q=584 and both linear sums zero, equality of the rotated native evolution, full inverse, all five Hermitian output norms, relative phase multipliers of B and the rational trace control. At the active reader require ΔH_rad=8(1−θ), ΔQ_rad=−8 and the opposite matter transfer. The four other readers must transfer zero. The old rank-43 reads 135 and 671 are two separately identified observations from #2028, not part of this new launch family. Recompute their local matter contact, branch, inverse, relative gap kick, projected incoming current and Hermitian transfer from its recorded cuts.

Define the oriented edge matter current as the sum of the two admitted rail currents (1−ρ)(a−b)/2. A refused rail contributes zero. The integer projection ℓ(x)=x3−x1 is inherited up to normalization from the public archive trace. Check the first matched edge current at all five receivers for the three centers: +2, −2 and 0 respectively. Determine separately the exact primitive edge counts ±1. For each of the 15 unrotated launches run 85 ticks, both C and B. At each site verify continuity in each species, zero transport of its residue at j=1 modulo five, full accounts and inverse. Test the sign-pair symmetry (r0,r2)↦(−r2,−r0) on all coordinates at every cut, and equality of the radiation fields and branch records. An active branch in the histories is required. The claim about a signed radiation source applies to sign-odd readings on this paired class only. It does not assume that electric charge is linear in field amplitudes.

## An alternative signed source and phase readout

Test a second finite two-tick device on the same torus with initial matching zero. Fix φ=−(ρ+ρ⁻¹), u=J/j=j+j⁻¹ and the integer component ν(a+bφ)=b of a real Hermitian account. Use two 2 by 2 tiles (columns 0,1 and 4,5, rows 0,1), each with source cell (d,−d,2,−2). In arm A their gaps in order are 2u and 2/u. In arm B exchange the tiles' gaps. All initial radiation and per-site scalar accounts coincide, each material center is zero, and the complete ledgers must match. Both source words are positive real in the principal embedding and have the same phase in the other embedding. Their local ν values are opposite. Score the first native B at (1,1): predict B2 and B1 with distinct equal-norm material outputs 2/j and 2j and opposite relative phase kicks. Record the outgoing quadratic radiation component and its spatial propagation to the initially empty horizontal neighbor (2,1) at the next C.

Prepare one further fixed tile at columns 0,1 and rows 2,3. Prescribe its first committed cell as (2j,−2j,1,1−4J), and preload its local inverse B. Initial C links equal tile values. Its first B must prepare the intended read without an intervention. At tick two on (1,2) require equal pre-C receiver, equal incoming radiation and admitted matter contacts for both source codes. Predict an active B1 for the 2j emission and B0 for the 2/j emission. On distinct diagnostic copies exchange only the source material pair between the two 1 B cuts, leaving every radiation rail and the other sites fixed. Require equal complete accounts, reversal of the receiver decision in both directions and full inverse back to the launch. Report whether the transmitted radiation values are additive negatives under the source interchange. These are two specified encodings of a signed component of H. An arbitrary physical charge-conjugation symmetry, Gauss law and force do not follow from the comparison.

## Frequency, transport and the matter sector

With no radiation prepare one column of antipodal matter with first-rail profiles (1,1,1,1), (1,−1,1,−1) and (1,1,−1,−1). They have the same full ledger and pointwise Hermitian density. Repeat with one counted fixed remote CORR word of 1. Test both native substeps of ten ticks, complete inverses, the anchored ratios for referenced launches, two-tick multipliers 1, ρ² and ρ, and complete-state periods including the layer bit. At cell translation by two sites the first two profiles have character +1 and the third has character −1. Check the −1 character operator by exact matrix algebra and by a further nonconstant admissible ring-valued example. Contrast four separated odd pairs and one even pair with identical total accounts. Record the first native support change.

For a separate exact transport comparison place a single CORR row on an 8 by 8 square. Copy it to a vertical column as opposite matter rails with radiation zero. Compare both native substeps and full-field inverses for all 81 repeated four-symbol patterns from {0,1,2} and both initial matching bits through ten ticks. Check the coordinate equality, inactive B and doubling of the Hermitian and scalar budgets. Both refused and admitted unequal contacts must occur. The row/column identity over arbitrary even lengths is tested here on a finite corpus and justified separately from the arithmetic of C and B. Compare its dispersion scope with H3-FUNDED-LONGWAVE-SECTOR. No fitted rest frequency, momentum, recoil, particle spectrum or electromagnetic field equation is assumed.

A mismatch in a registered exact equality falsifies that comparison. A failure of custody or execution gives no numerical result. These tests distinguish the native observation of a supplied relative phase, a conserved projected current, the response of radiation and a model frequency. They do not promote a dictionary or change the Canon.

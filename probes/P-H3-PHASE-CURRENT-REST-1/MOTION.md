# Motion at fixed stock

Let a two-site cell have Bloch character z under translation by two sites. A is (1+ρ)/2 and D is (1−ρ)/2. On the linear branch where the parity test admits the pair, the two successive matching matrices are

```text
S0 = [[A,D],[D,A]]
S1 = [[A,D/z],[Dz,A]]
det(S1 S0) = ρ²
tr(S1 S0) = 2A² + D²(z+1/z).
```

At z=1 the constant and alternating internal profiles have eigenvalues 1 and ρ². Both are exact integer dynamics for the displayed unit profiles. At z=−1 the matrix product is ρI. For initial rows (a,b,−a,−b) with a≡b modulo 2, the first two outputs u,v have even sum u+v=a+b. The second matching between v and −u, and between −v and u, therefore admits too. The entire profile is multiplied by ρ after two ticks, which preserves the condition for the next two. The probe uses common exact ledgers with identical pointwise norm, and repeats with an anchored algebraic phase reference. A temporal rotation at zero cell momentum is mode-dependent even under these controls. Both the branch choice and the time unit would matter in any rest-mass proposal.

There is also an exact connection to the public free-wave result. Take an even cyclic line of CORR values z_i with zero matter and zero other radiation. Place z_i on matter R0 and −z_i on R2 in a vertical line of the same length, with zero remaining matter and zero radiation. C on that line uses the same ordering and bit as on the original horizontal line. Its action on the negative rail is the negative of its action on the positive rail, including parity refusals. At B the source has matter gap zero and the target has radiation gap zero. Both collisions are idle. Thus the copying map intertwines each matching, each inverse and every time step, and is injective on this sector. The target's H and Q are exactly twice the source's. Linear radiation stock from the source is encoded in a named matter rail, and the full target has zero matter sum.

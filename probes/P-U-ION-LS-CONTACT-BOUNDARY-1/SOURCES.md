## S1: primary selected ion model

Pavel Hrmo et al., **Native qudit entanglement in a trapped ion quantum
processor**, Nature Communications **14**, 2242 (19 April 2023).
DOI: [10.1038/s41467-023-37375-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37375-2).
[Version of record](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37375-2).
[Figure 1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37375-2/figures/1).

Locations inspected: Results, Eqs. (1)-(6), Figs. 1-3, experimental setup
description and Discussion. Figure 1 was visually read on the publisher's
figure page, including the non-monotone mapping of labels 1-4 to D-state
magnetic quantum numbers. The article is published under CC BY 4.0;
this probe embeds no licensed image or article copy.

Used source facts: the labelled S/D carrier; the effective COM light-shift
Hamiltonian and its approximations; closed-loop duration; common rotations;
the cyclic echo and its simplified diagonal phase gate. The two-ion
experiment supports these ingredients, not a fourteen-ion contact history.

New model choices: identical light-shift profiles and COM coupling on both
ports; complete closed pulses before other operations; common bare drift;
fixed common label dictionary; isolation from other data; arbitrary
collective U(5) operations as a favourable mathematical enlargement.
The general source Hamiltonian uses ion-indexed shifts and does not assert
all these restrictions for every apparatus.

Original deductions: the explicit Magnus factorization with retained
spatial phases, swap-invariance of `A A^dagger`, the directional-contact
obstruction and the symbolic endpoint-energy table. The article is not
cited as having proved these TWIST-J-specific statements.

## S2: multilevel processor existence, not an imported gate library

Martin Ringbauer et al., **A universal qudit quantum processor with trapped
ions**, arXiv:[2109.06903v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06903v1),
14 September 2021; journal reference Nature Physics **18**, 1053 (2022),
DOI: [10.1038/s41567-022-01658-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01658-0).
[Inspected full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06903v1).

Locations inspected: abstract, native control description, supplementary
sections SIII-SIV. This independently demonstrates local dimension up to
seven and discusses individual control and MS-based synthesis. It is
background evidence of multilevel control, not evidence that its MS gates,
auxiliary levels or compilation recipes are already compatible with the
selected Hrmo LS model. No S2 gate is added to `C_closed_Ca5`.

## S3: multimode and phase limitations

Pavel Kamenskikh et al., **Analysis of the action of conventional
trapped-ion entangling gates in qudit space**,
arXiv:[2602.21886v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21886v1), 25 February 2026.
DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2602.21886](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.21886).
[Inspected full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21886v1).

Locations inspected: introduction; section II.1, Eqs. (7)-(13); section
II.2, Eqs. (14)-(22). This is a theoretical preprint, not a new experiment
certifying this probe. It treats mode-dependent displacements and both
local and pair phases. A single COM period is not an all-mode certificate;
different mode couplings can also give different local phases. Its
zero-order LS case identifies the S/D polarizability approximation behind
the simplified Hrmo construction. It supplies neither a numerical error
tolerance for fourteen ions nor a proof of the complete history contract.

The conditional trace-distance extension in MODEL.md is an original
mathematical implication. No numerical `delta_out` is inferred from S3.

## Source independence and limits of the scientific claim

The choice of carrier and effective interaction is imported from
independent physical literature. Selection of this restricted control
class is an explicit design decision. No experiment in S1-S4 is presented
as having executed `C4`, the two-contact protocol, or native U. No spectrum,
pulse parameter or decoder is adjusted to match a carry table. The proof
decides the frozen class, not the existence of a natural or unique physical
reading of J. Source facts, mathematical implications, formal replay and
physical certification remain separate evidence categories.

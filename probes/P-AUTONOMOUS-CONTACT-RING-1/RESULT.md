## Entropy and infinite-space boundary

For a finite ring at fixed proper account E, let M be the finite number of
admitted ring arrays. Any fixed finite-valued reader of the array and b_n,
with one output symbol per native tick, has

    B_out(ell) <= 4 M ell,

so its topological word growth and every supported per-tick Shannon entropy
rate are zero. The unbounded counter is not discarded; its admitted
valuation-parity projection has the proved linear block bound.

For the same radius-one law on the infinite line with fixed finite total
proper account E, there are at most E nonzero cells and a finite alphabet
A_E of possible occupied cell values. A fixed width-w observation obeys

    B_out(ell) <= 4 ell *
        sum_{k=0}^E binomial(w+2 ell,k)(|A_E|-1)^k.

This is polynomial in ell, so the per-tick entropy rate is again zero.
Unlimited empty space by itself therefore does not supply a positive rate
of new local information in this declared source class. This is a counting
proof, not an entropy estimate from the finite executed histories.

Both bounds require a fixed finite-valued reader, no-event ticks included,
and no arbitrary inspection of the full integer counter. Growing uncounted
memory, freely varying zero-account registers, infinite total account,
an information-bearing infinite background and arbitrary sparse event-only
sampling are outside the assertions. Infinite-line and finite-ring local
agreement is a graph-dependency statement at the same initial clock phase,
not a physical spacetime claim.

# Zero-first stabilizer chain and seven small stock certificates

Status: NON-CANONICAL. The group and coset arguments below are exact analytical
candidate results at action layer L1. The named finite certificates are exposed
forecasts for this probe, not completed runs. No physical carrier, energy reader,
or new Canon status is claimed.

## 1. Contract and finite permutation action

Let O = Z[j], with 1+j+j^2+j^3+j^4=0, p=j^2, u=1-j,
B=1-p and X=1+j-2j^3. For x in O set
Q(x)=Tr(x conjugate(x))/2. In the power basis, if x has coefficients a_0,...,a_3,

    2Q(x) = 5 sum(a_i^2) - (sum a_i)^2.

The integral contact C refuses when x-y is not in 2O. Otherwise it sends
(x,y) to (s+p d,s-p d), where s=(x+y)/2 and d=(x-y)/2. Each contact
preserves the pair sum, its Hermitian account and hence Q, and every labelled
slot modulo u. It satisfies C^5=identity, including its refused branch, so
its inverse is C^4. This is the integral contact law, not an identification
with a different native evolution law.

The six labelled slots have preparations and exact catalytic targets

    I_t = (tB,-tB,u,u,X,2),
    G_t = (u+tB,u-tB,0,0,X,2),    t in {-2,-1,0,1,2}.

Their total Q is 10t^2+33. The last two slots must return exactly. A common
word means one fixed sequence of labelled contacts; its admission decisions
may differ between inputs. It never receives the input label as a control.
Positive definiteness of Q makes each fixed-account lattice shell finite.
Thus the contacts generate a finite permutation action on the union of these
five relevant shells. C^5 alone would not establish that finiteness.

Use chronological right action: z.(ab) means first a, then b. The inherited
word W loads both odd inputs. The frozen bank in BANK.json supplies an
inverse-closed generating list for a subgroup K0 that fixes G_-1 and G_1
pointwise. Their actual word endpoints and inverse labels must be audited.
Set r_t=I_t.W for t=-2,0,2. The remaining bank problem is

    find k in K0 with r_t.k=G_t for all three even inputs.             (1)

Failure within K0 does not exclude a word in the full contact group.

## 2. Solve zero first without losing any bank solution

Find v in K0 such that r_0.v=G_0. If the complete K0 orbit of r_0 excludes
G_0, equation (1) is impossible. A capped or otherwise partial orbit cannot
establish exclusion. Once v exists, put s_t=r_t.v and

    K1 = {k in K0 : G_0.k=G_0}.

All zero-loading words in K0 form exactly the right coset v K1. Indeed, any
other zero-loading w gives v^-1 w in K1, and every v k loads zero. Therefore
(1) is equivalent to

    (G_-2,G_2) in (s_-2,s_2).K1.                                    (2)

Choosing a particular zero bridge does not discard another valid bank
solution. This is a coset identity, not a heuristic preference for a short
bridge. A bounded search using some subgroup H1 of K1 is still sound for
positive witnesses. A complete no-target orbit would exclude that subgroup
only; a bounded miss is inconclusive even there.

The same construction can be repeated. If a in K1 sends s_-2 to G_-2,
put q=s_2.a and

    K2 = {k in K1 : G_-2.k=G_-2}.

Then (1) is equivalent to G_2 belonging to q.K2, and any final bridge b in
K2 gives the common word W v a b. The chosen order is 0,-2,+2. At each
stage previously solved preparations are held exactly at macro boundaries;
contacts inside a macro may make excursions. Full elementary replay must
still verify all five final states and their inverse recovery.

## 3. Complete Schreier generators and the kernel obligation

Let K be generated by a finite inverse-closed macro list M. On a finite
orbit of a root g, choose a path U_x in these macros from g to every orbit
state x, with U_g empty. For every x and every m in M, let y=x.m and form

    L(x,m) = U_x m U_y^-1.                                         (3)

Every such loop fixes g. If the orbit is complete and every edge is retained,
these loops generate the full stabilizer of g in K. To see this, write a
stabilizer word m_1...m_n and let x_i=g.m_1...m_i. The corresponding product
of (3) telescopes: adjacent U_x^-1 U_x cancel, and x_0=x_n=g. The product
is the original word. This proof does not require the action on the root
orbit to be faithful.

That last point is essential. A nonempty word can act as the identity on
one root, or even on its complete orbit, yet move a remaining input. Such
kernel elements belong in the next stabilizer. In particular:

- include self-loops and all generator edges, not just the spanning tree;
- retain a word even if it fixes the current root;
- when claiming the full stabilizer, do not merge or discard words merely
  because they agree on one remaining input or sampled states; heuristic
  selection of a subgroup is explicitly different;
- exact equality of contact words after valid identities, or keeping one
  representative together with its inverse, is a safe reduction;
- selection of only some loops constructs a subgroup and must be reported
  as such, even when the root orbit used to construct them was complete.

A partial tree still gives valid loops whenever both endpoints and their
paths are known, but the telescoping proof of full generation no longer
applies. Complete closure under a selected list proves an orbit theorem for
that selected subgroup only. Neither a beam limit nor a generator limit is
a no-go theorem for the omitted generators.

The operational construction in this probe completes the zero orbit and its
full Schreier family, then explicitly selects generators of a subgroup H0
of K1. The rooted paths may use Dijkstra costs equal to the declared native
word lengths; the proof above needs their actual endpoints, not a shortest
path claim. Generator selection and all ties are frozen in the verifier.

For the next component, suppose a source walk F from s_-2 and a target walk
T from G_-2 meet at the same state. Then a=F T^-1 sends s_-2 to G_-2.
Separately, any two H0 words U,V starting at G_-2 and ending at the same
state give a collision loop U V^-1 that fixes G_-2. Both also fix zero and
the odd targets. A selected bank of these collision loops generates a
subgroup H2 of Stab_H0(G_-2). Searching from q=s_2.a to G_2 under H2 and
finding a bridge b produces the full witness W v a b.

The bounded construction may retain up to eight distinct bridges a_i for
-2 and try their resulting +2 sources q_i=s_2.a_i. They can share the same
collision-loop bank H2 because every a_i has the same solved endpoint G_-2.
A successful final bridge b_i gives W v a_i b_i. These are alternative
constructive choices, not a claim that eight bridges represent the entire
transporter coset or that their unsuccessful searches exhaust it.

This collision construction retains nonempty loops rather than discarding
them because they fix the current component. It does not prove that sampled
collision loops generate the full stabilizer. The deterministic birthday
walks and final bounded bidirectional search are constructive searches, not
complete even-orbit classifications. After the completed zero stage, every
resource-limited miss is INCONCLUSIVE. Complete exhaustion under a selected
bank would still concern that selected subgroup, not K0 or the full contact
group. The exact chain in Section 2 remains the criterion; this bounded
implementation seeks a positive certificate within it.

## 4. Sign exchange does not remove the final component

Let P exchange slots 1 and 2. Then I_-t=P I_t and G_-t=P G_t. Relabelling
also conjugates a contact word: P C_ab P is the contact on the relabelled
edge. Consequently a word for +2 provides a relabelled word for -2, generally
not the same word. Equality of separate orbit sizes is not a common-word
certificate. Reduction to one sign would require an established commuting
symmetry of the actual schedule, or an equivalent proved relation on the
whole relevant action. Neither is assumed here.

## 5. Seven small finite certificates

For a neutral stock S replace X by S in I_t. The following six stocks are
given by their unique zero-sum five-coefficient representatives:

    (-2, 1, 0, 1, 0), (0, 1, 0,-2, 1), (-1, 0,-1, 2, 0),
    (-1, 2, 0,-1, 0), (1, 0, 1,-2, 0), (0,-1, 0, 2,-1).

Each has Q(S)=15. The exposed certificate forecast is a complete 13-state
orbit for t=1, with only contacts 12,15,25 changing a state. Reference
slots 3 and 4 remain u and charged slot 6 remains 2. A finite list that
contains the preparation and is closed under all fifteen contacts proves
all future states lie in that list. Since u is nonzero, neither required
zero reference is reached. Exchanging slots 1 and 2 gives the corresponding
closed certificate for t=-1. smallstock.py prints the actual state lists,
checks every edge in both signs, and compares two exact arithmetic paths.
The count 13 is an exposed assertion, not a substitute for edge closure.

The seventh stock is S=2(j^2-j^4), Q(S)=20, with total account 48. Here use
a value alphabet instead of a full state orbit. Neutral values start with
{B,-B,u,S}; the sole charged slot starts with 2. A neutral-pair transition
need only be closed when Q(x)+Q(y)<=45, because the charged residue-2 slot
has Q>=3. A neutral/charged transition need only be closed when its account
is at most 48. All other slots have nonnegative Q. The residue bound follows
from the exact small-norm possibilities: Q=0 has only zero, Q=1 is absent,
and Q=2 consists of signed fifth roots, whose residues are 1 or -1, not 2.
For completeness, 2Q=sum_(i<k)(a_i-a_k)^2 for five integer coefficients.
A nonconstant range-one vector has value m(5-m), m=1,...,4; a range of
at least two has value at least 10. Thus the only value below 6 other than
zero is 4, from one exceptional coefficient or its complement.

The forecast is closure on 15 neutral and five charged values, excluding
u+B. Every reachable odd preparation lies in the resulting labelled
alphabet by induction. Both signs require u+B in one of their first two
slots, so exclusion proves the unrestricted-helper no-go. This closure is
budget-relative; arbitrary extra stock or a larger account is outside its
scope. smallstock.py prints both alphabets and audits all 375 ordered
neutral/neutral, neutral/charged and charged/neutral pairs, applying the
appropriate budget condition to the closure test. Two charged slots are
not present. Inverses follow from C^5 and preservation of the pair budget.

The auditor also checks that these seven stocks are distinct and outside
the previously established 124-stock classes. Only after these certificates
pass may they be combined into 131 exclusions in the declared 171-stock
ball. No fresh complete census of the remaining stocks is implied.

## 6. A precise limitation of the parity explanation

An initial stock parity equal to a data parity does not by itself trap the
stock away from the references. For the neutral Q15 stock

    S=-1-j^2+2j^4 = B modulo 2O,

C25 sends the values (-B,S) to (-1-j+2j^4,-1+j). The new slot 2 has the
parity of u. C23 then sends (-1-j+2j^4,u) to
(j^4-j^2,j^4+j^2-2j), changing reference slot 3. These are exact admitted
contacts. The small no-go certificates concern their specified complete
orbits or alphabets, not all stocks in a data parity class.

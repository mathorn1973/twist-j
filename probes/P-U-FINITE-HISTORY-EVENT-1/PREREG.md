## Field 1: equation and proposed conjunction

Let `theta_n=popcount(n) mod 2`, with fixed origin n=0, and let
`mu(0)=01, mu(1)=10`. A causal length-L clock window ending at n is

    w_L(n) = theta_(n-L+1) ... theta_n,       n >= L-1.

A. For k>=1, the length `L_k=5*2^(k-1)` admits an exact function
`phase_k(w_Lk(n)) = n mod 2^k` on every legal window. Five bits contain an
equal adjacent pair; such a pair begins at an odd global index. This fixes
the ending parity, and decimation at even global indices gives the parent
window for recursion. k=0 has the constant phase zero. In particular k=8
requires at most 640 past-and-current bits. No minimality is claimed.

B. Across the union of ALL fixed finite clock-only window readers, the
complete unconditional LOW-density set is

    { j/(3*2^k) : k>=0, 0<=j<=3*2^k } = (Z[1/2]/3) intersect [0,1].

For LOW/HIGH/SILENT readers, conditional on positive asymptotic acceptance,
the complete accepted LOW-ratio set is `Q intersect [0,1]`. Empty acceptance
has result UNDEFINED. Every admitted fixed reader has the stated ordinary
prefix limits. The claim is a union over all finite window lengths, not a
formula for every individual length. It applies to clock-only input.

For the upper bound, expand all four parent pairs by mu^j, with `B=2^j>=L`,
and take every offset 0<=r<B. Their frequencies are 1/6 for 00,11 and 1/3
for 01,10, giving all legal L-factor masses as integer multiples of 1/(6B).
For the constructive converse take M=2^k and L=3M. Phase plus the last three
parent bits defines exactly 6M recognizable classes of mass 1/(6M) each.
These classes may be unions of raw words, and no word is split.

C. For supplied integers 0<=a<=b, b>=1, choose a power M=2^k>=b. The fixed
causal reader using the reconstructed residue r returns

    LOW     if 0 <= r < a,
    HIGH    if a <= r < b,
    SILENT  if b <= r < M.

Its unconditional LOW mass is a/M, acceptance is b/M, and accepted LOW
ratio is exactly a/b. Complete M-tick cycles contain a LOW, b-a HIGH and
M-b SILENT outputs. The all-prefix limit follows with a bounded incomplete
cycle. A supplied (a,b) is part of the reader definition, not inferred from
a measured target or generated by U. The same clock-only law holds for every
native head. First construct the whole rational family independently of QDD;
only then compare its capacity to the 22 exposed supported QDD weights.

D. On synchronized native trajectories `z_n=4-3*theta_(n-1)` for n>=3.
A length-L history of these z values ending at n therefore gives a clock
window ending at n-1. Add one modulo M to its recovered residue to obtain
the phase at n. Every checkpoint in the window must be synchronized. This
is a causal checkpoint-history bridge, not a present-checkpoint-only reader.

E. For a fixed synchronized five-label native chart, the decorated sequence

Consequently any fixed finite decorated-checkpoint-window reader that is

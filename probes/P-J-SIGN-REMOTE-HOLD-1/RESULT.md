## Account, locality and limitations

H=12L+28-6phi, E=24L+50 and conserved sum S=2a. The two preparations have
identical complete H and E. Every stock and clock cell is counted before
execution. The receiver accesses only two declared co-located local arcs;
it reads neither the source nor a global clock counter. The actual graph
remoteness grows with d in both directions around the data ring.

This is occupied-region delay memory with a paid phase reference. A word
moves inside the receiver while its decoded value remains fixed. It is not
a stationary point latch, a literal-zero receiver, permanent archival
storage, independent renewal, a stream of separately supplied unknown
messages, native-U realization, or a physical detector/energy/time theorem.
Recurrence reuses the same original sign. Geometry, layer order, initial

## Matter and motion

The [primitive-support proof](anchor.md) uses \(\pi=1-\rho\), whose residue field is \(\mathbb F_5\). Remove the largest common power \(\pi^k\) from **matter alone** and mark every site where the remaining matter pair is nonzero modulo \(\pi\). C preserves both residues at each site, because its displacement contains a further factor \(\pi\). Whichever B the actual interaction selects acts invertibly on the local divided matter pair. It can never create or erase a mark. A nonempty finite set cannot equal itself translated by a nonzero vector on an unwrapped lattice. This argument covers arbitrary radiation even when its valuation differs from the material valuation, any number of contacts, and either matching color.

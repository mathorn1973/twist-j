# A loaded H3 detector with a delayed CLK record

This finite L1 protocol asks whether horizontal CORR produces native material work and a delayed change of a separate, funded vertical CLK coordinate. It tests an analytically tuned front first, then translates the same previously specified narrow-band packet to two distances. The receiver is present from the initial cut of every history.

## Fixed apparatus

Use the complete 384 by 6 torus with phase zero, and receiver at $(1,0)$. In $\mathbb Z[j]$ let $\rho=j^2$, $L=1-\rho$, $J=1+\rho$ and $u=L^3$. Prepare matter $(u,-u)$ at the receiver, CLK $b=u\rho+4$ at memory site $(1,5)$, and all other apparatus coordinates zero. The memory is a separate coordinate with counted initial stock $h(b)$ and linear word $b$. Its signed record is its native CLK word minus the same predeclared $b$.

The words $u$ and $b$ are nonzero modulo 2, because $\mathbb Z[j]/2$ is a field and $L,\rho$ are nonzero in it. Before the receiver fires, vertical C therefore keeps the two isolated matter words and the initially odd CLK fixed against their empty partners. Only the receiver can fire B. The code evaluates the complete field as a sparse map whose missing words are zero. It evaluates each edge with at least one nonzero input by the pinned native pair transport, and collides every site containing matter by the pinned native B. Vanishing pairs and zero-matter sites are exact identity cases. The first two tuned cuts at each distance are cross-checked against dense forward and inverse C and dense B. Preserve full ledgers before and after B at all ticks, check every step's inverse, and invert the terminal complete state back to its precise initial state, including the known alternating phase. Any disagreement, account drift or inverse failure is an integrity stop.

## Two analytically tuned fronts

Use distances $d=1,3$ horizontal bonds. For each distance and mode $V=J$ (tuned) or $V=1$ (detuned), prepare the pair of CORR words $\pm2^{d+1}L^{3-d}V$ on row zero. Keep all four pairs present in every front history. Index them by the order $(1,J),(1,1),(3,J),(3,1)$, using $i=0,1,2,3$. Their remote slots are $(120+2i,0)$ for the positive word and $(121+2i,0)$ for the negative word. For a local test, move only the chosen positive word to $(1-d,0)$. In the remote null, move none. Thus the complete initial linear and Hermitian ledger and the scalar stock are identical across all six arms, including the two distances. Remote words lie outside the ten-tick receiver cone. This matches aggregate funding; the incident waveform is intentionally amplitude-adjusted with distance and the tuned and detuned words have distinct local Hermitian norms.

The source begins on an even site. The leading path can advance one bond per native tick. Each admitted edge multiplies its leading word by $L/2$, reaching the receiver at post-C tick $d$ as $2uV$. The funding gives at least the required powers of two on that path. At that cut the receiver has $f=-uV$ and $d_\mathrm{mat}=u$. The tuned source predicts B1 and material Hermitian transfer $2h(u)(h(J)-1)$, with opposite radiation transfer. In the detuned arm the incident field half-gap and the matter gap have equal norms at this cut. It predicts B0 since $h(J)$ differs from one. The remote arm predicts no field, work or record through tick ten.

After tuned B1 the receiver's CLK is $u\rho$. Because $d$ is odd, next C pairs it with memory CLK $b=u\rho+4$. The memory receives the fixed nonzero change $-2L$, so the first write to this distinct coordinate is at $d+1$. At $d+2$ the new memory word is odd and the matching pairs it with an empty CLK site, so the same word stays through this declared one-tick hold. The predeclared criterion is exact contact at $d$, followed by exact memory delta $-2L$ at $d+1$ and $d+2$, for each tuned distance. Fixed cuts are $d,d+1,d+2,10$; report any later overwrites and any unexpected branch or null outcome. The one-tick retention criterion commits no prediction of permanent storage, a reusable reset operation or a detector linewidth. The same apparatus specification is reused across separate preparations.
